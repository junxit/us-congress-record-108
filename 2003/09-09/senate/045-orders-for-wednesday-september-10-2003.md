# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2003

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2003

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow. I ask further that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved, and the Senate then 
resume consideration of the current legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
