# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until 10:15 a.m. to give us an opportunity to try to 
ascertain the position of the majority leader and the Republican 
caucus.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 9:44 a.m., recessed until 
10:10 a.m. and reassembled when called to order by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Stevens).
```
