# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2622, FAIR AND ACCURATE CREDIT TRANSACTIONS ACT OF 2003

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2622, FAIR AND 
                ACCURATE CREDIT TRANSACTIONS ACT OF 2003

  Mr. Sessions, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 108-267) on the resolution (H. Res. 360) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2622) to amend the Fair Credit 
Reporting Act, to prevent identity theft, improve resolution of 
consumer disputes, improve the accuracy of consumer records, make 
improvements in the use of, and consumer access to, credit information, 
and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
