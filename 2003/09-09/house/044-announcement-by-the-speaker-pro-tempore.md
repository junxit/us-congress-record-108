# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. King of Iowa). As a general reminder, 
the Chair would like to reiterate that as stated in section 370 of the 
House Rules and Manual, suggesting mendacity on the part of the 
President is not in order, even by innuendo. As such, the Chair would 
reiterate that accusations of intentional deception are not in order.
  Furthermore, the Chair will remind Members that it is not in order to 
quote Senators' remarks spoken in the media.
```
