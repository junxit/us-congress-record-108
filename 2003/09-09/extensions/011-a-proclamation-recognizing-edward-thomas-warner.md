# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING EDWARD THOMAS WARNER

```
            A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING EDWARD THOMAS WARNER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 9, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas Edward Thomas Warner has devoted himself to serving others 
through his membership in the Boy Scouts of America; and
  Whereas, Edward Thomas Warner has shared his time and talent with the 
community in which he resides; and
  Whereas, Edward Thomas Warner has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and

[[Page 21587]]

  Whereas, Edward Thomas Warner must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication he put forth in earning the Eagle Scout Award;
  Therefore, I join with Troop 212, the residents of New Lexington, and 
the entire 18th Congressional District in congratulating Edward Thomas 
Warner as he receives the Eagle Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```
