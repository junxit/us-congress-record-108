# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING JASON MICHAEL BOETCHER

```
           A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING JASON MICHAEL BOETCHER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 9, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Jason Michael Boetcher has devoted himself to serving others 
through his membership in the Boy Scouts of America; and
  Whereas, Jason Michael Boetcher has shared his time and talent with 
the community in which he resides; and
  Whereas, Jason Michael Boetcher has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, Jason Michael Boetcher must be commended for the hard work 
and dedication he put forth in earning the Eagle Scout Award;
  Therefore, I join with the residents of Frazeysburg and the entire 
18th Congressional District in congratulating Jason Michael Boetcher as 
he receives the Eagle Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```
