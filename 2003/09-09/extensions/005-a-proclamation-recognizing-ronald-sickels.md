# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING RONALD SICKELS

```
               A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING RONALD SICKELS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 9, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Ronald Sickels has demonstrated professionalism and a 
dedication to safety; and
  Whereas, Ronald Sickels has logged 1 million miles, the equivalent of 
circling the earth's equator 40 times, without a single preventable 
accident; and
  Whereas, Ronald Sickels must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication he put forth over his years at Yellow Transportation;
  Therefore, I join with the Motor Freight Carriers Association and the 
residents of Ohio 18th Congressional District in congratulating Ronald 
Sickels for his outstanding achievement.

                          ____________________
```
