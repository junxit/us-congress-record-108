# REPORT ON H.R. 2765, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS, 2004

```
                              {time}  1430
     REPORT ON H.R. 2765, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS, 2004

  Mr. FRELINGHUYSEN, from the Committee on Appropriations, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 108-214) on the bill (H.R. 2765) making
```
