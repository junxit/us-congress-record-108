vote 359, on Motion to Instruct Conferees, I would have voted no. July 
15, 2003, Rollcall vote 360, on agreeing to H. Res. 316, I would have 
voted yea. Rollcall vote 361, on Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass, 
as amended, H.R. 2330, I would have voted yea.
