# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business until the hour of 5 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for not to exceed 1 minute each to 
comment on the historic speech we have just heard.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I yield to the Senator from Idaho since 
his State was mentioned.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Idaho.
```
