# GRANTING AN EASEMENT TO FACILITATE ACCESS TO THE LEWIS AND CLARK INTERPRETIVE CENTER IN NEBRASKA CITY, NEBRASKA

**Measures:** H.R. 255

```
   GRANTING AN EASEMENT TO FACILITATE ACCESS TO THE LEWIS AND CLARK 
             INTERPRETIVE CENTER IN NEBRASKA CITY, NEBRASKA

  The bill (H.R. 255) to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
grant an easement to facilitate access to the Lewis and Clark 
Interpretive Center in Nebraska City, Nebraska, was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
