# DIRECTING THE SECRETARY OF AGRICULTURE TO CONVEY CERTAIN LAND IN THE LAKE TAHOE BASIN MANAGEMENT UNIT, NEVADA

```
 DIRECTING THE SECRETARY OF AGRICULTURE TO CONVEY CERTAIN LAND IN THE 
                LAKE TAHOE BASIN MANAGEMENT UNIT, NEVADA

  The bill (H.R. 74) to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to convey 
certain land in the Lake Tahoe Basin Management Unit, Nevada, to the 
Secretary of the Interior, in trust for the Washoe Indian Tribe of 
Nevada and California, was considered, ordered to a third reading, read 
the third time, and passed.
```
