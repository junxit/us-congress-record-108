# ACTIVITIES OF THE SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

```
           ACTIVITIES OF THE SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, as chairman and vice chairman of the 
Select Committee on Intelligence, Senator Rockefeller and I have 
submitted to the Senate the Report of the Senate Select Committee on 
Intelligence of its activities during the 107th Congress from January 
3, 2001 to November 22, 2002. The Committee is charged by the Senate 
with the responsibility of carrying out oversight of the intelligence 
activities of the United States. Much of the work of the Committee is 
of necessity conducted in secrecy, yet the Committee believes that the 
Intelligence Community and this Committee should be as accountable as 
possible to the public. The public report to the Senate is intended to 
achieve that goal.
```
