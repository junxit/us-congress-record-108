# RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MINORITY LEADER

```
               RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MINORITY LEADER

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Nevada.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we also hope to finish at least the 
amendments we know of that deal with things nuclear on this bill. 
Senator Dorgan is standing by, ready to offer the next amendment. He 
has indicated he would agree to a time limit. I believe the amendment 
has been shown to the other side to see if they would be willing to 
enter into a reasonable time limit. Last night, he suggested an hour 
and a half equally divided. We will submit that to staff and see if we 
can get something worked out and agree to that in a short time, 
hopefully before the vote takes place.
```
