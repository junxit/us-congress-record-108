# VACATING ADOPTION OF SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 46, AMENDING SAID CONCURRENT RESOLUTION, AND ADOPTING CONCURRENT RESOLUTION AS SO AMENDED

```
                              {time}  1915
  VACATING ADOPTION OF SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 46, AMENDING SAID 
CONCURRENT RESOLUTION, AND ADOPTING CONCURRENT RESOLUTION AS SO AMENDED

  Mr. HUNTER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the action of 
the House adopting Senate Concurrent Resolution 46 be vacated to the 
end that the House hereby amend the concurrent resolution by striking 
``Secretary of the Senate'' and inserting in lieu thereof ``Clerk of 
the House'' and adopt the concurrent resolution, as so amended.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Ose). Is there objection to the request 
of the gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
