# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, tomorrow morning the Senate will resume 
consideration of this appropriations bill. At 9:30, there will be two 
consecutive votes on the pending amendments. The first is in relation 
to the Harkin amendment on Byrne grants, and the second is in relation 
to the Schumer amendment regarding port security.
  Senators should be advised that additional amendments and, therefore, 
additional votes are expected during tomorrow's session. The Senate 
will remain in session and a late night is contemplated in order to 
make substantial progress. If the Senate is unable to finish the bill 
during tomorrow's session, the Members may expect to return on Tuesday 
and remain in session until the bill is completed.
```
