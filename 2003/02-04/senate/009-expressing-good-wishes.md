# EXPRESSING GOOD WISHES

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
                         EXPRESSING GOOD WISHES

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I join with my good friend from Nevada in 
expressing our good wishes to the distinguished Senator from Kentucky, 
as well as our good friend, the distinguished Senator from Florida, who 
also has undergone a very serious operation. We are a family and our 
thoughts and concerns of those in this body who have had illnesses are 
with them. We wish them a very speedy recovery. The Senate will be a 
bit duller and quieter until they return, but I am confident they will 
do so soon.

                          ____________________
```
