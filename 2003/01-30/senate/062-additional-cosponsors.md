# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 101, S. 138, S. 145, S. 151, S. 185, S. 215, S. 229, S. 238

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 101

  At the request of Mr. Hatch, the name of the Senator from Alabama 
(Mr. Sessions) was added as a cosponsor of S. 101, a bill to authorize 
salary adjustments for Justices and judges of the United States for 
fiscal year 2003.


                                 S. 138

  At the request of Mr. Rockefeller, the names of the Senator from 
South Dakota (Mr. Johnson), the Senator from Maryland (Ms. Mikulski) 
and the Senator from Louisiana (Ms. Landrieu) were added as cosponsors 
of S. 138, a bill to temporarily increase the Federal medical 
assistance percentage for the medicaid program.


                                 S. 145

  At the request of Mr. Kyl, the name of the Senator from Colorado (Mr. 
Allard) was added as a cosponsor of S. 145, a bill to prohibit 
assistance to North Korea or the Korean Peninsula Development 
Organization, and for other purposes.


                                 S. 151

  At the request of Mr. Hatch, the name of the Senator from New York 
(Mr. Schumer) was added as a cosponsor of S. 151, a bill to amend title 
18, United States Code, with respect to the sexual exploitation of 
children.


                                 S. 185

  At the request of Mr. Leahy, the name of the Senator from Maryland 
(Mr. Sarbanes) was added as a cosponsor of S. 185, a bill to authorize 
emergency supplemental assistance to combat the growing humanitarian 
crisis in sub-Saharan Africa.


                                 S. 215

  At the request of Mrs. Feinstein, the names of the Senator from New 
Mexico (Mr. Bingaman), the Senator from Nebraska (Mr. Nelson) and the 
Senator from Washington (Ms. Cantwell) were added as cosponsors of S. 
215, a bill to authorize funding assistance for the States for the 
discharge of homeland security activities by the National Guard.


                                 S. 229

  At the request of Mr. Johnson, the names of the Senator from Nebraska 
(Mr. Nelson), the Senator from Louisiana (Mr. Breaux) and the Senator 
from Louisiana (Ms. Landrieu) were added as cosponsors of S. 229, a 
bill to provide for the merger of the bank and savings association 
deposit insurance funds, to modernize and improve the safety and 
fairness of the Federal deposit insurance system, and for other 
purposes.


                                 S. 238

  At the request of Mr. Reed, the names of the Senator from New Jersey 
(Mr. Lautenberg), the Senator from Rhode Island (Mr. Chafee) and the 
Senator from South Dakota (Mr. Johnson) were added as cosponsors of S. 
238,

[[Page S1814]]

a bill to reauthorize the Museum and Library Services Act, and for 
other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
