# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that morning 
business be extended until the hour of 2 p.m., with the time equally 
divided between the majority and minority leaders or their designees, 
and that Members be permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
