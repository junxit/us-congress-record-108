# Daily Digest/Highlights + Senate

**Measures:** S.Res. 41

```
                                           Monday, February 3, 2003

[[Page D84]]

                              Daily Digest


HIGHLIGHTS

      Senate agreed to S. Res. 41, Honoring the mission of the Space 
      Shuttle Columbia.


                                 Senate


Chamber Action
Routine Proceedings, pages S1845-46
Measures Introduced: One resolution was submitted, as follows: S. Res. 
41.
Measures Passed:
  Honoring the Space Shuttle Columbia: Senate agreed to S. Res. 41, 
honoring the mission of the Space Shuttle Columbia.
  Page S1845
Nomination--Agreement: A unanimous-consent agreement was reached 
providing that with respect to consideration of the nomination of 
Miguel A. Estrada, of Virginia, to be United States Circuit Judge for 
the District of Columbia Circuit, be changed so that the Senate will 
begin consideration of the nomination at 2:15 p.m., on Wednesday, 
February 5, 2003.
Appointment:
  National Commission for the Review of the Research and Development 
Programs of the United States Intelligence Community: The Chair, on 
behalf of the Minority Leader, pursuant to 50 U.S.C. 401, section 
1002(b) of the Intelligence Authorization Act, appointed the following 
to the National Commission for the Review of the Research and 
Development Programs of the United States Intelligence Community: Ms. 
Lofgren of California, and Mr. Maurice Sonnenberg of New York.
Adjournment: Senate met at 10:02 a.m, and pursuant to the provisions of 
S. Res. 41, adjourned at 10:11 a.m., until 4 p.m., on Tuesday, February 
4, 2003. (For Senate's program, see the remarks of the Majority Leader 
in today's Record on page S1846.)
```
