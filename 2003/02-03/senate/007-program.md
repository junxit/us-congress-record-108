# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. For the information of Senators, the Senate will reconvene 
tomorrow to allow Members to make statements regarding the Columbia 
tragedy. When the Senate concludes its business on Tuesday, it will 
reconvene on Wednesday morning.

with respect to the Estrada nomination be changed so that the Senate 
begins its consideration at 2:15 p.m. on Wednesday.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
