# THE CALENDAR

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed en bloc to the immediate consideration of the following Energy 
bills: Calendar No. 124, S. 246; Calendar No. 125, S. 500; Calendar No. 
127, S. 625; Calendar No. 128, S. 635; Calendar No. 129, H.R. 519; 
Calendar No. 130, H.R. 733; and Calendar No. 131, H.R. 788.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bills 
en bloc.
  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, I further ask unanimous consent that, 
where applicable, the committee amendments be agreed to, the bills, as 
amended, if amended, be read a third time and passed, the motions to 
reconsider be laid upon the table, and that any statements relating to 
the bills be printed in the Record, with the above occurring en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
