# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** S. 342

```
Committee Meetings
CONFERENCE REPORT--KEEPING CHILDREN AND FAMILIES SAFE ACT
Committee on Rules: Granted, by voice vote, a rule waiving all points 
of order against the conference report to accompany S. 342, Keeping 
Children and Families Safe Act of 2003, and against its consideration. 
Testimony was heard from Representative Hoekstra.
BALANCING SECURITY AND COMMERCE
Select Committee on Homeland Security: Subcommittee on Infrastructure 
and Border Security held a hearing on Balancing Security and Commerce, 
Testimony was heard from Robert Bonner, Commissioner, Bureau of Customs 
and Border Protection, Department of Homeland Security; and Richard M. 
Stana, Director, Homeland Security and Justice, GAO.
```
