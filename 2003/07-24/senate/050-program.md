# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow the Senate will resume consideration of S. 14, the Energy 
bill. Three fuel standards amendments were offered tonight. Senators 
are encouraged to come to the floor during tomorrow's session to debate 
the merits of these important amendments.
  There will be no rollcall votes tomorrow. The next rollcall vote will 
occur on Monday at 5 p.m. That vote will be on the nomination of Earl 
Yeakel to be a U.S. District Judge for the Western District of Texas. 
Following the 5 p.m. vote on Monday, the Senate may also vote in 
relation to any available amendments to the Energy bill.

                          ____________________
```
