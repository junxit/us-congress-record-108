# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Allen, Thomas H. (A000357) [D-ME]

**Measures:** H.R. 2861

```
H.R. 2861

                         Offered by: Mr. Allen

        Amendment No. 14: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. 421. When an numerical estimate of the costs and 
     benefits of an agency action is prepared in accordance with 
     Executive Order 12866 or section 812 of the Clean Air Act, 
     none of the funds provided in this Act may be expended to 
     apply monetary values for adult premature mortality that 
     differ based on the age of the adult.
```
