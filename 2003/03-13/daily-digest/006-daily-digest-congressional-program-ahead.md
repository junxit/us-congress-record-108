                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                Week of March 17 through March 22, 2003

                             Senate Chamber

  On Monday, at 2 p.m., Senate expects to begin consideration of the

consideration of the First Concurrent Budget Resolution, and may
