# SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 20--PERMITTING THE CHAIRMAN OF THE COMMITTEE ON RULES AND ADMINISTRATION OF THE SENATE TO DESIGNATE ANOTHER MEMBER OF THE COMMITTEE TO SERVE ON THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING IN PLACE OF THE CHAIRMAN

```
    SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 20--PERMITTING THE CHAIRMAN OF THE 
   COMMITTEE ON RULES AND ADMINISTRATION OF THE SENATE TO DESIGNATE 
  ANOTHER MEMBER OF THE COMMITTEE TO SERVE ON THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON 
                   PRINTING IN PLACE OF THE CHAIRMAN

  Mr. LOTT (for himself and Mr. Dodd) submitted the following 
concurrent resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                            S. Con. Res. 20

       Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives 
     concurring), That effective for the One Hundred Eighth 
     Congress, the Chairman of the Committee on Rules and 
     Administration of the Senate may designate another member of 
     the Committee to serve on the Joint Committee on Printing in 
     place of the Chairman.

                          ____________________
```
