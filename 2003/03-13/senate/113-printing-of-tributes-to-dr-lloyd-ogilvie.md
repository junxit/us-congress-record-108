# PRINTING OF TRIBUTES TO DR. LLOYD OGILVIE

**Speaking:** Bennett, Robert F. (B000382) [R-UT]

```
               PRINTING OF TRIBUTES TO DR. LLOYD OGILVIE

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the tributes 
to Dr. Lloyd Ogilvie, the retiring Senate Chaplain, be printed as a 
Senate document, with the understanding that Members have until 12 
noon, Friday, March 21, to submit these tributes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
