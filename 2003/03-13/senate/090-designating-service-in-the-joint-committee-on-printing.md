# DESIGNATING SERVICE IN THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING

```
         DESIGNATING SERVICE IN THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of S. Con. Res. 20, which was 
submitted earlier today by Senators Lott and Dodd.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 20) permitting the 
     Chairman of the Committee on Rules and Administration of the 
     Senate to designate another member of the Committee to serve 
     on the Joint Committee on Printing in place of the Chairman.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
concurrent resolution be agreed to, and the motion to reconsider be 
laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 20) was agreed to, as 
follows:

                            S. Con. Res. 20

       Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives 
     concurring), That effective for the One Hundred Eighth 
     Congress, the Chairman of the Committee on Rules and 
     Administration of the Senate may designate another member of 
     the Committee to serve on the Joint Committee on Printing in 
     place of the Chairman.

                          ____________________
```
