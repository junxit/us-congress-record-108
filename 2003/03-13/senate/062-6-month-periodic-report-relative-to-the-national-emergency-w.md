# 6-MONTH PERIODIC REPORT RELATIVE TO THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY WITH RESPECT TO IRAN--PM 23

```
6-MONTH PERIODIC REPORT RELATIVE TO THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY WITH RESPECT 
                             TO IRAN--PM 23

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Banking, Housing, and 
Urban Affairs:


[[Page S3727]]


To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 401(c) of the National Emergencies Act, 50 
U.S.C. 1641(c), section 204(c) of the International Emergency Economic 
Powers Act, 50 U.S.C. 1703(c), and section 505(c) of the International 
Security and Development Cooperation Act of 1985, 22 U.S.C. 2349aa-
9(c), I am transmitting a 6-month periodic report prepared by my 
Administration on the national emergency with respect to Iran that was 
declared in Executive Order 12957 of March 15, 1995.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, March 12, 2003.

                          ____________________
```
