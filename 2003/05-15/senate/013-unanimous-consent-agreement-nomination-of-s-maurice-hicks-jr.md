# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--NOMINATION OF S. MAURICE HICKS, JR., TO BE UNITED STATES DISTRICT JUDGE

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--NOMINATION OF S. MAURICE HICKS, JR., TO BE 
                      UNITED STATES DISTRICT JUDGE

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that at 5:30 p.m. on Monday, May 19, the Senate proceed to 
executive session and a vote on the confirmation of Executive Calendar 
No. 172, S. Maurice Hicks, Jr., to be a United States District Judge 
for the Western District of Louisiana. I further ask unanimous consent 
that following that vote, the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action and the Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
