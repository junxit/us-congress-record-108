# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO THE UNITED STATES DELEGATION OF THE CANADA- UNITED STATES INTERPARLIAMENTARY GROUP

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO THE UNITED STATES DELEGATION OF THE CANADA-
                 UNITED STATES INTERPARLIAMENTARY GROUP

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Cole). Pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 276d, 
clause 10 of rule 1, and the order of the House of January 8, 2003, the 
Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the following Members of 
the House to the United States Delegation of the Canada-United States 
Interparliamentary Group, in addition to Mr. Houghton of New York, 
chairman, appointed on March 13, 2003:
  Mr. Oberstar of Minnesota,
  Mr. Dreier of California,
  Mr. Shaw of Florida,
  Ms. Slaughter of New York,
  Mr. Stearns of Florida,
  Mr. Peterson of Minnesota,
  Mr. Manzullo of Illinois,
  Mr. Smith of Michigan,
  Mr. English of Pennsylvania,
  and Mr. Souder of Indiana.

                          ____________________
```
