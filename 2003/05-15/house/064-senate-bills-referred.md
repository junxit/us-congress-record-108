# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 195, S. 709

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 195. An act to amend the Solid Waste Disposal Act to 
     bring underground storage tanks into compliance with subtitle 
     I of that Act, to promote cleanup of leaking underground 
     storage tanks, to provide sufficient resources for such 
     compliance and cleanup, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       S. 709. An act to award a congressional gold medal to Prime 
     Minister Tony Blair; to the Committee on Financial Services.
```
