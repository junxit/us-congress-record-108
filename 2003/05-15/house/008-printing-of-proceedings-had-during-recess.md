# PRINTING OF PROCEEDINGS HAD DURING RECESS

**Speaking:** Diaz-Balart, Lincoln (D000299) [R-FL]

```
               PRINTING OF PROCEEDINGS HAD DURING RECESS

  Mr. LINCOLN DIAZ-BALART of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous 
consent that the proceedings had during the recess be printed in the 
Congressional Record and that all Members and former Members who spoke 
during the recess have the privilege of revising and extending their 
remarks.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
