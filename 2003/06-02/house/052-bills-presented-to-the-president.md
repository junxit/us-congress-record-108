# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on May 23, 2003 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.J. Res. 51. Increasing the statutory limit on the public 
     debt.
       H.R. 2. To provide the reconciliation pursuant to section 
     201 of the concurrent resolution on the budget for fiscal 
     year 2004.
       H.R. 1298. To provide assistance to foreign countries to 
     combat HIV/AIDS, tuberculosis, and malaria, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 2185. To extend the Temporary Extended Unemployment 
     Compensation Act of 2002.
```
