# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Measures:** H.Res. 159, H.Res. 195, H.R. 1465

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. LARSON of California. Mr. Speaker, had I been present, I would 
have voted: ``yea'' on H. Res. 159: expressing profound sorrow on the 
occasion of the death of Irma Rangel, rollcall No. 227; ``yea'' on H. 
Res. 195: congratulating Sammy Sosa of the Chicago Cubs for hitting 500 
major league home runs, rollcall No. 228; and ``yea'' on H.R. 1465; to 
designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 
4832 East Highway 27 in Iron Station, North Carolina, as the ``General 
Charles Gabriel Post Office'', rollcall No. 229.
```
