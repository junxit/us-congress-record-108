# FEDERAL OCEAN AND COASTAL ACTIVITIES REPORT--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
FEDERAL OCEAN AND COASTAL ACTIVITIES REPORT--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT 
                          OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Resources, the Committee on Science, and the Committee on 
Transportation and Infrastructure:

To the Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with section 5 of the Oceans Act of 2000 (33 U.S.C. 
857-19), I transmit herewith the first biennial Federal Ocean and 
Coastal Activities Report as prepared by my Administration.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, March 19, 2003.

                          ____________________
```
