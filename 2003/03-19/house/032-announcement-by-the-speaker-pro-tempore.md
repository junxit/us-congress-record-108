# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 314, H.R. 417, H.R. 699

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Pursuant to clause 8, rule XX, 
proceedings will resume on three of the motions to suspend the rules 
previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 314, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 417, by the yeas and nays; and
  H.R. 699, by the yeas and nays.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. The 
remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.
```
