# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 95, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2004

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 95, 
        CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2004

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 108-44) on the resolution (H. Res. 151) 
providing for consideration of the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 
95) establishing the congressional budget for the United States 
Government for fiscal year 2004 and setting forth appropriate budgetary 
levels for fiscal years 2003 and 2005 through 2013, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
