# FIRST BIENNIAL FEDERAL OCEAN AND COASTAL ACTIVITIES REPORT--PM 26

```
   FIRST BIENNIAL FEDERAL OCEAN AND COASTAL ACTIVITIES REPORT--PM 26

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation:

To The Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with section 5 of the Oceans Act of 2000 (33 U.S.C. 
857-19), I transmit herewith the first biennial Federal Ocean and 
Coastal Activities Report as prepared by my Administration.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, March 19, 2003.

                          ____________________
```
