**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

proceed immediately to executive session to consider the following 
nominations on today's executive calendar: Calendar Nos. 62, 63, 64, 
65, 66, 67, 68, 69, and all nominations on the Secretary's desk. I 
further ask unanimous consent that the nominations be confirmed en 
bloc, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the Senate then return 
to legislative session.
