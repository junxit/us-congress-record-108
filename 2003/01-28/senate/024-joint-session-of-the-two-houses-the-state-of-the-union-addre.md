# JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--THE STATE OF THE UNION ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--THE STATE OF THE UNION ADDRESS BY THE 
                     PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Presiding Officer of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on 
the part of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the 
House of Representatives to escort the President of the United States 
into the House Chamber for the joint session to be held tonight, 
Tuesday, January 28, 2003, at 9 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from New York.

                          ____________________
```
