# ELECTION OF MAJORITY MEMBERSHIP TO COMMITTEE ON HOUSE ADMINISTRATION

**Speaking:** Calvert, Ken (C000059) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 34

```
  ELECTION OF MAJORITY MEMBERSHIP TO COMMITTEE ON HOUSE ADMINISTRATION

  Mr. CALVERT. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican Conference, 
I call up a privileged resolution (H. Res. 34) election of majority 
membership on the Committee on House Administration, and ask for its 
immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 34

       Resolved, That the following named Members be, and are 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committee of the 
     House of Representatives: Committee on House Administration: 
     Mr. Ehlers; Mr. Mica; Mr. Linder; Mr. Doolittle and Mr. 
     Reynolds.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
