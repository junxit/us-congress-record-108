                   COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR WEDNESDAY,

  Committee on Veterans' Affairs, to hold an organizational meeting,

  Committee on Ways and Means, to hold an organizational meeting, 11

  Subcommittee on Health, to hold an organizational meeting, 4 p.m.,

  Subcommittee on Oversight, to hold on organizational meeting, 2

  Subcommittee on Trade, to hold an organizational meeting, 2:30
