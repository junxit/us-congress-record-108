# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** Bayh, Evan (B001233) [D-IN]

```
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  Mr. BAYH. Mr. President, on rollcall vote No. 221 I voted nay. It was 
my intention to vote yea. Therefore, I ask unanimous consent that I be 
permitted to change my vote. This will in no way change the outcome of 
the vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
