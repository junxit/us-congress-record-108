# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Lipinski, William O. (L000342) [D-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. WILLIAM O. LIPINSKI

                              of illinios

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 17, 2003

  Mr. LIPINSKI. Mr. Speaker, on July 14, 2003, I was unavoidably 
detained and missed rollcall vote No. 357, the Ackerman-LaTourette 
amendment to the Agriculture Appropriations bill which would require 
that the USDA expend no funds to approve meat from downed animals--
animals that are too sick to walk or stand--for food. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``yea''.
```
