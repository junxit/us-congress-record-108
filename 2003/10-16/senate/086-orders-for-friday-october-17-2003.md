# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 17, 2003

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S. 1689

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 17, 2003

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask unanimous 
consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn 
until 9 a.m. October 17. I further ask that following the prayer and 
the pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day and the Senate then resume 
consideration of S. 1689, the Iraq-Afghanistan supplemental 
appropriations bill as provided under the original order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
