# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow the Senate will be in a period of morning business until 
approximately 10 a.m.
  Following morning business, the Senate will resume consideration of 
H.R. 2555, the Department of Homeland Security appropriations bill. 
Again, it is the majority leader's intention to complete action on this 
bill during tomorrow's session.
  There are a number of Senators who have expressed an interest in 
offering amendments. The majority leader would encourage those Members 
to contact the bill managers as soon as possible.
  Rollcall votes are expected throughout the day tomorrow as the Senate 
will continue work through the remaining amendments to the bill. 
Senators will be notified when the first vote is scheduled.
```
