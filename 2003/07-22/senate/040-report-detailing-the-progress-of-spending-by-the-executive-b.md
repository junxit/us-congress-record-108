# REPORT DETAILING THE PROGRESS OF SPENDING BY THE EXECUTIVE BRANCH DURING THE FIRST TWO QUARTERS OF FISCAL YEAR 2003 IN SUPPORT OF PLAN COLOMBIA--PM 46

```
   REPORT DETAILING THE PROGRESS OF SPENDING BY THE EXECUTIVE BRANCH 
 DURING THE FIRST TWO QUARTERS OF FISCAL YEAR 2003 IN SUPPORT OF PLAN 
                            COLOMBIA--PM 46

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Appropriations:
To The Congress of The United States:
  Consistent with section 3204(e), Public Law 106-246, I am providing a 
report prepared by my Administration detailing the progress of spending 
by the executive branch during the first two quarters of Fiscal Year 
2003 in support of Plan Colombia.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, July 22, 2003.

                          ____________________
```
