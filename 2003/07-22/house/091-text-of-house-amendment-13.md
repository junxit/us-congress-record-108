# Text of House Amendment 13

**Speaking:** McCollum, Betty (M001143) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2800

```
H.R. 2800

                        Offered By: Ms. McCollum

       Amendment No. 13: In the item relating to ``child survival 
     and health programs fund'', add at the end before the period 
     the following: ``: Provided further, That of the funds 
     appropriated under this heading for HIV/AIDS assistance, not 
     less than $200,000,000 shall be available for assistance for 
     orphans and vulnerable children affected by HIV/AIDS''.
```
