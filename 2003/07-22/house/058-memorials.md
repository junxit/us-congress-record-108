# MEMORIALS

                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       149. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Michigan, relative to House 
     Concurrent Resolution No. 11 memorializing the United States 
     Congress to enact legislation that will address the issue of 
     the improper labeling and classification of dairy products; 
     to the Committee on Agriculture.
       150. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to House Resolution No. 09 
     memorializing the United States Congress to enact legislation 
     to provide for tariff rate qoutas for dry milk protein 
     concentrates that are equivalent to the import quotas 
     currently in place on other dairy products; to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means.
