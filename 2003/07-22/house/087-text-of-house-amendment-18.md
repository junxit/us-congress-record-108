# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Rush, Bobby L. (R000515) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2799

```
H.R. 2799

                          Offered By: Mr. Rush

       Amendment No. 18: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for the sentencing phase of any Federal prosecution 
     in which the penalty of death is sought by the United States.
```
