**Measures:** H.Res. 329, H.R. 2738, H.R. 2739

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2738, UNITED 
 STATES-CHILE FREE TRADE AGREEMENT IMPLEMENTATION ACT, AND H.R. 2739, 
    UNITED STATES-SINGAPORE FREE TRADE AGREEMENT IMPLEMENTATION ACT

for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2738) to implement the United 
States-Chile Free Trade Agreement, and for consideration of the bill 
(H.R. 2739) to implement the United States-Singapore Free Trade 
Agreement, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
