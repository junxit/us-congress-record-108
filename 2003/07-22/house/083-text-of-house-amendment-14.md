# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Tancredo, Thomas G. (T000458) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2799

```
H.R. 2799

                        Offered By: Mr. Tancredo

       Amendment No. 14: At the end of the bill after the last 
     section (preceding the short title) insert the following new 
     title:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Department of State to assist any foreign 
     government in the development of consular identification 
     cards.
```
