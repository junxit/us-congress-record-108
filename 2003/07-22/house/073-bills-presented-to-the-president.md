# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 733, H.R. 2330

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on July 22, 2003, he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.R. 733. A bill to authorize the Secretary of the Interior 
     to acquire the McLoughlin House in Oregon City, Oregon, for 
     inclusion in Fort Vancouver Historic Site, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 2330. To sanction the ruling Burmese military junta, 
     to strengthen Burma's democratic forces and support and 
     recognize the National League of Democracy as the legitimate 
     representative of the Burmese people, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
