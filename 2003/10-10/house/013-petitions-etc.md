# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       41. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Wayne County 
     Commission, Michigan, relative to Resolution No. 2003-283 
     petitioning the United States Congress to enact legislation 
     to provide for tariff rate quotas for dry milk protein 
     concentrates that are equivalent to the import quotas 
     currently in place on other dairy products; which was 
     referred to the Committee on Ways and Means.
```
