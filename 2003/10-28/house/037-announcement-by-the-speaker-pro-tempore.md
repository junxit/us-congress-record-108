# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 1, H.R. 6, H.Con.Res. 291, H.Res. 409, H.R. 1308, H.R. 2359

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on one motion to suspend the rules and on three motions to 
instruct conferees previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 2359, a suspension;
  H.R. 6, a motion to instruct;
  H.R. 1, a motion to instruct;
  HR. 1308, a motion to instruct, all by the yeas and nays.
  Votes on suspending the rules with respect to H. Con. Res. 291 and H. 
Res. 409 will be taken tomorrow. The next three votes will be conducted 
as 5-minute votes. The fifth and final vote in this series will be a 
15-minute vote.




                          ____________________
```
