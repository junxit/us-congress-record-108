                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 377

  At the request of Ms. Landrieu, the name of the Senator from Indiana 
(Mr. Bayh) was added as a cosponsor of S. 377, a bill to require the 
Secretary of the Treasury to mint coins in commemoration of the 
contributions of Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., to the United States.
