# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 438, H.Res. 439

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions were 
introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. KENNEDY of Minnesota (for himself, Mr. Kline, 
             Mr. Sabo, Mr. Gutknecht, and Mr. Oberstar):
       H. Res. 438. A resolution congratulating John Gagliardi, 
     football coach of St. John's University, on the occasion of 
     his becoming the all-time winningest coach in collegiate 
     football history; to the Committee on Education and the 
     Workforce.
           By Mr. REYES (for himself and Mr. Dreier):
       H. Res. 439. A resolution honoring the life and career of 
     Willie Shoemaker and expressing the condolences of the House 
     of Representatives to his family and friends on his death; to 
     the Committee on Government Reform.
```
