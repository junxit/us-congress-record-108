                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ELTON GALLEGLY

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

  Mr. GALLEGLY. Mr. Speaker, on Wednesday, September 10, I was unable

500). Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea.'' Additionally, I

Tax Relief, Simplification and Equity Act (rollcall vote 501) and on 
H.R. 1, the Medicare Prescription Drug and Modernization Act (rollcall 
vote 502). Had I been present, I would have voted ``nay'' on each 
motion.
