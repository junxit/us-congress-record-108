# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will resume 
consideration of the House message accompanying S. 3, the partial-birth 
abortion ban bill. The Senate will continue that debate until 10:30 
this morning. At 10:30, the Senate will begin consideration of the 
Interior appropriations bill. Amendments are expected on that 
legislation. Therefore, rollcall votes will occur throughout the day.
  In addition, the Senate may consider judicial nominations that are on 
the Executive Calendar cleared for action. Therefore, if necessary, 
rollcall votes will be scheduled on those nominations throughout the 
day as well.
  I thank all Members for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
