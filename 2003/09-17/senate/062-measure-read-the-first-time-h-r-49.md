# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 49

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 49

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 49 which was 
just received from the House is at the desk, and I ask for its first 
reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the title of the bill 
for the first time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 49) to permanently extend the moratorium 
     enacted by the Internet Tax Freedom Act, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I now ask for its second reading and object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
  The bill will be read the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
