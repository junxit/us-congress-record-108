# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent we now go into a 
period of morning business with Senators being allowed to speak for 5 
minutes therein until the hour of 2 p.m. this afternoon, at which we 
will return to the business of Interior appropriations.
  Mr. DODD. Reserving the right to object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The unanimous consent request is for a period 
of morning business so Members can speak for up to 5 minutes on a topic 
of their choosing.
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Connecticut.

                          ____________________
```
