# Text of Senate Amendment 1731

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2691

```
  SA 1731. Mr. REID (for himself, Mr. Lieberman, Ms. Landrieu, Mr. 
Kennedy, Mrs. Murray, and Ms. Cantwell) proposed an amendment to the

[[Page S11705]]

bill H.R. 2691, making appropriations for the Department of the 
Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2004, and for other purposes; as follows:

       On page 137, between lines 23 and 24, insert the following:

     SEC. 3__. COMPETITIVE SOURCING STUDIES.

       None of the funds made available by this Act shall be used 
     to initiate any competitive sourcing studies after the date 
     of enactment of this Act.
                                 ______
```
