# Text of Senate Amendment 1730

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

```
  SA 1730. Ms. COLLINS (for herself and Ms. Snowe) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by her to the bill H.R. 2691, making 
appropriations for the Department of the Interior and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2004, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 64, line 21, after ``6a(i))'' insert the following: 
     ``, of which $1,000,000 shall be available to the National 
     Forest Foundation for the Downeast Lakes Forestry 
     Partnership, Maine''.
```
