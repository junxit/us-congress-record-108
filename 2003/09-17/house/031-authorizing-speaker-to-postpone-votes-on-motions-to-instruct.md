# AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO POSTPONE VOTES ON MOTIONS TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES CONSIDERED TODAY UNTIL TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2003

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

```
AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO POSTPONE VOTES ON MOTIONS TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES 
           CONSIDERED TODAY UNTIL TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2003

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Speaker be 
authorized to postpone further proceedings on any record vote ordered 
on the question of agreeing to a motion to instruct conferees 
considered today until Tuesday, September 23, 2003.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
