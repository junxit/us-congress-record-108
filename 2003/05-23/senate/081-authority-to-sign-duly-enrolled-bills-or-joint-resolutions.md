# AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
       AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader or the assistant 
majority leader be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint 
resolutions.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
