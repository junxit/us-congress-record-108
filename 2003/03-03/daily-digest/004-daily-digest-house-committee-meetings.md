# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
COMBATING TERRORISM: A PROLIFERATION OF STRATEGIES
Committee on Government Reform: Subcommittee on National Security, 
Emerging Threats and International Relations held a hearing on 
``Combating Terrorism: A Proliferation of Strategies.'' Testimony was 
heard from Raymond Decker, Director, Defense Capabilities and 
Management Team, GAO; James Gilmore, III Chairman, Advisory Panel to 
Assess the Domestic Response Capabilities for Terrorism Involving 
Weapons of Mass Destruction; and public witnesses.
```
