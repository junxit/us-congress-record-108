# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, there has been an objection made to the 
Judiciary Committee meeting today. Thus, as the only way to allow that 
committee to continue its very important work for the people of the 
United States of America, I am forced to ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate stand in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:06 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 3:05 p.m. when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Crapo).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Utah.

                          ____________________
```
