# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 11 a.m., with the first 30 minutes to be 
equally divided between the Senator from Texas and the Democratic 
leader or their designees, and the second 30 minutes to be equally 
divided between the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from Texas.

                          ____________________
```
