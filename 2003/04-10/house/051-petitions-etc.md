# PETITIONS, ETC.

                            PETITIONS, ETC.

       9. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislature of 
     Rockland County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 67 of 
     2003 petitioning the United States Congress to enact the

       10. Also, a petition of the Legislature of Rockland County, 
     New York, relative to Resolution No. 64 of 2003 petitioning 
     the United States Congress to urge federal, state and 
     railroad officials to support and assist Rockland County's 
     effort to establish a quiet zone on the west shore (river) 
     freight rail line within Rockland County; to the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure.
