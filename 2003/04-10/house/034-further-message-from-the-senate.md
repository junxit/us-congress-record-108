# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 1584

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks,

concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 1584. An act to implement effective measures to stop
```
