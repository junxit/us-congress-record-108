# BRUCE WOODBURY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2254

```
                  BRUCE WOODBURY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2254) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1101 Colorado Street in Boulder City, Nevada, 
as the ``Bruce Woodbury Post Office building,'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time and passed.
```
