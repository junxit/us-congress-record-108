# BRIAN C. HICKEY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  BRIAN C. HICKEY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2452) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 339 Hicksville Road in Bethpage, New York, as 
the ``Brian C. Hickey Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time and passed.
```
