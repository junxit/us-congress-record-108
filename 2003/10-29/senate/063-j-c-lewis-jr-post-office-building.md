# J.C. LEWIS, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  J.C. LEWIS, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2533) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 10701 Abercorn Street in Savannah, Georgia, 
as the ``J.C. Lewis, Jr. Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time and passed.
```
