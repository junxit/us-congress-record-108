# EDDIE MAE STEWARD POST OFFICE

```
                     EDDIE MAE STEWARD POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 1883) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1601-1 Main Street in Jacksonville, Florida, 
as the ``Eddie Mae Steward Post Office,'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time and passed.
```
