# BARBARA B. KENNELLY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                BARBARA B. KENNELLY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2746) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 141 Weston Street in Hartford, Connecticut, 
as the ``Barbara B. Kennelly Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time and passed.
```
