# ROBERT A. BORSKI POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2328

```
                 ROBERT A. BORSKI POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2328) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2001 East Willard Street in Philadelphia, 
Pennsylvania, as the ``Robert A. Borski Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time and passed.
```
