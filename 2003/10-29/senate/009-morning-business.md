# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 30 
minutes. The first 15 minutes is under the control of the Senator from 
Texas, Mrs. Hutchison, or her designee, and the second 15 minutes is 
under the control of the Democratic leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Missouri.
  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I claim the time that has been set aside for 
the Senator from Texas.
```
