# WALT DISNEY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1610

```
                    WALT DISNEY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1610) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 230 East Ritchie Avenue in Marceline, 
Missouri, as the ``Walt Disney Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time and passed.
```
