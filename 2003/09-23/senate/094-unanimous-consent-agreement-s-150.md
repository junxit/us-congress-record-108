# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 150

**Speaking:** Burns, Conrad R. (B001126) [R-MT]

**Measures:** S. 150

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 150

  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate Commerce Committee reports out S. 150, the Internet Tax 
Nondiscrimination Act of 2003, the bill be referred to the Committee on 
Finance for up to 30 calendar days, and if the Committee on Finance 
does not report out the bill within that time, it will be discharged 
and placed on the Legislative Calendar.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
