# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2003

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2003

  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Wednesday, September 24. I further ask that following the prayer and 
the pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then begin a 
period of morning business for 1 hour, with the first 30 minutes under 
the control of Senator Hutchison or her designee and the remaining 30 
minutes under the control of the minority leader or his designee; 
provided that following morning business, the Senate proceed to 
consideration of Calendar No. 278, H.R. 2765, the District of Columbia 
appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
