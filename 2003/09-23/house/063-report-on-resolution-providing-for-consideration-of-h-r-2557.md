# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2557, WATER RESOURCES DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 2003

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2557, WATER 
                   RESOURCES DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 2003

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 108-282) on the resolution (H. Res. 375) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2557) to provide for the 
conservation and development of water and related resources, to 
authorize the Secretary of the Army to construct various projects for 
improvements to rivers and harbors of the United States, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
