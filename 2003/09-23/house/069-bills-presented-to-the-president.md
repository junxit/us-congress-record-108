# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on September 22, 2003, 
he presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills.

       H.R. 13. To reauthorize the Museum and Library Services 
     Act, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 659, To amend section 242 of the National Housing Act 
     regarding the requirements for mortgage insurance under such 
     Act for hospitals.
       H.R. 978. To amend chapter 84 of title 5, United States 
     Code, to provide that certain Federal annuity computations 
     are adjusted by 1 percentage point relating to periods of 
     receiving disability payments, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
