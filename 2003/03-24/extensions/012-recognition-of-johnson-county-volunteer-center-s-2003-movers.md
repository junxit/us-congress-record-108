                               of kansas

                  in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 24, 2003

  Mr. MOORE. Mr. Speaker, the Volunteer Center of Johnson County is 
hosting the third annual Movers and Shakers Recognition on April 22nd, 
to honor 84 young people from Johnson County, Kansas, who have 
performed meaningful community service. Students in middle schools and 
high schools have been nominated by school personnel and nonprofit 
agencies based on their commitment and dedication to community service. 
Volunteering has been recognized as a key component in a child's 
education and I am proud that Johnson County students are active and 
willing to serve their communities.
