# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRAHAM of South Carolina. For the information of all Senators, we 
will return to the budget resolution tomorrow morning. The chairman and 
ranking member of the Budget Committee have exchanged copies of the 
final amendments that remain in order to the resolution. We now have a 
number of amendments to dispose of prior to a final vote on the 
resolution. The Senate will begin voting on these remaining amendments 
upon convening tomorrow morning. Members are asked to remain in the 
Chamber during the second phase of the so-called vote-arama. With the 
cooperation of all Members, we will be able to move through the 
remaining amendments in a more efficient and orderly manner. The Senate 
will proceed to a final vote on the budget resolution no later than 4 
p.m. on Wednesday. Therefore, I would advise my colleagues that we have 
a lot of work before us this week.

                          ____________________
```
