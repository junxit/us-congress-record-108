# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Bennett). Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 5 p.m., with time to be equally divided 
between the leaders or their designees.
```
