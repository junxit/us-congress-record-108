# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 1900

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Mr. Trandahl, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled a 
bill of the House of the following title, which was thereupon signed by 
the Speaker.

       H.R. 1900. An act to award gold medal to Jackie Robinson 
     (posthumously), in recognition of his many contributions to 
     the Nation, and to express the sense of the Congress that 
     there should be a national day in recognition of Jackie 
     Robinson.

                          ____________________
```
