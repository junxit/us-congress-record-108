# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 12:45 with the time equally divided between the majority leader 
and the Senator from North Dakota or their designees.
  The Senator from Wyoming.

                          ____________________
```
