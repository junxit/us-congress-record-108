# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business for not to exceed 
60 minutes, with the first 30 minutes under the control of the minority 
leader or his designee and the second 30 minutes under the control of 
the Senator from Texas, Mrs. Hutchison, or her designee.
  Who yields time?
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I yield 10 minutes to the Senator from 
Nebraska, and I ask unanimous consent that both sides have their full 
30 minutes.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Nebraska is recognized for 10 minutes.

                          ____________________
```
