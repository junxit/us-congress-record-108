# CONSENT TO ASSEMBLE OUTSIDE THE SEAT OF GOVERNMENT

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 1

```
           CONSENT TO ASSEMBLE OUTSIDE THE SEAT OF GOVERNMENT

  Mr. FRIST. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 1, which is at 
the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 1) regarding consent 
     to assemble outside the seat of government.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. FRIST. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
concurrent resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 1) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
