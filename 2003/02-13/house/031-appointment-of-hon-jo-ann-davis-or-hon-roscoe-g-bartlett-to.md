# APPOINTMENT OF HON. JO ANN DAVIS OR HON. ROSCOE G. BARTLETT TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH FEBRUARY 25, 2003

```
 APPOINTMENT OF HON. JO ANN DAVIS OR HON. ROSCOE G. BARTLETT TO ACT AS 
   SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS 
                       THROUGH FEBRUARY 25, 2003

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                February 13, 2003.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Jo Ann Davis or, if not 
     available to perform this duty, the Honorable Roscoe G. 
     Bartlett to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills 
     and joint resolutions through February 25, 2003.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
