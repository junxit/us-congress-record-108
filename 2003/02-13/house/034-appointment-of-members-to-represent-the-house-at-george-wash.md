# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO REPRESENT THE HOUSE AT GEORGE WASHINGTON'S BIRTHDAY CEREMONIES

**Speaking:** Foley, Mark (F000238) [R-FL]

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO REPRESENT THE HOUSE AT GEORGE WASHINGTON'S 
                          BIRTHDAY CEREMONIES

  Mr. FOLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it shall be in 
order for the Speaker to appoint two Members of the House, one upon the 
recommendation of the minority leader,

[[Page H677]]

to represent the House of Representatives at appropriate ceremonies for 
the observance of George Washington's birthday to be held on Friday, 
February 21, 2003.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
