# NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS

**Speaking:** Campbell, Ben Nighthorse (C000077) [R-CO]

**Measures:** S.Res. 248, S. 1529, S. 1721

```
                      NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS

  (At the request of Mr. McConnell, the following statement was ordered 
to be printed in the Record.)

[[Page S14458]]

                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. CAMPBELL. Mr. President, I announce that the Committee on Indian 
Affairs will meet on Thursday, November 13, 2003, at 10 a.m., in room 
485 of the Russell Senate Office Building to conduct a business meeting 
to consider S. 1529, the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act amendments of 
2003; S. 1721, the American Indian Probate Reform Act of 2003; and S. 
Res. 248, a resolution expressing the sense of the Senate concerning 
the Individual Indian Money Account Trust Funds lawsuit.
  Those wishing additional information may contact the Indian Affairs 
Committee at 224-2251.
```
