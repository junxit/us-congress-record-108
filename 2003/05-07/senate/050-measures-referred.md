# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 1596, H.R. 1625, H.R. 1740

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first time and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1596. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 2318 Woodson Road in St. 
     Louis, Missouri, as the ``Timothy Michael Gaffney Post Office 
     Building''; to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 1625. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 1114 Main Avenue in Clifton, 
     New Jersey, as the ``Robert P. Hammer Post Office Building''; 
     to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 1740. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 1502 East Kiest Boulevard in 
     Dallas, Texas, as the ``Dr. Caesar A.W. Clark, Sr. Post 
     Office Building''; to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
