# Text of Senate Amendment 1219

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

```
  SA 1219. Ms. COLLINS (for herself and Ms. Snowe) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by her to the bill H.R. 2658, making 
appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2004, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table, as follows:

       Insert after section 8123 the following:
       Sec. 8124. Of the amount appropriated by title II of this 
     Act under the heading ``Shipbuilding and Conversion, Navy'', 
     $20,000,000 shall be available for DDG-51 modernization.
                                 ______
```
