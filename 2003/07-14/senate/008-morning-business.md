# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK], Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there now be 
a period for the transaction of morning business until the hour of 5:30 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for not to exceed 5 
minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. STEVENS. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak for 10 
minutes in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
