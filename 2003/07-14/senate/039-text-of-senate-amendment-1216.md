# Text of Senate Amendment 1216

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
  SA 1216. Mr. GRASSLEY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 2658, making appropriations for the Department 
of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2004, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table, as follows:

       On page 120, between lines 17 and 18, insert the following:
       Sec. 8124. (a) Subsection (a) of section 2474 of title 10, 
     United States Code, is amended by striking ``depot-level 
     activity'' and inserting ``industrial activity''.
       (b) Subsection (b)(1)(A) of such section is amended by 
     inserting ``manufacturing or'' after ``including any''.
                                 ______
```
