# Text of Senate Amendment 1225

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
  SA 1225. Mr. STEVENS (for Mr. Dodd) proposed an amendment to 
amendment SA 1217 proposed by Mr. Stevens to the bill H.R. 2658, making 
appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2004, and for other purposes; as follows:

       On page 120, between lines 17 and 18, insert the following:
       Sec. 8124. Of the total amount appropriated by title IV 
     under the heading ``Research, Development, Test and 
     Evaluation, Army'', up to $3,000,000 may be used for the 
     Broad Area Unmanned Responsive Resupply Operations aircraft 
     program.
                                 ______
```
