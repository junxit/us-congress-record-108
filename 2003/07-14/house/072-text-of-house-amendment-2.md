# Text of House Amendment 2

**Speaking:** Holt, Rush (H001032) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2691

```
H.R. 2691

                          Offered By: Mr. Holt

       Amendment No. 2: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following section:
       Sec. 3____. None of the funds made available in this Act 
     may be used to manage recreational snowmobile use in 
     Yellowstone and Grand Teton National Parks and the John D. 
     Rockefeller, Jr. Memorial Parkway, except in accordance with 
     National Park Service One-Year Delay Rule published November 
     18, 2002 (36 CFR part 7, RIN 1024-AD06).
```
