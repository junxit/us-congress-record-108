# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Rahall, Nick J., II (R000011) [D-WV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2691

```
H.R. 2691

                         Offered By: Mr. Rahall

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:
       Sec. ____. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to kill, or assist others in killing, any Bison in 
     the Yellowstone National Park herd.
```
