# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow the Senate will resume consideration of the budget resolution. 
At 9:45 tomorrow morning, the Senate will begin a series of votes on 
the remaining amendments on the budget resolution. There will be a 
number of amendments to be included in this stacked series of votes, 
and Members are encouraged to remain in the Chamber during this very 
busy session. With the cooperation of all Senators, we will be able to 
move to a vote on final passage at a reasonable time tomorrow.
```
