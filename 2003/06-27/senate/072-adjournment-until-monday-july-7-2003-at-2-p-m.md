# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 7, 2003, AT 2 P.M.

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 231

```
           ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 7, 2003, AT 2 P.M.

  Mr. FRIST. If there is no further business to come before the Senate, 
I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in adjournment under the 
provisions of H. Con. Res. 231; further, that the Senate adjourn as an 
additional mark of respect for Senator Strom Thurmond.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 4:14 p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, July 7, 2003, at 2 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
