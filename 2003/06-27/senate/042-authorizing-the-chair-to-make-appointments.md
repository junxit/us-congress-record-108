# AUTHORIZING THE CHAIR TO MAKE APPOINTMENTS

```
               AUTHORIZING THE CHAIR TO MAKE APPOINTMENTS

  Mr. FRIST. Madam President, notwithstanding the Senate's adjournment 
or recess for the Fourth of July recess, I ask unanimous consent that 
the Chair be authorized to make chair appointments.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
