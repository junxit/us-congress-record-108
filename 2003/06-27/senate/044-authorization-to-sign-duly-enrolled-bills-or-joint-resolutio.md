# AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
     AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that during this 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader or the assistant 
majority leader or Senator Allen be authorized to sign duly enrolled 
bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
