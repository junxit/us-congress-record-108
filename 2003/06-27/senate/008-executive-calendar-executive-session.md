                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations on 
today's Executive Calendar: Calendar No. 248, the nomination of LTG 
John P. Abizaid to be General; and Calendar Nos. 254 through 277, and 
all nominations on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, the Army, and 
the Navy.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nominations be confirmed, en 
bloc; that the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table; that the 
President be immediately notified of the Senate's action; and that the 
Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nominations were considered and confirmed, en bloc, as follows:
