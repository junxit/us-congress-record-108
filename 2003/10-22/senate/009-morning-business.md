# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until 
11:30 a.m., with the time equally divided between the two leaders or 
their designees. The first 30 minutes will be under the control of the 
Senator from Texas, Mrs. Hutchison, or her designee, and the second 30 
minutes will be under the control of the Democratic leader or his 
designee.

                          ____________________
```
