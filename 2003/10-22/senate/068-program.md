# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow, following morning business, the Senate will begin 
consideration of the Transportation appropriations bill. It is my hope 
we can complete action on this bill in an expedited manner, and the two 
managers will be here tomorrow morning to begin working through 
amendments to that bill. Senators should expect amendments to be 
offered and debated throughout the course of the day. Therefore, 
rollcall votes should be expected throughout the day as well. Senators 
will be notified when the first vote is scheduled.
```
