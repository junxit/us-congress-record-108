# AMERICA, A LIBERATING NATION

```
                      AMERICA, A LIBERATING NATION

  (Mr. HAYWORTH asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HAYWORTH. Popular representation, Mr. Speaker, in our 
constitutional Republic is a wonderful thing. It has led some to say 
that the preceding speaker in the well would make a good President. It 
has led others to say that the preceding speaker in the well would make 
a good President of France.
  The fact remains, Mr. Speaker, that the United States of America rose 
up against a tyrant, not only because of weapons of mass destruction, 
but because the tyrant himself was a weapon of mass destruction. Take a 
look at the mass graves, the children buried with their dolls, the 
millions of people who were sacrificed by the regime of Saddam Hussein. 
And yet there are those, earnest in their intent, to tell us somehow 
that this Nation is evil, to go to sloganeering: ``No blood for oil.'' 
The fact remains, historically it was that tyrant who invaded Kuwait 
for oil, it was that tyrant who went to war with Iran for oil.
  The fact is, the United States of America is a liberating Nation, not 
a conquering Nation. We stand here unashamedly rejoicing in that fact.

                          ____________________
```
