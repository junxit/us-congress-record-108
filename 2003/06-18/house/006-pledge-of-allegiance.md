# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the gentleman from Florida (Mr. Lincoln 
Diaz-Balart) come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of 
Allegiance.
  Mr. LINCOLN DIAZ-BALART of Florida led the Pledge of Allegiance as 
follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of 
     America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one nation 
     under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.

                          ____________________
```
