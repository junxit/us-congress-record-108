# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1625

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on June 17, 2003 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bill.

       H.R. 1625. To designate the facility of the United States 
     Postal Service located at 1114 Main Avenue in Clifton, New 
     Jersey, as the ``Robert P. Hammer Post Office Building''.
```
