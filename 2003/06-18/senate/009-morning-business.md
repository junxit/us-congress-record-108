# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
begin a period for morning business until the hour of 10 a.m., with the 
time equally divided between the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from the great State of New Hampshire.
```
