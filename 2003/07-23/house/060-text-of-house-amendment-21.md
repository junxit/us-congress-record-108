# Text of House Amendment 21

**Speaking:** Weiner, Anthony D. (W000792) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2800

```
H.R. 2800

                         Offered By: Mr. Weiner

       Amendment No. 21: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               prohibition on assistance to saudi arabia

       Sec. ____. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to provide assistance to the Government of Saudi 
     Arabia.
```
