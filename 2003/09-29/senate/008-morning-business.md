# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business until the hour of 2 
p.m., with the time equally divided and controlled in the usual form.
  In my capacity as a Senator from the State of Alaska, I suggest the 
absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
