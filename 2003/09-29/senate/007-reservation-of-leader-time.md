# RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                       RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the leadership 
time is reserved.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. In my capacity as a Senator from the State 
of Alaska, I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum call 
be rescinded.
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
