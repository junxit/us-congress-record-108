# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, for the information of Senators, three 
back-to-back rollcall votes will commence at 11 a.m. tomorrow in 
relation to the pending amendments to the appropriations bill. The 
first vote will be on the Feingold amendment No. 200 dealing with 
military training. The second vote will be on the Mikulski amendment 
No. 61 on public-private competition. The third vote will be on the 
Murray amendment No. 39 regarding community action programs, CAP.
  The managers of the bill will continue to work with the Members in an 
effort to reach short time agreements on any remaining amendments so 
that the Senate can complete action on the bill at a reasonable time 
tomorrow. Additional votes will occur throughout the day on Thursday. I 
thank the Members for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
