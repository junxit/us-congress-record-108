# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


           committee on commerce, science, and transportation

  Ms. COLLINS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation be authorized to 
meet on Wednesday, January 22, 2003, at 2:30 p.m., in SR-253, to 
consider the nomination of Asa Hutchinson to be Under Secretary of the 
Department of Homeland Security.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
