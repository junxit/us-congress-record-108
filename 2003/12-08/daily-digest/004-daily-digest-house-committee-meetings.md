# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2673

```
Committee Meetings
CONFERENCE REPORT--CONSOLIDATED APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2004
Committee on Rules: Granted, by voice vote, a rule waiving all points 
of order against the conference report to accompany H.R. 2673, 
Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2004, and against its consideration. 
The rule provides that the conference report shall be considered as 
read. Testimony was heard from Chairman Young and Representatives Obey 
and Andrews.
```
