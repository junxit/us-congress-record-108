# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 10 a.m., with the time to be equally divided between the 
Senator from Texas and the Democratic leader or their designees.
  The Senator from Montana.
```
