# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1006, THE CAPTIVE WILDLIFE SAFETY ACT

**Speaking:** McKeon, Howard P. "Buck" (M000508) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1006

```
   REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1006, THE CAPTIVE 
                          WILDLIFE SAFETY ACT

  Mr. McKEON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the gentleman 
from Louisiana (Mr. Alexander) be removed as a cosponsor of H.R. 1006, 
the Captive Wildlife Safety Act, as he was mistakenly added as a 
cosponsor to this legislation.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
