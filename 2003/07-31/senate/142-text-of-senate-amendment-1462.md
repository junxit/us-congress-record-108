# Text of Senate Amendment 1462

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
  SA 1462. Ms. CANTWELL submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by her to the bill S. 14, to enhance the energy security of the United 
States, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       At the end of subtitle G, add the following:

     SEC. 11____. RELIEF FOR RATEPAYERS.

       Any contract for the sale of electric energy at wholesale 
     that contains rates, terms, or conditions affected by any 
     manipulative, deceptive, or fraudulent activity by a party to 
     the contract shall be unenforceable.
                                 ______
```
