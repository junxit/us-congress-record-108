# Text of Senate Amendment 1512

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
  SA 1512. Mr. SCHUMER (for himself and Mrs. Clinton) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill S. 14, to enhance 
the energy security of the United States, and for other purposes; which 
was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       
       At the end of title I, add the following:

                       Subtitle D--Miscellaneous

     SEC. ____. FINGER LAKES NATIONAL FOREST WITHDRAWAL.

       All Federal land within the boundary of Finger Lakes 
     National Forest in the State of New York is withdrawn from--
       (1) all forms of entry, appropriation, or disposal under 
     the public land laws; and
       (2) disposition under all laws relating to oil and gas 
     leasing.
                                 ______
```
