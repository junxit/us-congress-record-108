# Text of Senate Amendment 1460

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
  SA 1460. Ms. CANTWELL submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by her to the bill S. 14, to enhance the energy security of the United 
States, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       Strike section 1182 and insert the following:

     SEC. ____. PROHIBITION OF MARKET-BASED RATES.

       Section 205(a) of the Federal Power Act (16 U.S.C. 824d(a)) 
     is amended by adding at the end the following: ``Nothing in 
     this Act confers on the Commission authority to authorize 
     market-based rates for the sale of electric energy at 
     wholesale.''.
                                 ______
```
