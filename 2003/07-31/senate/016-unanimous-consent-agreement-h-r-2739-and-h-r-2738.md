# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2739 AND H.R. 2738

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2738, H.R. 2739

```
          UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2739 AND H.R. 2738

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that immediately 
following the vote on the passage of the Energy bill, all debate time 
be yielded back and the Senate proceed to a vote on passage of H.R. 
2739, the Singapore bill, to be followed by a vote on passage of H.R. 
2738, the Chile free-trade legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
