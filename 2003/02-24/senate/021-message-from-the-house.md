# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 2

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of January 7, 2003, the Secretary of 
the Senate, on February 18, 2003, during the recess of the Senate, 
received a message from the House of Representatives announcing that 
the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill.

       H.J. Res. 2. A joint resolution making consolidated 
     appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2003, 
     and for other purposes.

  Under the authority of the order of January 7, 2003, the enrolled 
bill was signed by the Acting President pro tempore (Mr. Frist) on 
February 19, 2003.
```
