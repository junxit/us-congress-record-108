# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 28, 2003

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

**Measures:** S. 14

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 28, 2003

  Mr. HATCH. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until 11 a.m., 
Monday, July 28. I further ask unanimous consent that following the 
prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate then resume consideration of S. 14, the Energy bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.




                          ____________________
```
