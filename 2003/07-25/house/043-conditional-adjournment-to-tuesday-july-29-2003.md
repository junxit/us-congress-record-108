# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, JULY 29, 2003

```
           CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, JULY 29, 2003

  Mr. DREIER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 4 p.m. on Tuesday, July 29, 2003, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 259, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
