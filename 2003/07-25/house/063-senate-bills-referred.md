# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 62

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. Con. Res. 62. Concurrent resolution honoring the service 
     and sacrifice of Korean War veterans; to the Committee on 
     Veteran Affairs; in addition to the Committee on 
     International Relations for a period to be subsequently 
     determined by the Speaker, in each case for consideration of 
     such provisions as fall within the jurisdiction of the 
     committee concerned.
```
