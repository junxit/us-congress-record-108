# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, JULY 30, 2003, TO FILE A PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENTS OF TRANSPORTATION, TREASURY AND INDEPENDENT AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS BILL...

**Speaking:** Young, C. W. Bill (Y000031) [R-FL]

```
PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, JULY 
30, 2003, TO FILE A PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENTS OF TRANSPORTATION, 
      TREASURY AND INDEPENDENT AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 2004

  Mr. YOUNG of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Appropriations have until midnight, July 30, 2003, to file 
a privileged report, making appropriations for the Departments of 
Transportation and Treasury, and independent agencies for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2004, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```
