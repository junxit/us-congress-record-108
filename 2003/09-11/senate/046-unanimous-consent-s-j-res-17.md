# UNANIMOUS CONSENT--S.J. Res. 17

```
                    UNANIMOUS CONSENT--S.J. Res. 17

  Mr. FRIST. I ask consent that the debate time allocated for the 
consideration of S.J. Res. 17 be delayed to begin at 9:30 a.m. on 
Tuesday, September 16, with all other provisions remaining in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. FRIST. For the information of all Senators, this consent will now 
move the debate and vote in relation to the FCC rule resolution from 
Monday evening to Tuesday evening. With this consent granted, there 
will be no votes on Monday. The next vote will occur Tuesday morning.

                          ____________________
```
