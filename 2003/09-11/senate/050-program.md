# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. For the information of all Senators, on Monday the Senate 
will resume debate on the energy and water appropriations bill. 
Senators Feinstein and Kennedy will have an amendment to that 
legislation which will be debated during Monday's session.
  As announced earlier, any votes ordered on Monday will be delayed 
until Tuesday.
  In addition, on Monday, under a previous understanding, the Senate 
will debate a motion to go to conference on the partial-birth abortion 
bill. Also, as previously ordered, the FCC rule resolution will be 
debated and voted on Tuesday morning.
```
