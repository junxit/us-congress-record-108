# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, today we will remember the second 
anniversary of the September 11 tragedy with a series of moments of 
silence. These times will be announced by the ringing of a bell of 
remembrance just outside these Chamber doors.
  In a moment, at a time that corresponds with the initial attack on 
the World Trade Center, the Democratic leader will join me in ringing 
that bell just off the floor.
```
