# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 3289

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN], Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3289

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 3289

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that at 11 a.m. on Monday, 
November 3, the Senate proceed to the consideration of the conference 
report to accompany H.R. 3289, the Iraq-Afghanistan supplemental. I 
further ask that the time until 5 p.m. be equally divided between the 
chairman and ranking member of the committee, with the minority time 
allocated as follows: Senator Byrd, 60 minutes; Senator Durbin, 30 
minutes; Senator Kennedy, 30 minutes; Senator Dorgan, 15 minutes; 
Senator Dayton, 10 minutes; Senator Daschle, 30 minutes. I further ask 
unanimous consent that at 5 p.m. the conference report be adopted with 
the motion to reconsider laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Nevada.
  Mr. REID. I ask the distinguished majority leader to amend the time 
of the minority to have Senator Dayton, 15 minutes; Senator Daschle, 20 
minutes; Senator Harkin, 5 minutes. It all works out to the same amount 
of time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, the request, with the 
modification, is agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
