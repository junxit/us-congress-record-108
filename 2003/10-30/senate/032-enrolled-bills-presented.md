# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, October 30, 2003, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 470. An act to extend the authority for the construction 
     of a memorial to Martin Luther King, Jr.
       S. 926. An act to amend section 5379 of title 5, United 
     States Code, to increase the annual and aggregate limits on 
     student loan repayments by Federal agencies.

  The enrolled bills previously signed by the Speaker of the House, 
were signed on today, by the President pro tempore (Mr. Stevens).

                          ____________________
```
