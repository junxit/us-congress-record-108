# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Burns, Max (B001249) [R-GA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. MAX BURNS

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 29, 2003

  Mr. BURNS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to provide an explanation for my 
absence during votes yesterday evening.

Congressional Delegation trip to Iraq. I appreciate the opportunity to 
visit our troops serving overseas and to witness firsthand the 
situation in Iraq. Our delegation arrived into the Washington area
```
