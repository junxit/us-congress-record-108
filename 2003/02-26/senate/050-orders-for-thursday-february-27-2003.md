# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2003

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2003

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 12 
noon, Thursday, February 27. I further ask that following the prayer 
and pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate return to 
executive session and resume the consideration of the nomination of 
Miguel Estrada to be a Circuit Judge for the District of Columbia 
Circuit.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
