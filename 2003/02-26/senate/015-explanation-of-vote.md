# EXPLANATION OF VOTE

```
                          EXPLANATION OF VOTE

  Mr. NELSON of Nebraska. Mr. President, on Monday, February 24, I was 
unable to be present to vote on S. 151, the PROTECT Act, because I was 
part of the U.S. Presidential Delegation attending the inauguration of 
His Excellency Roh Moo-Huyn, President of the Republic of Korea. Had I 
been present I would have voted ``aye.''
```
