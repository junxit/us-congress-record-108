# SENATE RESOLUTION 194--TO DISCHARGE THE HENRY W. SAAD NOMINATION

**Measures:** S.Res. 194

```
    SENATE RESOLUTION 194--TO DISCHARGE THE HENRY W. SAAD NOMINATION

  Mr. FRIST submitted the following resolution; which was submitted and 
read:

                              S. Res. 194

       Resolved, That the Committee on the Judiciary is discharged 
     from the further consideration of the nomination of Henry W. 
     Saad, of Michigan, to be a United States Circuit Judge for 
     the Sixth Circuit.
```
