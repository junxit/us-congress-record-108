# SENATE RESOLUTION 193--TO DISCHARGE THE SUSAN BIEKE NEILSON NOMINATION

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 193--TO DISCHARGE THE SUSAN BIEKE NEILSON NOMINATION

  Mr. FRIST submitted the following resolution; which was ordered to 
lie over, under the rule:

                              S. Res. 193

       Resolved, That the Committee on the Judiciary is discharged 
     from the further consideration of the nomination of Susan 
     Bieke Neilson, of Michigan, to be a United States Circuit 
     Judge for the Sixth Circuit.
```
