# SENATE RESOLUTION 195--TO DISCHARGE THE RICHARD A. GRIFFIN NOMINATION

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 195--TO DISCHARGE THE RICHARD A. GRIFFIN NOMINATION

  Mr. FRIST submitted the following resolution; which was ordered to 
lie over, under the rule:

                              S. Res. 195

       Resolved, That the Committee on the Judiciary is discharged 
     from the further consideration of the nomination of Richard 
     A. Griffin, of Michigan, to be a United States Circuit Judge 
     for the Sixth Circuit.
```
