# Text of Senate Amendment 1746

**Speaking:** Voinovich, George V. (V000126) [R-OH]

```
  SA 1746. Mr. VOINOVICH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 2691, making appropriations for the Department 
of the Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2004, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       On page 22, line 9, after ``2005'' insert the following: 
     ``, of which $1,000,000 shall be available to the Dayton/
     Montgomery Port Authority for the restoration and development 
     of buildings in the West Third St. National Historic District 
     in Dayton, Ohio''.
                                 ______
```
