# Text of Senate Amendment 1743

**Speaking:** Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

```
  SA 1743. Mr. LEAHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 2691, making appropriations for the Department of 
the Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 2004, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Funds appropriated for the Green Mountain National Forest 
     previously or in this report may be used for the acquisition 
     of lands in the Blueberry Lake area.
                                 ______
```
