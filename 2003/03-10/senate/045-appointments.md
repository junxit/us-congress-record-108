# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 276d-276g., as amended, appoints the Senator from 
Idaho (Mr. Crapo) as Chairman of the Senate Delegation to the Canada to 
the Canada-U.S. Interparliamentary Group conference during the 108th 
Congress.
  The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, in accordance with 22 
U.S. 1928a-1928d, as amended, appoints the Senator from Delaware (Mr. 
Binden) as Vice Chairman of the Senate Delegation to the NATO 
Parliamentary Assembly during the 108th Congress.
```
