# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2754

```
Committee Meetings
  No committee meetings were held.

Joint Meetings
OCTOBER EMPLOYMENT
Joint Economic Committee: Committee concluded a hearing to examine the 
current employment-unemployment situation for October 2003, focusing on 
nonfarm payroll employment, and productivity and economic growth, after 
receiving testimony from Kathleen P. Utgoff, Commissioner, Bureau of 
Labor Statistics, Department of Labor.
ENERGY AND WATER DEVELOPMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT
Conferees agreed to file a conference report on the differences between 
the Senate and House passed versions of H.R. 2754, making 
appropriations for energy and water development for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 2004.
```
