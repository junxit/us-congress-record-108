# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 6, H.R. 2754

```
Committee Meetings
CONFERENCE REPORT--ENERGY AND WATER DEVELOPMENT APPROPRIATIONS
Committee on Rules: Granted, by voice vote, a rule waiving all points of 
order against the conference report to accompany H.R. 2754, making 
appropriations for energy and water development for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2004, and against its consideration.
CONFERENCE REPORT--ENERGY POLICY ACT OF 2003
Committee on Rules: Granted, by voice vote, a rule waiving all points of 
order against the conference report to accompany H.R. 6, Energy Policy Act 
of 2003, and against its consideration. The rule provides that the 
conference report shall be considered as read. Testimony was heard from 
Chairman Tauzin and Representative Barton of Texas.
F
```
