# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on October 30, 2003 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.J. Res. 52. Recognizing the Dr. Samuel D. Harris National 
     Museum of Dentistry, an affiliate of the Smithsonian 
     Institution in Baltimore, Maryland, as the official national 
     museum of dentistry in the United States.
       H.J. Res. 75. Making further continuing appropriations for 
     the fiscal year 2004, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 1516. An act to provide for the establishment by the 
     Secretary of Veterans Affairs of additional cemeteries in the 
     National Cemetery Administration.
```
