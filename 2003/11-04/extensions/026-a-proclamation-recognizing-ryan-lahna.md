# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING RYAN LAHNA

```
                 A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING RYAN LAHNA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Ryan Lahna has devoted himself to serving others through his 
membership in the Boy Scouts of America; and
  Whereas, Ryan Lahna has shared his time and talent with the community 
in which he resides; and
  Whereas, Ryan Lahna has demonstrated a commitment to meet challenges 
with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, Ryan Lahna must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication he put forth in earning the Eagle Scout Award;
  Therefore, I join with Troop 402, the residents of Coshocton, and the 
entire 18th Congressional District in congratulating Ryan Lahna as he 
receives the Eagle Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```
