# ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 1, MEDICARE PRESCRIPTION DRUG AND MODERNIZATION ACT OF 2003

```
ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 
      1, MEDICARE PRESCRIPTION DRUG AND MODERNIZATION ACT OF 2003

  Mr. BISHOP of New York. Mr. Speaker, subject to rule XXII, clause 
7(c), I hereby announce my intention to offer a motion to instruct on 
H.R. 1, the prescription drug bill.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       Mr. Bishop of New York moves that the managers on the part 
     of the House at the conference on the disagreeing votes of 
     the two Houses on the Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 1 be 
     instructed to reject division B of the House bill.

                          ____________________
```
