**Speaking:** LaTourette, Steven C. (L000553) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2622

 AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 
        2622, FAIR AND ACCURATE CREDIT TRANSACTIONS ACT OF 2003

  Mr. LaTOURETTE. Mr. Speaker, on a gratifying endorsement of my
