# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 1668

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Mr. Trandahl, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly an 
enrolled bill of the House of the following title, which was thereupon 
signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 1668. An act to designate the United States courthouse 
     located at 101 North Fifth Street in Muskogee, Oklahoma, as 
     the ``Ed Edmondson United States Courthouse''.
```
