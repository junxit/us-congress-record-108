# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the 
House of the following title:

       H.R. 1668. An act to designate the United States courthouse 
     located at 101 North Fifth Street in Muskogee, Oklahoma, as 
     the ``Ed Edmondson United States Courthouse''.

                          ____________________
```
