# Text of Senate Amendment 1969

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
  SA 1969. Mr. BYRD proposed an amendment to the bill H.R. 2800, making 
appropriations for foreign operations, export financing, and related

[[Page S13265]]

programs for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2004, and for other 
purposes, as follows:

       At the appropriate place, add the following:
       Sec. XX. (a) None of the funds made available by this Act 
     or any other Act may be used by the Coalition Provisional 
     Authority (CPA) unless the Administrator of the Coalition 
     Provisional Authority is an officer of the United States 
     Government appointed by the President by and with the advice 
     and consent of the Senate.
       (b) This provision shall be effective March 1, 2004.
                                 ______
```
