# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there now 
be a period for morning business, with Senators entitled to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each.
  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, reserving the right to object, I did not 
hear what the unanimous consent request was.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I was just asking unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business, with Senators entitled to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  Mrs. BOXER. Fine.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from California.

                          ____________________
```
