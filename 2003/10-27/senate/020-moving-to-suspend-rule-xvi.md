# MOVING TO SUSPEND RULE XVI

**Speaking:** Lugar, Richard G. (L000504) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2800

```
                       MOVING TO SUSPEND RULE XVI

  Mr. LUGAR. Mr. President, I hereby provide notice that I intend to 
move to suspend rule XVI of the Standing Rules of the Senate during the 
Senate's consideration of H.R. 2800 in order to offer the amendment No. 
1974 to that bill.
  (The amendment is printed in today's Record under ``Morning 
Business.'')
```
