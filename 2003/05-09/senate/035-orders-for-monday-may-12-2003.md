# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 12, 2003

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 12, 2003

  Mr. FRIST. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 2 
p.m., Monday, May 12. I further ask that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the Journal of the 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and that the Senate 
immediately proceed to the consideration of Calendar No. 90, S. 2, the 
reconciliation bill, as provided under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
