# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 16

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 16

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 16 is at the 
desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 16) to authorize salary adjustments for 
     Justices and judges of the United States for fiscal year 
     2003.

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask for its second reading and object 
to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
