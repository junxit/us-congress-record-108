# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that morning 
business be extended for 20 minutes, and that the additional minutes be 
evenly divided between the Democrats and the Republicans.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the Senator from South 
Carolina be recognized for 20 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from South Carolina is recognized 
for 20 minutes.

                          ____________________
```
