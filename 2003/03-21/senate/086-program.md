# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. For the information of all Senators, on Monday the Senate 
will be in a period of morning business throughout the afternoon. There 
will be no rollcall votes on Monday.
  I want to thank my colleagues on both sides of the aisle for the 
constructive week we have had. Under the previous agreement, we now 
have a time certain for passage of the budget resolution, and I thank 
my colleagues for their cooperation.
  The Senate will return to the budget resolution on Tuesday morning at 
9:30 and immediately begin a long series of stacked rollcall votes. 
Members are encouraged to remain in the Chamber during Tuesday's 
session as numerous votes are expected. With the cooperation of all 
Senators, we can reach a final vote on the resolution in a timely 
manner.

                          ____________________
```
