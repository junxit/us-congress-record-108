# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 534

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 534

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 534

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 534 is at the 
desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 534) to amend title 18, United States Code, to 
     prohibit human cloning.

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I ask for its second reading and object 
to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
read a second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
