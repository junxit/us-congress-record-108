# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO CONGRESSIONAL-EXECUTIVE COMMISSION ON THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA

```
  APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO CONGRESSIONAL-EXECUTIVE COMMISSION ON THE 
                       PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 6913, and the order of

Executive Commission on the People's Republic of China:
```
