# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 4, S. 151

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  A bill and a concurrent resolution of the Senate of the following 
titles were taken from the Speaker's table and, under the rule, 
referred as follows:

       S. 151. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, with 
     respect to the sexual exploitation of children, to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
       S. Con. Res. 4. Concurrent Resolution welcoming the 
     expression of support of 18 European nations for the 
     enforcement of United Nations Security Counsel Resolution 
     1441; to the Committee on International Relations.

                          ____________________
```
