# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10 a.m., with the time equally divided 
between the Senator from Texas and the Democratic leader or their 
designees.
```
