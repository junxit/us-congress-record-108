# AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Sununu, John E. (S001078) [R-NH]

```
         AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. SUNUNU. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that during this 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader, the assistant majority 
leader, or Senator Warner be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or 
joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
