# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 15, 2003

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 15, 2003

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 9:30 
a.m., Wednesday, January 15. I further ask that following the prayer 
and pledge,

[[Page 518]]

the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and there then be a period of morning business 
until 10:30 a.m., with the time equally divided and Senators permitted 
to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
