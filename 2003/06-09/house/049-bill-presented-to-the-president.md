# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on June 6, 2003 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bill.

       H.R. 192. To amend the Microenterprise for Self-Reliance 
     Act of 2000 and the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 to 
     increase assistance for the poorest people in developing 
     countries under microenterprise assistance programs under 
     those Acts, and for other purposes.
```
