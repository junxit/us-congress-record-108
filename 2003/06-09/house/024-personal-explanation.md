# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, I regret that I could not be 
present today, Monday, June 9, 2003, to vote on rollcall vote Nos. 249, 
250, and 251 due to a family medical emergency.
  Had I been present, I would have voted: ``yea'' on rollcall vote No. 
249 on H.R. 1610, to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 120 East Ritchie Avenue in Marceline, MO, as 
the ``Walt Disney Post Office Building''; ``yea'' on rollcall vote No. 
250 on H. Con. Res. 162, honoring the city of Dayton, OH, and its many 
partners, for hosting ``Inventing Flight: The Centennial Celebration'', 
a celebration of the centennial of Wilbur and Orville Wright's first 
flight; and ``yea'' on rollcall vote No. 251 on S. 763, to designate 
the Federal building and United States courthouse located at 46 East 
Ohio Street in Indianapolis, IN, as the ``Birch Bayh Federal Building 
and United States Courthouse.''

                          ____________________
```
