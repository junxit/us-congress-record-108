# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that on Thursday, at a time determined by the majority leader, 
with the concurrence of the Democratic leader, the Senate proceed to 
executive session and that the nomination of Gordon England, to be 
Deputy Secretary of Homeland Security, be discharged from the 
Governmental Affairs Committee; further, that the Senate then proceed 
to its consideration; that there be 20 minutes of debate equally 
divided between the chairman and ranking member; provided further, that 
following the use or yielding back of time, the Senate proceed to a 
vote on the nomination, with no intervening action or debate; that 
following the vote, the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action, and the Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
