# MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2003

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 13

```
     MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2003

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.J. Res. 13, which is at the 
desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the joint resolution by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (H.J. Res. 13) making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2003, and for other 
     purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the joint 
resolution.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the joint 
resolution be read three times, passed, and the motion to reconsider be 
laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The joint resolution (H.J. Res. 13) was read the third time and 
passed.

                          ____________________
```
