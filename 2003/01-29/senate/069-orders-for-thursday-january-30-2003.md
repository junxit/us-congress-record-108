# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 30, 2003

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 30, 2003

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 11 
a.m., Thursday, January 30. I further ask unanimous consent that 
following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and there then be a 
period for morning business until 1 p.m., with the time equally divided 
and Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
