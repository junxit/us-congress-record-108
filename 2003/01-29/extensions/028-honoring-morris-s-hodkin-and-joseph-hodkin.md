# HONORING MORRIS S. HODKIN AND JOSEPH HODKIN

```
              HONORING MORRIS S. HODKIN AND JOSEPH HODKIN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE ISRAEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 29, 2003

  Mr. ISRAEL. Mr. Speaker, I rise to acknowledge the works of Morris S. 
Hodkin and Joseph Hodkin. These outstanding individuals from Long 
Island were honored last night at the B'nai B'rith Banking and Finance 
Distinguished Achievement Award Dinner.
  Mr. Morris Hodkin and Mr. Joseph Hodkin of Daley-Hodkin Corporation 
have dedicated years of service to the corporate credit industry in New 
York. They are also to be commended for their continued activities in 
charitable endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
