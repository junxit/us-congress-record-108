# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

```
    ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. BONILLA. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 63), and I 
ask unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the resolution.
  The Clerk read as follows:

                               H. Res. 63

       Resolved, That the following Members be and are hereby 
     elected to the following standing committees of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Budget: Mr. Shays to rank after Mr. Nussle; and Ms. Ginny 
     Brown-Waite of Florida to rank after Mr. Hensarling.
       Education and the Workforce: Mr. Gingrey.
       Science: Mr. Gilchrest to rank after Mrs. Biggert; and Mr. 
     Feeney to rank after Mr. Bonner.
       Small Business: Mr. Chocola.
       Standards of Official Conduct: Mr. Hastings of Washington; 
     Mrs. Biggert; Mr. Hulshof; and Mr. LaTourette.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
