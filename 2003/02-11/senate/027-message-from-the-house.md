# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:22 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that pursuant to 
the provisions of section 201(a)(2) of the Congressional Budget and 
Impoundment Control Act of 1974, Public Law 93-344, the Speaker of the 
House of Representatives and the President pro tempore of the Senate 
appoints Mr. Douglas Holtz-Eakin as Director of the Congressional 
Budget Office for a term of office expiring on January 3, 2007.
```
