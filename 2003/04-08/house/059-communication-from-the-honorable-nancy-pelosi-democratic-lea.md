# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE NANCY PELOSI, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
    COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE NANCY PELOSI, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Burgess) laid before the House the 
following communication from Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                         House of Representatives,


                              Office of the Democratic Leader,

                                                    April 8, 2003.

     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to (40 U.S.C. 188a), I hereby 
     appoint Rep. Chaka Fattah as a member of the United States 
     Capitol Preservation Commission for the 108th Congress.
           Best Regards,
     Nancy Pelosi.
```
