# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO COORDINATING COUNCIL ON JUVENILE JUSTICE AND DELINQUENCY PREVENTION

```
REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO COORDINATING COUNCIL ON JUVENILE JUSTICE AND 
                         DELINQUENCY PREVENTION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Quinn). Pursuant to section 206 of the 
Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention Act of 1974 (42 U.S.C. 
5616), and the order of the House of January 8, 2003, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's reappointment of the following member on the 
part of the House to the Coordinating Council on Juvenile Justice and 
Delinquency Prevention for a 3-year term:
  Mr. Michael J. Mahoney of Chicago, Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
