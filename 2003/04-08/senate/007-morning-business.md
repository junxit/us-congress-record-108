# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 10:30 a.m., with time to be equally divided between the Senator 
from Texas, Mrs. Hutchison, and the Democratic leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Texas is recognized.
```
