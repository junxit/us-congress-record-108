# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gallegly, Elton (G000021) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 127, H.R. 1055, H.R. 1368

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ELTON GALLEGLY

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 8, 2003

  Mr. GALLEGLY. Mr. Speaker, on April 7, 2003, I was unable to vote on 
H.R. 1055, the ``Dr. Roswell N. Beck Post Office Building'' Designation 
Act (rollcall vote 109), H. Res. 127, Expressing the sense of the House 
that a month should be designated as ``Financial Literacy for Youth 
Month'' (rollcall 110), and H.R. 1368, ``Norman Shumway Post Office 
Building'' Designation Act (rollcall vote 111). Had I been present, I 
would have voted ``yes'' on all three measures.
```
