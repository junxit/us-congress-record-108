# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 2003

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2765

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 2003

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Friday, September 26. I further ask unanimous consent that following 
the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume 
consideration of H.R. 2765, the District of Columbia appropriations 
bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
