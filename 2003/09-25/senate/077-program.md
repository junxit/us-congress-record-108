# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow the Senate will resume consideration of the District of 
Columbia appropriations bill. The two managers will be here tomorrow, 
and Senators are encouraged to come to the floor to offer and debate 
their amendments. There will be no rollcall votes tomorrow. Any votes 
ordered during tomorrow's session will be stacked to occur on Monday at 
approximately 5:30 p.m.
```
