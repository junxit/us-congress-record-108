# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1657

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1657

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I understand that S. 1657, which was 
introduced earlier today, is at the desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1657) to amend section 44921 of title 49, United 
     States Code, to provide for arming of cargo pilots against 
     terrorism.

  Mr. DeWINE. I now ask for its second reading and object to its second 
reading on this matter.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will have its second reading on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
