# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 27, 2003

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 27, 2003

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 10 
a.m. on Thursday, March 27. I further ask that following the prayer and 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and there then be a period for morning business 
until 11 a.m., with the time equally divided between the two leaders or 
their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
