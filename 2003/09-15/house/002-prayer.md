# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord of the living and guardian of the Nation, we commend to Your 
loving attention the Members of the House of Representatives and all 
those who serve in government.
  Give to all the freedom to embrace the tasks You set before them 
today. May their standard of work ethic be a model for all citizens.
  Grant safety and health to all in their family, their colleagues, and 
all those they love.
  By working together, may the Nation be strengthened and world peace 
be realized.
  In Your Divine Providence we trust, now and forever. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
