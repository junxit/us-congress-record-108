# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 459

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII 
          WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio (during the Special Order of Mr. Cummings) from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 108-390) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 459) waiving a requirement of clause 6(a) of 
rule XIII with respect to consideration of certain resolutions reported 
from the Committee on Rules, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
