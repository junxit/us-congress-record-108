# EXPRESSING THE IMPORTANCE OF MOTORSPORTS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 320

```
                EXPRESSING THE IMPORTANCE OF MOTORSPORTS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 320, which is at the 
desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 320) expressing the 
     sense of the Congress regarding the importance of 
     motorsports.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent 
resolution be agreed to, the preamble be agreed to, the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table, and that any statements relating to 
the concurrent resolution be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 320) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
