# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 1274

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 1274

  Mr. FRIST. I understand that H.R. 1274, which was just received from 
the House, is at the desk and I now ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1274) to direct the Administrator of General 
     Services to convey to Fresno County, California, the existing 
     Federal courthouse in that county.

  Mr. FRIST. I now ask for its second reading and object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will receive its second reading on 
the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
