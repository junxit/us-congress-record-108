# MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2004

```
     MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2004

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed to the 
consideration of H.J. Res. 78, which is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (H.J. Res. 78) making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2004, and for other 
     purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. FRIST. I ask the amendment at the desk be agreed to, the joint 
resolution, as amended, be read the third time and passed, the motion 
to reconsider be laid upon the table, and any statements relating to 
the joint resolution be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The amendment (No. 2208) was agreed to, as follows:
       On page 2, line 7, strike ``23'' and insert ``24''.

  The joint resolution (H.J. Res. 78), as amended, was read the third 
time and passed.
```
