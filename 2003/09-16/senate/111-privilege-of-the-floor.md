# PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Feingold, Russell D. (F000061) [D-WI], Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 17, H.R. 2754

```
                         PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. FEINGOLD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that privilege 
of the floor be granted to Andrew Ayers, a legal intern with my 
Judiciary Committee staff, during consideration of the debate on S. 
Res. 17.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, on behalf of Senator Bingaman, I ask 
unanimous consent that Dr. Jonathan Epstein and Mr. Eric Burman, 
legislative fellows in his office, be given floor privileges during the 
pendency of H.R. 2754, and any votes thereupon.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
