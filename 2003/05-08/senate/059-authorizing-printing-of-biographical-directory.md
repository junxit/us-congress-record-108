# AUTHORIZING PRINTING OF BIOGRAPHICAL DIRECTORY

```
             AUTHORIZING PRINTING OF BIOGRAPHICAL DIRECTORY

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 138, which is at 
the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 138) authorizing the 
     printing of the Biographical Directory of the United States 
     Congress, 1774-2005.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
concurrent resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table, with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 138) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
