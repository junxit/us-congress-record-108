# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE TO TUESDAY, MAY 27, 2003

```
                              {time}  0215
     CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE TO TUESDAY, MAY 27, 2003

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Tuesday, May 27, 2003,

its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 191, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.

request of the gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
