# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Rohrabacher, Dana (R000409) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 191

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. ROHRABACHER. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 
191, 108th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Accordingly, pursuant to the previous order of the House of today, 
the House stands adjourned until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, May 27, 2003, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 191, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  Thereupon (at 2 o'clock and 17 minutes a.m., Friday, May, 23, 2003), 
pursuant to the previous order of the House of today, the House 
adjourned until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, May 27, 2003, unless it sooner has 
received a message from the Senate transmitting its adoption of House 
Concurrent Resolution 191, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.

                          ____________________
```
