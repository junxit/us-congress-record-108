# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING ERIC WAGNER

```
                 A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING ERIC WAGNER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 22, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker,
  Whereas, Eric Wagner has devoted himself to serving others through 
his membership in the Boy Scouts of America; and
  Whereas, Eric Wagner has shared his time and talent with the 
community in which he resides; and
  Whereas, Eric Wagner has demonstrated a commitment to meet challenges 
with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, Eric Wagner must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication he put forth in earning the Eagle Scout Award;
  Therefore, I join with Troop 85, the residents of Yorkville, and the 
entire 18th Congressional District in congratulating Eric Wagner as he 
receives the Eagle Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```
