# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 9, 2003

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 9, 2003

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask unanimous 
consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn 
until 9:30 a.m., Tuesday, September 9. I further ask that following the 
prayer and pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume 
consideration of H.R. 2660, the Labor, Health and Human Services and 
Education appropriations bill. I further ask consent that the Senate 
recess from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
