# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 1:03 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following concurrent resolution, without amendment:

       S. Con. Res. 63. Concurrent resolution authorizing the use 
     of the rotunda of the Capitol for the unveiling of the 
     portrait bust of Vice President Dan Quayle on September 10, 
     2003.
```
