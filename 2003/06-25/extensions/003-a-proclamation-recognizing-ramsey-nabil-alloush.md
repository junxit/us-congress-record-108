# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING RAMSEY NABIL ALLOUSH

```
            A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING RAMSEY NABIL ALLOUSH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 25, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, whereas, Ramsey Alloush graduated from John F.
```
