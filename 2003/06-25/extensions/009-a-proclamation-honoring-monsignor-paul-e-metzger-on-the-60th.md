# A PROCLAMATION HONORING MONSIGNOR PAUL E. METZGER ON THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF ORDINATION

```
     A PROCLAMATION HONORING MONSIGNOR PAUL E. METZGER ON THE 60TH 
                       ANNIVERSARY OF ORDINATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 25, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker,
  Whereas, Monsignor Paul E. Metzger celebrated the 60th anniversary of 
his ordination on April 17, 2003; and
  Whereas, Monsignor Paul E. Metzger should be commended for his 
faithfulness to the church; and
  Whereas, Monsignor Paul E. Metzger served as a pastor for the St. 
John Church in Bellaire, Ohio for 26 years; and
  Whereas, Monsignor Paul E. Metzger continues to serve the community 
of Middletown as the Director of Residential Individual Options and 
through his dedication to the Holy Family Parish;
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District in recognizing Monsignor Paul E. Metzger as he celebrates the 
60th Anniversary of Ordination.

                          ____________________
```
