# NORMAN D. SHUMWAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 NORMAN D. SHUMWAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1368) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 7554 Pacific Avenue in Stockton, California, 
as the ``Norman D. Shumway Post Office Building'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
