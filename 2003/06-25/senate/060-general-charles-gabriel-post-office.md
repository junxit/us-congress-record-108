# GENERAL CHARLES GABRIEL POST OFFICE

```
                  GENERAL CHARLES GABRIEL POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 1465) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 4832 East Highway 27 in Iron Station, North 
Carolina, as the ``General Charles Gabriel Post Office'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
