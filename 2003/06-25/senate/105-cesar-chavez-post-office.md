# CESAR CHAVEZ POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 925

```
                        CESAR CHAVEZ POST OFFICE

  This bill (H.R. 925) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1859 South Ashland Avenue in Chicago, 
Illinois, as the ``Cesar Chavez Post Office,'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
