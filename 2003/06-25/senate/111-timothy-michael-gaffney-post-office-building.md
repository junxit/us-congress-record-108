# TIMOTHY MICHAEL GAFFNEY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1596

```
              TIMOTHY MICHAEL GAFFNEY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1596) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2318 Woodson Road in St. Louis, Missouri, as 
the ``Timothy Michael Gaffney Postal Office Building'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
