# ADMIRAL DONALD DAVIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1609

```
               ADMIRAL DONALD DAVIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1609) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 201 West Boston Street in Brookfield, 
Missouri, as the ``Admiral Donald Davis Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
