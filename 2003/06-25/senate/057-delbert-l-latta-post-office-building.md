# DELBERT L. LATTA POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 DELBERT L. LATTA POST OFFICE BUILDING

  This bill (H.R. 985) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 111 West Washington Street in Bowling Green, 
Ohio, as the ``Delbert L. Latta Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
