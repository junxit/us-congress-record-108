# FLOYD SPENCE POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                   FLOYD SPENCE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  This bill (H.R. 917) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1830 South Lake Drive in Lexington, South 
Carolina, as the ``Floyd Spence Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
