# MICHAEL J. HEALY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 825

```
                 MICHAEL J. HEALY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 825) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 7401 West 100th Place in Bridgeview, 
Illinois, as the ``Michael J. Healy Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
