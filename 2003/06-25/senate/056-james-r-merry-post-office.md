# JAMES R. MERRY POST OFFICE

```
                       JAMES R. MERRY POST OFFICE

  This bill (H.R. 981) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 141 Erie Street in Linesville, Pennsylvania, 
as the ``James R. Merry Post Office'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
