# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. WARNER. For the information of all Senators, tomorrow the Senate 
will resume debate on the Department of Defense authorization bill. We 
have two amendments relating to TRICARE on the bill. I will be talking 
to the sponsors of those amendments and the ranking member, as stated 
by the distinguished minority whip.
  We are encouraging other Members to come forward and work with the 
ranking member and myself in order to schedule consideration of 
amendments. Senators should expect rollcall votes on amendments 
throughout the day tomorrow.
  I also think it could be into the night, Mr. President, because we 
are anxious to complete this bill.
```
