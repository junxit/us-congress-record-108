# NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS

```
                      NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS


           committee on agriculture, nutrition, and forestry

  Mr. COCHRAN. Mr. President, I announce that the Committee on 
Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry will conduct a business meeting on 
May 21, 2003 in SR-328A at 9:30 a.m. The purpose of this meeting will 
be to consider the nominations of Glen Klippenstein, Julia Bartling, 
and Lowell Junkins to be members of the Board of Directors of the 
Federal Agricultural Mortgage

[[Page 12103]]

Corporation and Tom Dorr to be a member of the Board of Directors of 
the Commodity Credit Corporation and to be Under Secretary of 
Agriculture for Rural Development.
```
