# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 6 p.m., with the time equally divided between 
the two leaders or their designees and with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each.

[[Page 7807]]

  Under the previous order, the first hour shall be equally divided 
between the Senator from Texas, Mrs. Hutchison, and the Senator from 
Arkansas, Mrs. Lincoln, or their designees.
  The Senator from Texas.

                          ____________________
```
