# HAPPY 80TH BIRTHDAY

```
                          HAPPY 80TH BIRTHDAY

  Mr. SCHUMER. First, I want to wish the President of the Senate a 
happy 80th birthday. I come from New York. We pride ourselves on good 
salmon. At his little gathering last night, the Alaska salmon looked 
beautiful and tasted as good as any salmon I ever tasted. I want to 
wish him a happy birthday and thank him for celebrating it with all of 
us.

                          ____________________
```
