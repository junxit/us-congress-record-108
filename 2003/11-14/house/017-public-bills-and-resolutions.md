# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 440, H.R. 3487, H.R. 3488

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions were 
introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. WALDEN of Oregon (for himself, Mr. DeFazio, Ms. 
             Hooley of Oregon, Mr. Wu, Mr. Blumenauer, and Ms. 
             Dunn):
       H.R. 3487. A bill to establish an alternative trigger for 
     determining if an extended benefit period is in effect for a 
     State for purposes of certain benefits under the Temporary 
     Extended Unemployment Compensation Act of 2002; to the 
     Committee on Ways and Means.
           By Mr. HINOJOSA (for himself, Mr. Rodriguez, Mr. Ortiz, 
             and Mr. Grijalva):
       H.R. 3488. A bill to allow certain Mexican nationals to be 
     admitted as nonimmigrant visitors for a period of 6 months; 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. ORTIZ (for himself, Mr. Hinojosa, and Mr. 
             Rodriguez):
       H. Res. 440. A resolution providing that the executive 
     branch should remove certain entry restrictions for Mexican 
     nationals, and for other purposes; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
