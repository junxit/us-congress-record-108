# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1434

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 1434

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1434

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand that S. 1434, introduced by 
Senator Lincoln earlier today, is at the desk. I ask for its first 
reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
first time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1434) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to accelerate the increase in the refundability of the child 
     tax credit, and for other purposes.

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading and object 
to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will receive its second reading on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
