# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 22, 2003

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2555

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 22, 2003

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until 9:45 a.m. 
Tuesday, July 22. I further ask that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in 
the day, and the Senate then begin a period of morning business until 
11 a.m., with the time equally divided between the two leaders or their 
designees; provided that at 11 a.m. the Senate resume consideration of 
Calendar No. 192, H.R. 2555, the Department of Homeland Security 
appropriations bill.
  I further ask consent that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 
p.m. for the weekly party lunches.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
