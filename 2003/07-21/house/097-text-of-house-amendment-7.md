# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2799

```
H.R. 2799

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill after the last 
     section (preceding the short title) insert the following new 
     title:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Department of State to prohibit any 
     organization, project, or activity from promoting the 
     participation of women in international peace efforts, 
     particularly in Africa and the Middle East.
```
