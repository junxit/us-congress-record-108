# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 2789

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2789

```
         REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 2789

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to 
remove the following cosponsors from H.R. 2789:
  The gentleman from Tennessee (Mr. Davis); the gentleman from 
Tennessee (Mr. Jenkins); the gentleman from South Carolina (Mr. Brown); 
the gentleman from Georgia (Mr. Norwood); the gentleman from Georgia 
(Mr. Collins); the gentleman from Michigan (Mr. McCotter); the 
gentleman from California (Mr. Cardoza); and the gentlewoman from North 
Carolina (Mrs. Myrick).
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from South Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
