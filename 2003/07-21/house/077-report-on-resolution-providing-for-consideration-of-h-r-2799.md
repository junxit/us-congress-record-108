**Measures:** H.Res. 326, H.R. 2799

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2799, 
DEPARTMENTS OF COMMERCE, JUSTICE AND STATE, THE JUDICIARY, AND RELATED 
                   AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2004

  Mr. LINDER (during the special order of Mr. Smith of Michigan) from 
the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 108-
226) on the resolution (H. Res. 326) providing for consideration of the 
bill (H.R. 2799) making appropriations for the Departments of Commerce, 
Justice, and State, the Judiciary, and related agencies for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2004, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
