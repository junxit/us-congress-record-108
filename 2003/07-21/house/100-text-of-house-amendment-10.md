# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Paul, Ron (P000583) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2799

```
H.R. 2799

                          Offered By: Mr. Paul

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

          limitation on united states contributions to unesco

       Sec. ____. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be made available for the United Nations Educational, 
     Scientific, and Cultural Organization (UNESCO).
```
