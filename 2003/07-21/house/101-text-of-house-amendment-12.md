# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2799

```
H.R. 2799

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 12: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to engage in negotiations the purpose of which is to 
     enter into a trade agreement with another country and in 
     which provisions on the temporary entry of professionals are 
     offered or accepted by a representative of the United States.
```
