# Text of House Amendment 3

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2799

```
H.R. 2799

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 3: At the end of the bill after the last 
     section (preceding the short title) insert the following new 
     title:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Department of State to regulate the issuance 
     of consular identification cards by foreign missions in the 
     United States.
```
