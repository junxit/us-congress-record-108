# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 21, 2003

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

**Measures:** S. 1050

```
                   ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 21, 2003

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 9:30 
a.m., Wednesday, May 21. I further ask that following the prayer and 
the pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume 
consideration of S. 1050, the Department of Defense authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
