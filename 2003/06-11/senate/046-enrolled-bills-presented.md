# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 222, S. 273

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, June 11, she had 
presented to the President of the United States the following enrolled 
bills:

       S. 222. An act to approve the settlement of the water 
     rights claims of the Zuni Indian Tribe in Apache County, 
     Arizona, and for other purposes.
       S. 273. An act to provide for the expeditious completion of 
     the acquisition of land owned by the State of Wyoming within 
     the boundaries of Grand Teton National Park, and for other 
     purposes.

                          ____________________
```
