# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 121. An act to enhance the operation of the AMBER Alert 
     communications network in order to facilitate the recovery of 
     abducted children, to provide for enhanced notification on 
     highways of alerts and information on such children, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary in addition 
     to the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure for a 
     period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each 
     case for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
       S. 141. An act to improve the calculation of the Federal 
     subsidy rate with respect to certain small business loans, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on the Budget in 
     addition to the Committee on Small Business for a period to 
     be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
```
