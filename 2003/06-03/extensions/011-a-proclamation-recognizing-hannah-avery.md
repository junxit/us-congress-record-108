# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING HANNAH AVERY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
                A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING HANNAH AVERY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 3, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, Whereas, Hannah Avery has devoted herself to 
serving others through her membership in the Girl Scouts; and
  Whereas, Hannah Avery has shared her time and talent with the 
community in which she resides; and
  Whereas, Hannah Avery has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, Hannah Avery must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication she put forth in earning the Girl Scout Gold Award;
  Therefore, I join with the Girl Scouts, the residents of Kingston and 
the entire 18th Congressional District in congratulating Hannah Avery 
as she receives the Girl Scout Gold Award.

                          ____________________
```
