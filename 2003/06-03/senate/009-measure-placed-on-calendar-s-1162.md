# MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1162

```
                  MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1162

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I understand that S. 1162 is at the desk 
and is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. DOMENICI. I ask that it be in order to read the title of the 
measure.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the title of the bill for 
the second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1162) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to accelerate the increase in the refundability of the child 
     tax credit, and for other purposes.

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask that the Senate proceed to the 
measure and object to further proceeding.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  Under rule XIV, the measure will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
