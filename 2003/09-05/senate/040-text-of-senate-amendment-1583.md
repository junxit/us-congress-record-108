# Text of Senate Amendment 1583

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
  SA 1583. Mr. FRIST (for Mr. Hatch) proposed amendment to the 
resolution S. Res. 210, expressing the sense of the Senate that 
supporting a balance between work and personal life is in the best 
interest of national worker productivity, and that the President should 
issue a proclamation designating October of 2003 as ``National Work and 
Family Month''; as follows:

       Strike all after the resolving clause and insert the 
     following:
     That--
       (1) it is the sense of the Senate that--
       (A) reducing the conflict between work and family life 
     should be a national priority; and
       (B) the month of October of 2003 should be designated as 
     ``National Work and Family Month''; and
       (2) the Senate requests that the President issue a 
     proclamation calling upon the people of the United States to 
     observe ``National Work and Family Month'' with appropriate 
     ceremonies and activities.
                                 ______
```
