# Text of Senate Amendment 1584

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
  SA 1584. Mr. FRIST (for Mr. Hatch) proposed an amendment to the 
resolution S. Res. 210, expressing the sense of the Senate that 
supporting a balance between work and personal life is in the best 
interest of national workers productivity, and that the President 
should issue a proclamation designating October of 2003 as ``National 
Work and Family Month''; as follows:

       Amend the title so as to read: ``Expressing the sense of 
     the Senate that supporting a

[[Page S11171]]

     balance between work and personal life is in the best 
     interest of national workers productivity, and that the 
     President should issue a proclamation designating October of 
     2003 as ``National Work and Family Month''.''.
                                 ______
```
