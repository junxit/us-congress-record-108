                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

             Week of September 8 through September 13, 2003

                             Senate Chamber

  On Monday, at 1 p.m., Senate will resume consideration of H.R. 2660, 
Labor/HHS/Education Appropriation Act.
