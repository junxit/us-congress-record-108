# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Green, Mark (G000545) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2859

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MARK GREEN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 4, 2003

  Mr. GREEN of Wisconsin. Mr. Speaker, after a review of the votes from 
July 25, 2003, I noticed I inadvertently voted against the Toomey 
amendment to H.R. 2859 (Rollcall No. 458). That was a mistake on my 
part due to my distraction with other legislative business during that 
particular series of votes. I wish to state for the record that I 
support the Toomey amendment and wish to be recorded as voting ``aye.''
```
