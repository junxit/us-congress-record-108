# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED & PROPOSED

**Measures:** S. 610, S. 811, H.R. 1006, H.R. 2799

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED & PROPOSED

       SA 2212. Mr. HATCH submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed by him to the bill H.R. 2799, making appropriations 
     for the Departments of Commerce, Justice, and State, the 
     Judiciary, and related agencies for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2004, and for other purposes; which was ordered 
     to lie on the table.
       SA 2213. Mr. HATCH submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed by him to the bill H.R. 2799, supra; which was 
     ordered to lie on the table.
       SA 2214. Mr. ENSIGN (for Mr. Voinovich (for himself and Mr. 
     Carper)) proposed an amendment to the bill S. 610, to amend 
     the provisions of title 5, United States Code, to provide for 
     workforce flexibilities and certain Federal personnel 
     provisions relating to the National Aeronautics and Space 
     Administration, and for other purposes.
       SA 2215. Mr. ENSIGN (for Mr. Inhofe (for himself and Mr. 
     Jeffords)) proposed an amendment to the bill H.R. 1006, to 
     amend the Lacey Act Amendments of 1981 to further the 
     conservation of certain wildlife species.
       SA 2216. Mr. ENSIGN (for Mr. Shelby) proposed an amendment 
     to the bill S. 811, to support certain housing proposals in 
     the fiscal year 2003 budget for the Federal Government, 
     including the downpayment assistance initiative under the 
     HOME Investment Partnership Act, and for other purposes.
```
