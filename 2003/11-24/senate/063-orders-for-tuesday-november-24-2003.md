# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 24, 2003

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 24, 2003

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 8:15 a.m., 
Tuesday, November 25. I further ask that following the prayer and 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of the 
conference report to accompany H.R. 1, the Medicare Prescription Drug 
Modernization Act, as provided under the previous order. I further ask 
unanimous consent that the final 10 minutes prior to the 9:15 a.m. vote 
be equally divided between the two leaders, with the majority leader in 
control of the final 5 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
