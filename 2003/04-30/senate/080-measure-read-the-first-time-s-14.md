# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 14

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 14

```
                   MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 14

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand that S. 14, introduced earlier 
today, is at the desk and I ask for its first reading.

       A bill (S. 14) to enhance the energy security of the United 
     States, and for other purposes.
```
