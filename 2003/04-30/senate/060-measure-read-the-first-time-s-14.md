# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 14

```
                   MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 14

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand that S. 14, introduced earlier 
today, is at the desk and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the first 
time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 14) to enhance the energy security of the United 
     States, and for other purposes.

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading and object 
to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will remain at 
the desk.
```
