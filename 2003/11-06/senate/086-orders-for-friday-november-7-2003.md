# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 2003

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 150

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 2003

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Friday, November 7; I further ask that following the prayer and pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of S. 
150, the Internet tax moratorium bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
