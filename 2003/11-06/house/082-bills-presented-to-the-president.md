# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 2691, H.R. 3288, H.R. 3289

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on November 5, 2003 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.R. 2691. Making appropriations for the Department of the 
     Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2004, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 3288. To amend title XXI of the Social Security Act to 
     make technical corrections with respect to the definitions of 
     qualifying State.
       H.R. 3289. Making emergency supplemental appropriations for 
     defense and for the reconstruction of Iraq and Afghanistan 
     for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2004, and for other 
     purposes.

                          ____________________
```
