                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. SCOTT McINNIS

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 9, 2003

  Mr. McINNIS. Mr. Speaker, due to a family medical issue, I missed 
several votes on June

26, 2003. I was not able to be present to vote on H.R. 1, the Medicare 
Prescription Drug and Modernization Act of 2003. Had I been present, I 
would have voted yes on passage of H.R. 1, Rollcall No. 332. In 
addition, had I been present, I would have voted yes on passage of the 
Military Construction Appropriations Act for Fiscal Year 2004, Rollcall 
No. 325; yes on H. Res. 277, Expressing Support for Freedom in Hong 
Kong; yes on passage of the Health Savings and Affordability Act, 
Rollcall No. 328; and yes on the Intelligence Authorization Act for 
Fiscal Year 2004, Rollcall No. 333.
