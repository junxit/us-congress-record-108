# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, ADM Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Lord God almighty, You have made all the people of this Earth for 
Your glory. Yet, too often we choose our own destructive paths. Deliver 
our own world from hatred, cruelty, and revenge. Save us from violence, 
discord, confusion, and sin. Guide and bless our Senators that their 
labors will please You and be a blessing to the nations of the Earth. 
May we be a people at peace among ourselves and determined to be Your 
instruments of reconciliation. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
