# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Lugar, Richard G. (L000504) [R-IN], Feingold, Russell D. (F000061) [D-WI], Biden, Joseph R., Jr. (B000444) [D-DE], Lincoln, Blanche L. (L000035) [D-AR]

**Measures:** S. 925

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. LUGAR. I ask unanimous consent the following persons and fellows 
detailed to the Foreign Relations Committee be granted the privilege of 
the floor during the consideration of S. 925: Paul Foldi, Michael 
Mattler, Jason Hamm, and Peter Gadzinski.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. FEINGOLD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Nicolaas 
Corneliss, a fellow on my staff, be granted privileges of the floor for 
the duration of the Congress.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BIDEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Perry Cammack, 
a Javits fellow working on the staff of the Foreign Relations 
Committee, be granted the privilege of the floor during consideration 
of S. 925.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. LINCOLN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
privilege of the floor be granted to Matt Linstroth and Jason Wolf 
during consideration of the Child Tax Credit legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
