                Announcement by the Speaker Pro Tempore

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson) (during the vote). Members are 
advised that 2 minutes remain in this vote.

                              {time}  1533

  So (two-thirds having voted in favor thereof) the rules were 
suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title of the bill was amended so as to read: ``A bill to amend 
title 10, United States Code, to provide permanent authority for the 
exemption for certain members of the uniformed services from an 
otherwise-applicable requirement for the payment of subsistence charges 
while hospitalized.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
  Stated for:
  Ms. SOLIS. Mr. Speaker, during rollcall vote No. 537 on H.R. 2298, on 
exempting members of the Armed Forces from subsistence charges while 
hospitalized, I was unavoidably detained. Had I been present, I would 
have voted ``yea.''
