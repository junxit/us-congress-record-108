# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 355, H.Res. 372, H.R. 2297, H.R. 2998, H.R. 3108

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on questions previously postponed and on the questions 
postponed yesterday.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 3108, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 2297, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 2998, by the yeas and nays;
  House Resolution 355, by the yeas and nays; and
  House Resolution 372, by the yeas and nays.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. The 
remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.
```
