# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House, reports that on June 20, 2003 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills:

       H.R. 389. To authorize the use of certain grant funds to 
     establish an information clearinghouse that provides 
     information to increase public access to defibrilation in 
     schools.
       H.R. 519. To authorize the Secretary of Interior to conduct 
     a study of the San Gabriel River Watershed, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 788. To revise the boundary of the Glen Canyon 
     National Recreation Area in the States of Utah and Arizona.
```
