# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2555, DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2004

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2555, 
        DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2004

  Mr. LINCOLN DIAZ-BALART of Florida (during Special Order of Mr. 
Gingrey), from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 108-175) on the resolution (H. Res. 293) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2555) making appropriations for the 
Department of Homeland Security for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 2004, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
