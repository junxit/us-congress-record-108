# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Engel, Eliot L. (E000179) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2555

```
H.R. 2555

                         Offered By: Mr. Engel

       Amendment No. 5: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to issue a certification of offsite emergency 
     evacuation plans of nuclear power plants.
```
