# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 177, H.Con.Res. 209, H.Res. 264, H.R. 1460, H.R. 2465

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on four motions to suspend the rules previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H. Res. 264, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Res. 177, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Con. Res. 209, by the yeas and nays; and
  H.R. 2465, by the yeas and nays.
  Proceedings on H.R. 1460 will resume tomorrow.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.
```
