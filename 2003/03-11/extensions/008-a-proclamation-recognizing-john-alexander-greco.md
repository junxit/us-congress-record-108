# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING JOHN ALEXANDER GRECO

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
            A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING JOHN ALEXANDER GRECO

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 11, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, Whereas, John Alexander Greco has devoted 
himself to serving others through his membership in the Boy Scouts of 
America; and
  Whereas, John Alexander Greco has shared his time and talent with the 
community in which he resides; and
  Whereas, John Alexander Greco has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, John Alexander Greco must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication he put forth in earning the Eagle Scout Award;
  Therefore, I join with Troop 150, the residents of Minerva, and the 
entire 18th Congressional District in congratulating John Alexander 
Greco as he receives the Eagle Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```
