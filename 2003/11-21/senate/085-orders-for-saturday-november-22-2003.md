# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 22, 2003

```
                 ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 22, 2003

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Saturday, November 22. I further ask unanimous consent that following 
the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then begin 
consideration of the conference report to accompany H.R. 1, the 
Medicare Prescription Drug Modernization Act, as provided under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
