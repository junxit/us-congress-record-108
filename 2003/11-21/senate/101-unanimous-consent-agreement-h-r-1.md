# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 1

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 1

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the contingent 
upon its availability, the Senate proceed to the consideration of the 
conference report to accompany H.R. 1, the Medicare Prescription Drug 
Modernization Act, at 10 a.m. tomorrow; provided that for duration of 
tomorrow's session, consideration of the conference report be for 
debate only, and the speakers be recognized in an alternating fashion.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
