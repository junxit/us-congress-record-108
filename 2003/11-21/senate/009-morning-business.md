# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there now be 
a period for morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Who seeks recognition?
  Mr. CHAMBLISS. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REED. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum call 
be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
