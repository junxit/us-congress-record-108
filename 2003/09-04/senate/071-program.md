# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. Madam President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow the Senate will resume debate on the Labor-HHS-Education 
appropriations bill. At approximately 9:30 a.m., the Senate will begin 
two back-to-back rollcall votes. The first vote will be in relation to 
the Clinton amendment on bioterrorism workforce, which will be 
immediately followed by a vote in relation to the Harkin amendment on 
school renovation. Following those votes, Members are encouraged to 
remain in the Chamber to offer and debate their amendments. The 
chairman and ranking member will be here to accommodate those Members 
who are available to offer amendments tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```
