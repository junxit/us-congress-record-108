# PRESIDENT OF THE ARIZONA FARM BUREAU RETIRES

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
              PRESIDENT OF THE ARIZONA FARM BUREAU RETIRES

● Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I would like to say a few words to my 
colleagues about a respected Arizonan, Ken Evans, who is stepping down 
as president of the Arizona Farm Bureau on November 1.
  Ken has been a community leader, farmer, rancher, businessman, and 
agriculturist in my state of Arizona for many decades. As president of 
the Farm Bureau for the last 11 years, he has been an outstanding 
spokesman for the agricultural community. He has helped both the 
agricultural producers and the consumers of Arizona with his energetic 
leadership. His aid and advice were invaluable to me and other 
policymakers, both in the state and here in Washington.
  I express my thanks for his service and wish him success in all of 
his future endeavors.●

                          ____________________
```
