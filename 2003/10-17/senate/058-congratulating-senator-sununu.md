# CONGRATULATING SENATOR SUNUNU

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                     CONGRATULATING SENATOR SUNUNU

  Mr. FRIST. Last, Mr. President, I congratulate the current occupant 
of the chair, the junior Senator from New Hampshire. Just a short time 
ago, about an hour and a half ago, at approximately 6 p.m., he reached 
his 100th hour of presiding time. That wasn't all just tonight, but I 
know he may feel like it at this time, 7:30 in the evening--100 hours 
during this first session of the 108th Congress. Everybody in this body 
owes him a debt of gratitude for his service to this institution as he 
presides over us just about every Friday, I believe, with a firm but a 
very fair gavel.

                          ____________________
```
