# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1805 and S. 1806

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 1805, S. 1806

```
          MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1805 and S. 1806

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand there are two bills at the 
desk that are due a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. That is correct. The clerk will report the 
titles of the bills.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1805) to prohibit civil liability actions from 
     being brought or continued against manufacturers, 
     distributors, dealers, or importers of firearms or ammunition 
     for damages resulting from the misuse of their products by 
     others.
       A bill (S. 1806) to prohibit civil liability actions from 
     being brought or continued against manufacturers, 
     distributors, dealers, or importers of firearms or ammunition 
     for damages resulting from the misuse of their products by 
     others.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I object en bloc to further proceedings on these 
measures at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the rule, the bills will be placed on 
the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
