# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 21, S. 1785

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. GRASSLEY (for himself, Mr. Harkin, Mr. Nelson of 
             Florida, Mr. Cochran, and Mr. Lott):
       S. 1785. A bill to authorize the operation of National 
     Guard counterdrug schools; to the Committee on Armed 
     Services.
           By Mr. KYL:
       S.J. Res. 21. A joint resolution expressing the sense of 
     Congress that the number of years during which the death tax 
     under subtitle B of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is 
     repealed should be extended, pending the permanent repeal of 
     the death tax; read the first time.
```
