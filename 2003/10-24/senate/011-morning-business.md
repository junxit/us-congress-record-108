# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for morning business until the hour of 10 a.m. with the 
time equally divided between the Senator from Texas, Mrs. Hutchison, or 
her designee, and the Democratic leader or his designee.
```
