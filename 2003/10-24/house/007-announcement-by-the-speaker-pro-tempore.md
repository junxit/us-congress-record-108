# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, the Speaker 
signed the following enrolled bills on Wednesday, October 22, 2003:
  S. 3, to prohibit the procedure commonly known as partial-birth 
abortion;
  S. 1591, to redesignate the facility of the United States Postal 
Service located at 48 South Broadway, Nyack, New York, as the ``Edward 
O'Grady, Waverly Brown, Peter Paige Post Office Building.''
```
