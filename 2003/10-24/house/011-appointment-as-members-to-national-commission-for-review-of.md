# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR REVIEW OF RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS OF UNITED STATES INTELLIGENCE COMMUNITY

```
[[Page H9816]]
 APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR REVIEW OF RESEARCH 
    AND DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS OF UNITED STATES INTELLIGENCE COMMUNITY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 1002(b) of the 
Intelligence Authorization Act for fiscal year 2003 (50 U.S.C. 401 
note), and the order of the House of January 8, 2003, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following Member of the 
House to the National Commission for the Review of the Research and 
Development Programs of the United States Intelligence Community:
  Mr. Hoekstra of Michigan;
  And from private life:
  Mr. K. Stuart Shea, Chantilly, Virginia;
  Mr. Gardner G. Peckham, Chevy Chase, Maryland.
```
