# APPOINTMENT AS DIRECTOR OF OFFICE OF INTERPARLIAMENTARY AFFAIRS

```
    APPOINTMENT AS DIRECTOR OF OFFICE OF INTERPARLIAMENTARY AFFAIRS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 103(c) of Public Law 
108-83 (2 U.S.C. 130-2), and the order of the House of January 8, 2003, 
the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of Ms. Martha C. Morrison 
as Director of the Office of Interparliamentary Affairs of the United 
States House of Representatives.
```
