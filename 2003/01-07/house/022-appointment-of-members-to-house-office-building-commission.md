# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO HOUSE OFFICE BUILDING COMMISSION

```
       APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO HOUSE OFFICE BUILDING COMMISSION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to 40 United 
States Code, 175 and 176, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment 
of the gentleman from Texas (Mr. DeLay) and the gentlewoman from 
California (Ms. Pelosi) as members of the House Office Building 
Commission to serve with himself.
  There was no objection.
```
