# DAILY HOUR OF MEETING

```
                         DAILY HOUR OF MEETING

  Mr. DREIER. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 9) 
and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 9

       Resolved, That unless otherwise ordered, before Monday, May 
     19, 2003, the hour of daily meeting of the House shall be 2 
     p.m. on Mondays; noon on Tuesdays; and 10 a.m. on all other 
     days of the week; and from Monday, May 19, 2003, until the 
     end of the first session, the hour of daily meeting of the 
     House shall be noon on Mondays; 10 a.m. on Tuesdays, 
     Wednesdays, and Thursdays; and 9 a.m. on all others days of 
     the week.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
