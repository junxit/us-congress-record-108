# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent there now be a 
period of morning business for up to 2 hours, equally divided in the 
usual form, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection? Without objection, it 
is so ordered.
```
