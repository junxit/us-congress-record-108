# ELECTING EMILY J. REYNOLDS OF TENNESSEE AS SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

```
   ELECTING EMILY J. REYNOLDS OF TENNESSEE AS SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. FRIST. I send a resolution to the desk and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the resolution by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 7) electing Emily J. Reynolds of 
     Tennessee as Secretary of the Senate.

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, the resolution is 
agreed to.
  The resolution (S. Res. 7) reads as follows:

                               S. Res. 7

       Resolved, That Emily J. Reynolds of Tennessee be, and she 
     is hereby, elected Secretary of the Senate.

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table and that any statements relating to this appointment be 
printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
