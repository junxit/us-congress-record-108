# NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S.Res. 5

```
 NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT 
                       PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I send a resolution to the desk and ask for 
its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the resolution by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:
  A resolution (S. Res. 5) notifying the House of Representatives of 
the election of a President pro tempore of the U.S. Senate.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, the resolution is 
agreed to.
  The resolution (S. Res 5) reads as follows:

                               S. Res. 5

       Resolved, That the House of Representatives be notified of 
     the election of Ted Stevens, a Senator from the State of 
     Alaska, as President pro tempore.


[[Page S7]]


  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
