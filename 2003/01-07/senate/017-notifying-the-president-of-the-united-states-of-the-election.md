# NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF A SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S.Res. 8

```
   NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF A 
                        SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. FRIST. I send a resolution to the desk and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the resolution by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 8) notifying the President of the 
     United States of the election of a Secretary of the Senate.

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, the resolution is 
agreed to.
  The resolution (S. Res. 8) reads as follows:

                               S. Res. 8

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of the Honorable Emily J. Reynolds 
     of Tennessee as Secretary of the Senate.

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
