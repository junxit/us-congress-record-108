# APPOINTMENTS MADE DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
                  APPOINTMENTS MADE DURING ADJOURNMENT

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Chair announces the following

Democratic leader, the appointment of Jonathan M. Jacobson of New York
```
