# ADMINISTRATION OF OATH TO SENATOR TED STEVENS AS PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE FOR THE 108TH CONGRESS

```
ADMINISTRATION OF OATH TO SENATOR TED STEVENS AS PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE 
                  OF THE SENATE FOR THE 108TH CONGRESS

  The VICE PRESIDENT. The President pro tempore-elect will be escorted 
to the desk for the oath of office by the President pro tempore-elect, 
the Senator from West Virginia (Mr. Byrd).
  The President pro tempore-elect, escorted by Senator Byrd, advanced 
to the desk of the Vice President, the oath was administered to him by 
the Vice President, and he subscribed to the oath in the Official Oath 
Book.
  [Applause, Senators rising.]
  [The President pro tempore assumed the chair.]

                          ____________________
```
