                                 Senate

  The seventh day of January being the day prescribed by Senate Joint 
Resolution 53 for the meeting of the 1st session of the 108th Congress, 
the Senate assembled in its Chamber at the Capitol and at 12 noon was 
called to order by the Vice President (Mr. Cheney).
