# ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 1, MEDICARE PRESCRIPTION DRUG AND MODERNIZATION ACT OF 2003

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 
      1, MEDICARE PRESCRIPTION DRUG AND MODERNIZATION ACT OF 2003

  Mr. BROWN of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, subject to rule XXII, clause 7, I 
hereby announce my intention to offer a motion to instruct on H.R. 1, 
Medicare Prescription Drug and Modernization Act of 2003.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       Mr. Brown of Ohio moves that the managers on the part of 
     the House at the conference on the disagreeing votes of the 
     two Houses on the Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 1 be 
     instructed to reject the provisions of subtitle C of title II 
     of the House bill.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Notice will appear in the Record.

                          ____________________
```
