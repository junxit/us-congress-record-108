# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed bills and a concurrent resolution 
of the following titles in which the concurrence of the House is 
requested:

       S. 162. An act to provide for the use and distribution of 
     certain funds awarded to the Gila River Pima-Maricopa Indian 
     Community, and for other purposes.
       S. 222. An act to approve the settlement of the water 
     rights claims of the Zuni Indian Tribe in Apache County, 
     Arizona, and for other purposes.
       S. Con Res. 20. Concurrent resolution permitting the 
     Chairman of the Committee on Rules and Administration of the 
     Senate to designate another member of the Committee to serve 
     on the Joint Committee on Printing in place of the Chairman.

                          ____________________
```
