# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 23

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, there will be a period for morning 
business until 2 p.m. At that time, the Senate will begin consideration 
of S. Con. Res. 23, the budget resolution. By statute, there will be 50 
hours of debate. So Senators should expect late nights and numerous 
votes throughout this week.
  As previously announced, there will be no votes today. However, it is 
the leader's intention to complete action on this matter of the budget 
this week.
  There will be a cloture vote on Mr. Estrada's nomination tomorrow. 
Senators will be notified as to the precise time of that vote as soon 
as the majority leader and minority leader have consulted.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Cornyn). The Senator from Iowa.

                          ____________________
```
