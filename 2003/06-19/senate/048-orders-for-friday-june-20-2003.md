# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 20, 2003

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 20, 2003

  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent when the Senate 
completes its business today it stand in adjournment until 9 a.m., 
Friday, June 20. I further ask that following the prayer and pledge, 
the

[[Page 15587]]

morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of Calendar No. 
140, S. 504, the American History and Civics Act of 2003, as provided 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
