# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 703

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  At 2:59 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 703. An act to designate the regional headquarters 
     building for the National Park Service under construction in 
     Omaha, Nebraska, as the ``Carl T. Curtis National Park 
     Service Midwest Regional Headquarters Building''.
```
