# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Becerra, Xavier (B000287) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 54, H.Res. 106, H.Res. 111

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. XAVIER BECERRA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 5, 2003

  Mr. BECERRA. Mr. Speaker, on Tuesday, March 4, 2003, I was unable to 
cast my floor vote on rollcall numbers 40, 41 and 42. The votes I 
missed include rollcall vote 40 on Suspending the Rules and Agreeing to 
H. Res. 106, Congratulating Lutheran Schools; rollcall vote 41 on 
Suspending the Rules and Agreeing to H. Con. Res. 54, Expressing 
support of National Visiting Nurse Association Week; and rollcall vote 
42 on Suspending the Rules and Agreeing to H. Res. 111, Honoring the 
Legacy of Fred Rogers.
  Had I been present for the votes, I would have voted ``aye'' on 
rollcall votes 40, 41 and 42.

                          ____________________
```
