# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON STANDARDS OF OFFICIAL CONDUCT

**Speaking:** Clyburn, James E. (C000537) [D-SC]

**Measures:** H.Res. 23

```
    ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON STANDARDS OF OFFICIAL CONDUCT

  Mr. CLYBURN. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Democratic Caucus, I 
offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 23) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 23

       Resolved, That the following Member be, and is hereby, 
     elected to the Committee on Standards of Official Conduct:
       Mr. Berman of California.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  The motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
