appeal of the ruling of the chair; ``aye'' on rollcall No. 11 on the 
motion to instruct offered by the gentleman from Wisconsin, Mr. Obey; 
and ``yea'' on rollcall No. 12 on passage of H. Res. 10, a resolution 
congratulating the Ohio State University Buckeyes football team.
