# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, tomorrow morning we will resume debate 
on the Medicare modernization conference report. Under the previous 
order, there will be approximately 3\1/2\ hours of debate prior to the 
cloture vote on the conference report which is locked in to occur at 
12:30 p.m. The cloture vote on the conference report will be the first 
vote of the day. It is my hope and expectation that cloture will be 
successful. This issue deserves an up-or-down vote. I urge my 
colleagues on the other side of the aisle to allow this process to move 
forward.
```
