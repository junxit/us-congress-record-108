# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gutierrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. LUIS V. GUTIERREZ

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 21, 2003

  Mr. GUTIERREZ. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably absent from this 
chamber on September 3, 2003. I would like the record to show that, had 
I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall votes 460, 461
```
