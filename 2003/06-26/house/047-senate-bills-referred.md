# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 163, S. 498, S. 867

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 163. An act to reauthorize the United States Institute 
     for Environmental Conflict Resolution, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Education and the Work Force 
     and the Committee on Resources.
       S. 498. An act to authorize the President to posthumously 
     award a gold medal on behalf of Congress to Joseph A. De 
     Laine, in recognition of his contributions to the Nation; to 
     the Committee on Financial Services.
       S. 867. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 710 Wicks Lane in Billings, 
     Montana, as the ``Ronald Reagan Post Office Building''; to 
     the Committee on Government Reform.

                          ____________________
```
