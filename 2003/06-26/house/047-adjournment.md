# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 231, 
I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the previous order of the House 
of today, the House stands adjourned until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, July 1, 
2003, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 231, in which 
case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.
  Thereupon (at 2 o'clock and 47 minutes a.m. Friday, June 27, 2003, 
legislative day of June 26, 2003), pursuant to the previous order of 
the House of today, the House adjourned until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, July 
1, 2003, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 231.

                          ____________________
```
