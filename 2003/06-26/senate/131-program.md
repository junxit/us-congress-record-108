# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Tomorrow, the Senate will be in a period for morning 
business. Members will be able to pay tribute to our departed friend 
and colleague Strom Thurmond. We will give Members an opportunity to 
submit statements for the Record so they can be compiled for a printed 
tribute to Senator Thurmond. There will be no rollcall votes tomorrow.
  Again, I thank my colleagues for their hard work over the past 
several weeks. We will have more to say about recent accomplishments of 
the Senate tomorrow and the events which culminated in tonight's 
passage--or this morning's passage--of the historic prescription drug 
benefits bill.

                          ____________________
```
