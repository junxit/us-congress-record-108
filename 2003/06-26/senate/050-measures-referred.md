# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 49, H.Con.Res. 49, H.R. 1511

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bill and joint resolution were read the first and the 
second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1511. An act to award a congressional gold medal to 
     Prime Minister Tony Blair; to the Committee on Banking, 
     Housing, and Urban Affairs.
       H.J. Res. 49. Joint resolution recognizing the important 
     service to the Nation provided by the Foreign Agricultural 
     Service of the Department of Agriculture on the occasion of 
     its 50th anniversary; to the Committee on Agriculture, 
     Nutrition, and Forestry.

  The following concurrent resolution was read, and referred as 
indicated:

       H. Con. Res. 49. Concurrent resolution expressing the sense 
     of the Congress that the sharp escalation of anti-Semitic 
     violence within many participating States of the Organization 
     for Security and Cooperation in Europe (OSCE) is of profound 
     concern and efforts should be undertaken to prevent future 
     occurrences; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.

                          ____________________
```
