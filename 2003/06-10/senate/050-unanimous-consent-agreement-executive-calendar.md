# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask 
unanimous consent that at 11 o'clock a.m., on Wednesday, June 11, the 
Senate proceed to executive session for the consideration of Calendar 
No. 220, the nomination of Richard Wesley, to be United States Circuit 
Judge for the Second Circuit; provided further that there then be 15 
minutes for debate equally divided between the chairman and ranking 
member prior to a vote on the confirmation of the nomination, with no 
intervening action or debate. I further ask consent that following the 
vote, the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, and 
the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask the Senator to modify his request to 
allow the chairman and ranking member, or their designees, to control 
the time. I also say this: If he accepts that modification, this will 
be the 129th judge we will have approved during the tenure of President 
Bush, and this will be the 36th circuit judge.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I so modify my unanimous consent 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to the modified request?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
