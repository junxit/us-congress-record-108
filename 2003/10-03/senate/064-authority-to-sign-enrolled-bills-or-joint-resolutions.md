# AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
         AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that during the adjournment period 
of the Senate, the majority leader be authorized to sign duly enrolled 
bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
