# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      committee on armed services

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Armed Services be authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Friday, October 3, 2003, at 9:30 a.m., in closed session, 
to receive a briefing on the interim report on Iraq's weapons of mass 
destruction programs.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
