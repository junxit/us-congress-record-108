# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 10 a.m., with the time equally divided between the Senator from 
Texas, Mrs. Hutchison, and the Democrat leader, or their designee.
  The Senator from Colorado is recognized.
```
