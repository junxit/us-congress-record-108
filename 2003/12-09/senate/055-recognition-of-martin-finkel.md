# RECOGNITION OF MARTIN FINKEL

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```
                      RECOGNITION OF MARTIN FINKEL

● Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I wish to recognize Martin Finkel, 
a distinguished doctor and family friend. Dr. Finkel has practiced 
medicine for over 30 years on the Upper West Side of Manhattan.
  Martin Finkel, M.D., F.A.C.P., P.C., a Diplomat of the American Board 
of Internal Medicine and Gastroenterology, was voted for inclusion in 
the October edition of the prestigious ``Guide to America's Top 
Physicians.''
  In designating this distinction, the editors of the Guide noted that 
Dr.

[[Page S16117]]

Finkel was ``among the select few that have earned this prestigious 
recognition.'' I join them today in their salute to Dr. Martin 
Finkel.●

                          ____________________
```
