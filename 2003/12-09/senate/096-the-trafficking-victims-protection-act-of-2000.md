# THE TRAFFICKING VICTIMS PROTECTION ACT OF 2000

```
             THE TRAFFICKING VICTIMS PROTECTION ACT OF 2000

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 2620, which is at the 
desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will state the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2620) to authorize appropriations for fiscal 
     years 2004 and 2005 for the Trafficking Victims Protection 
     Act of 2000, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
read the third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon 
the table, and that any statements related to the bill be printed in 
the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 2620) was read the third time and passed.
```
