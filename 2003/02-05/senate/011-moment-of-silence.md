# MOMENT OF SILENCE

```
                           MOMENT OF SILENCE

  Mr. FRIST. Madam President, in response to the resolution, I ask 
unanimous consent that we have a moment of silence, here and in the 
Galleries as well, out of respect for the astronauts, their families, 
and the much larger NASA community who are mourning as we speak. A 
moment of silence, please.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  (The Senate observed a moment of silence.)
  Mr. FRIST. Thank you.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader.

                          ____________________
```
