# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. Res. 18

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 18

```
               UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. Res. 18

  Mr. HATCH. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate receives H.J. 
Res. 18, the continuing resolution, if it is the same text as the 
resolution I send to the desk, it be read the third time and passed, 
that the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and any 
statements relating to it be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
