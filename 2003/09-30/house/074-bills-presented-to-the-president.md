# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

       Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on September 
     29, 2003 he presented to the President of the United States, 
     for his approval, the following bills.

       H.J. Res. 69. Making continuing appropriations for the 
     fiscal year 2004, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 2657. Making appropriations for the Legislative Branch 
     for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2004, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 2658. Making appropriations for the Department of 
     Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2004, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.R. 3161. To ratify the authority of the Federal Trade 
     Commission to establish a do-not-call registry.
       H.R. 3087. To provide an extension of highway, highway 
     safety, motor carrier safety, transit, and other programs 
     funded out of the Highway Trust Fund pending enactment of a 
     law reauthorizing the Transportation Equity Act for the 21st 
     Century.

                          ____________________
```
