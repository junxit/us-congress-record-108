# RECESS

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH], Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
having arrived, the Senate stands in recess until 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:30 p.m., recessed until 2:16 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. 
Voinovich).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Ohio.
  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
