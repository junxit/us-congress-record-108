# A PROCLAMATION HONORING CLARENCE R. SMITH, JR.

```
             A PROCLAMATION HONORING CLARENCE R. SMITH, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 10, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, Whereas, Clarence R. Smith, Jr. was born on 
July 10, 1928 and is celebrating his 75th birthday; and
  Whereas, Clarence R. Smith, Jr. is active in the Youngstown community 
with his work at the Diamond Steel Construction Company and through his 
involvement in the Boy Scouts of America, the Elks Club, the Rotary 
Club, and the United Methodist Church; and
  Whereas, Clarence R. Smith, Jr. must be commended for his years of 
dedicated service to his friends and his community; and
  Whereas, Clarence R. Smith, Jr. is a devoted husband, father, and 
grandfather;
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District in congratulating Clarence R. Smith, Jr. as he celebrates his 
75th Birthday.

                          ____________________
```
