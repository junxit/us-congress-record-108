# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate be 
in a period for morning business until 2 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DeWINE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Dole). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
