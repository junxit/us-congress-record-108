# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 2657

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate agrees to the report of the committee of 
conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment 
of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 2657) entitled ``An Act making 
appropriations for the Legislative Branch for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2004, and for other purposes.''.
```
