# PERIODIC REPORT ON NATIONAL EMERGENCY WITH RESPECT TO IRAQ--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
  PERIODIC REPORT ON NATIONAL EMERGENCY WITH RESPECT TO IRAQ--MESSAGE 
                FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on International Relations and ordered to be printed:

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 401(c) of the National Emergencies Act, 50 
U.S.C. 1641(c), and section 204(c) of the International Emergency 
Economic Powers Act, 50 U.S.C. 1703(c), I am providing a 6-month 
periodic report prepared by my Administration on the national emergency 
with respect to Iraq that was declared in Executive Order 12722 of 
August 2, 1990.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, January 29, 2003.

                          ____________________
```
