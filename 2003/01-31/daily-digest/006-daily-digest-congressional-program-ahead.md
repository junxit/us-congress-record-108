# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** H.R. 395

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of February 3 through February 7, 2003

  For the House Congressional Program Ahead, see Daily Digest of 
Thursday, January 30, 2003.
  The following House committee meetings for the above week were 
updated as of January 31, 2003:

  Committee on Armed Services, February 5, to meet for 
organizational purposes and to consider an Oversight Plan for the 
108th Congress, 12:30 p.m., and to hold a hearing on the Fiscal Year 
2004 Defense Budget, 2 p.m., 2118 Rayburn.
  Committee on the Budget, February 4, to meet for organizational 
purposes, and to consider an Oversight Plan for the 108th Congress; 
followed by a hearing on the President's Budget for Fiscal Year 
2004, 10 a.m., 210 Cannon.
  February 5, hearing on the Department of the Treasury Budget 
Priorities Fiscal Year 2004, 10 a.m., 210 Cannon.
  Committee on Education and the Workforce, February 5, to meet for 
organizational purposes, and to consider an Oversight Plan for the 
108th Congress, 4:30 p.m., 2175 Rayburn.
  Committee on Energy and Commerce, February 5, Subcommittee on 
Telecommunications and the Internet, hearing entitled ``Health of 
the Telecommunications Sector: A Perspective from Investors and 
Economists,'' 1 p.m., 2123 Rayburn.
  February 6, full Committee, hearing entitled ``Medicaid Today: The 
States' Perspective,'' 10 a.m., 2123 Rayburn.
  Committee on Financial Services: February 6, to meet for 
organizational purposes, and to consider an Oversight Plan for the 
108th Congress, 11 a.m., 2128 Rayburn.
  Committee on House Administration, February 5, to meet for 
organizational purposes, and to consider an Oversight Plan for the 
108th Congress, 4:30 p.m., 1310 Longworth.
  Committee on International Relations, February 6, to meet for 
organizational purposes, and to consider an Oversight Plan for the 
108th Congress, 11 a.m., 2172 Rayburn.
  Committee on the Judiciary, February 6, to meet for organizational 
purposes, 10 a.m., 2141 Rayburn.
  Committee on Rules, February 4, to consider the following: a 
resolution making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 
2003; and H.R. 395, Do-Not-Call Implementation Act; 6:30 p.m., H-313 
Capitol.
  Committee on Veterans' Affairs, February 5, hearing on the State 
of Veterans' Employment, 2 p.m., 334 Cannon.
  Committee on Ways and Means, February 4, 5 and 6, hearings on the 
President's Fiscal Year 2004 Budget proposals, 2 p.m., on February 4 
and 5, and 9:30 a.m., on February 6, 1100 Longworth.
  Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence, February 5, executive, 
to meet for organizational purposes, 2:30 p.m., H-405 Capitol.
```
