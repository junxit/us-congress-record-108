# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1528, TAXPAYER PROTECTION AND IRS ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2003

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1528, TAXPAYER 
             PROTECTION AND IRS ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2003

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 108-158) on the resolution (H. Res. 282) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1528) to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to protect taxpayers and ensure 
accountability of the Internal Revenue Service, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
