# Text of House Amendment 15

**Speaking:** Walden, Greg (W000791) [R-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 2691

```
H.R. 2691

                    Offered By: Mr. Walden of Oregon

       Amendment No. 15: At the end of the bill (before the short
```
