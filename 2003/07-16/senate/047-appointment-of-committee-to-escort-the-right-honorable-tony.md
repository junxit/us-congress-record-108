# APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT THE RIGHT HONORABLE TONY BLAIR, PRIME MINISTER OF THE UNITED KINGDOM OF GREAT BRITAIN AND NORTHERN IRELAND

```
  APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT THE RIGHT HONORABLE TONY BLAIR, 
  PRIME MINISTER OF THE UNITED KINGDOM OF GREAT BRITAIN AND NORTHERN 
                                IRELAND

  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
President of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the 
part of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the 
House of Representatives to escort the Right Honorable Tony Blair, 
Member of Parliament, Prime Minister of the United Kingdom of Great 
Britain and Northern Ireland, into the House Chamber for the joint 
meeting tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


  

                          ____________________
```
