# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until the 
hour of 10 a.m., with the first 15 minutes under the control of the 
majority leader or his designee, and the next 15 minutes under the 
control of Senator Mikulski or her designee.
```
