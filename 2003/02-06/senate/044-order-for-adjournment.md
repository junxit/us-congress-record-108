# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, if there is no further business, I ask 
unanimous consent that the Senate resume executive session, and that 
following the remarks of the assistant Democratic leader for 15 minutes 
and the Senator from Delaware for up to 15 minutes, the Senate then 
stand in adjournment under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
