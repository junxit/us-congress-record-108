# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I have 
some information for Senators. The Senate will resume debate on the 
nomination of Miguel Estrada this morning. We had a productive debate 
on the Estrada nomination on yesterday afternoon, and it is the 
majority leader's objective to arrive at an agreement with the other 
side of the aisle regarding the consideration and vote on the 
nomination in the near future.
  As previously announced, there will be no rollcall votes today. It is 
anticipated that the Senate will adjourn around noon. Therefore, 
Senators who wish to speak on the Estrada nomination are encouraged to 
make arrangements to do so earlier in the day.
```
