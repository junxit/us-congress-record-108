# SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

 SENATE RESOLUTION 238--AUTHORIZING REGULATIONS RELATING TO THE USE OF 
                           OFFICIAL EQUIPMENT

  Mr. LOTT (for himself and Mr. Dodd) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 238

       Resolved, That (a) the Committee on Rules and 
     Administration of the Senate may issue regulations to 
     authorize a Senator or officer or employee of the Senate to 
     use official equipment for purposes incidental to the conduct 
     of their official duties.
       (b) Any use under subsection (a) shall be subject to such 
     terms and conditions as set forth in the regulations.
```
