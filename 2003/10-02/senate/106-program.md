# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. For the information of all Senators, tomorrow morning, as 
was just pointed out, following morning business, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the Iraq/Afghanistan Supplemental 
Appropriations bill. There will be no rollcall votes during tomorrow's 
session. Senators will have the opportunity throughout tomorrow to come 
to the floor and offer amendments on the bill. However, no action will 
occur on any of the amendments tomorrow.
  Under a previous order, the next rollcall vote will occur Tuesday, 
October 14, at 2:30 p.m.
```
