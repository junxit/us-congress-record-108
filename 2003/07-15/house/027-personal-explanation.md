# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Pitts, Joseph R. (P000373) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1950, H.R. 2330

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. PITTS. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall votes 360 and 361, I was 
inadvertently detained.
  May the record reflect that on rollcall vote No. 360, I would have 
voted ``yea,'' in support of the rule for the consideration of H.R. 
1950.
  And, on rollcall vote No. 361, on passage of H.R. 2330, the Burmese 
Freedom and Democracy Act, I would have voted ``yea,'' as I am an 
original cosponsor of the bill.
```
