# CONGRATULATIONS ON THE BIRTH OF CATHERINE ANNE PAYNE

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
          CONGRATULATIONS ON THE BIRTH OF CATHERINE ANNE PAYNE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 15, 2003

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, congratulations to Mr. 
Thomas ``Chip'' Payne and Shannon Payne, on the birth of their 
daughter, Catherine Anne Payne, on Thursday, July 10th, 2003 at 6:23 
a.m. Catherine was born at Columbia's Richland Memorial Hospital 
weighing 7 pounds and being 19\1/2\ inches in length. I extend my 
warmest wishes for a successful and happy life who is blessed to have 
devoted parents.




                          ____________________
```
