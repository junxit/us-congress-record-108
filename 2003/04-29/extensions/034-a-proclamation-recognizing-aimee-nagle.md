# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING AIMEE NAGLE

```
                 A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING AIMEE NAGLE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 29, 2003

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker,
  Whereas, Aimee Nagle has devoted herself to serving others through 
her membership in the Girl Scouts; and
  Whereas, Aimee Nagle has shared her time and talent with the 
community in which she resides; and
  Whereas, Aimee Nagle has demonstrated a commitment to meet challenges 
with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, Aimee Nagle must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication she put forth in earning the Girl Scout Gold Award;
  Therefore, I join with the Girl Scouts, the residents of West 
Lafayette and the entire 18th Congressional District in congratulating 
Aimee Nagle as she receives the Girl Scout Gold Award.

                          ____________________
```
