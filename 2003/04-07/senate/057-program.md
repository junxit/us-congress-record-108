# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. For the information of all Senators, on behalf of 
Senator Frist, the majority leader, the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business tomorrow until 10:30 a.m. Following morning business, 
the Senate will resume debate on the nomination of Priscilla Owen. A 
number of Senators have indicated that they are prepared to speak on 
her nomination, and I hope they will do so during tomorrow's session.
  Also as a reminder, it is my expectation that the Senate will take up 
the CARE Act tomorrow afternoon under the agreement reached last week.
  As mentioned this morning, there are a number of issues that may be 
addressed this week prior to the Easter recess therefore, Senators 
should expect votes each day of the session.
```
