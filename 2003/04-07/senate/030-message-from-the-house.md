# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:00 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1559. An act making emergency wartime supplemental 
     appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2003, 
     and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to 20 U.S.C. 4303, and the 
order of the House of January 8, 2003, the Speaker appoints the 
following Member of the House of Representatives to the Board of 
Trustees of Gallaudet University: Ms. Woolsey of California.
  The message further announced that pursuant to 20 U.S.C. 955(b) note, 
the Minority Leader appoints the following Member of the House of 
Representatives to the National Council on the

[[Page 8598]]

Arts for the 108th Congress: Ms. McCollum of Minnesota.

                          ____________________
```
