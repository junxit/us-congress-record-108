# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 5 p.m. Under the previous order, the time until 5 p.m. shall be 
equally divided between the Senator from Texas, Mrs. Hutchison, and the 
Democratic leader or their designees.
  The Senator from Colorado.

                          ____________________
```
