# AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
       AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during this 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader or the assistant 
majority leader or Senator Santorum be authorized to sign duly enrolled 
bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
