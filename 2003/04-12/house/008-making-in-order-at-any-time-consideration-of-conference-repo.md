# MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 1559, EMERGENCY WARTIME SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2003

**Speaking:** Young, C. W. Bill (Y000031) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1559

```
MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 
     1559, EMERGENCY WARTIME SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2003

  Mr. YOUNG of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it 
shall be in order at any time to consider the conference report to 
accompany H.R. 1559; all points of order against the conference report 
and against its consideration are waived; the conference report shall 
be considered as read; and clause 10 of rule XX shall not apply to the 
question of adoption of the conference report.
```
