# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1875

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1875

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
due its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the title of the bill 
for the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1875) to amend the Employee Retirement Income 
     Security Act of 1974, the Public Health Service Act, and the 
     Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the mental health 
     benefits parity provisions for an additional year.

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be placed on 
the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
