# RICHARD D. WATKINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3175

```
                RICHARD D. WATKINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3175) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2650 Cleveland Avenue, NW in Canton, Ohio, as 
the ``Richard D. Watkins Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
