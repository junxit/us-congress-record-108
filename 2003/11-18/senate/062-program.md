# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow, following morning business, the Senate may begin 
consideration of the Energy Policy Act conference report. The official 
conference papers have now arrived and copies will be available to all 
Members. If we are unable to reach consent for a time limit on the 
Energy conference report, it may be necessary to file cloture during 
tomorrow's session.
  The Senate may also take up other conference reports as they become 
available and nominations on the Executive Calendar during tomorrow's 
session. Therefore, rollcall votes should be expected tomorrow.
```
