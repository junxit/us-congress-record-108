# APPOINTMENT OF HONORABLE TOM DAVIS OF VIRGINIA OR HONORABLE MAC THORNBERRY TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS UNTIL THE DAY THE HOUSE CONVENES FOR THE SECOND...

```
    APPOINTMENT OF HONORABLE TOM DAVIS OF VIRGINIA OR HONORABLE MAC 
  THORNBERRY TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND 
   JOINT RESOLUTIONS UNTIL THE DAY THE HOUSE CONVENES FOR THE SECOND 
                     SESSION OF THE 108TH CONGRESS

  The Speaker pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                November 25, 2003.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Tom Davis or, if not 
     available to perform this duty, the Honorable Mac Thornberry 
     to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and 
     joint resolutions until the day the House convenes for the 
     second session of the 108th Congress.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
