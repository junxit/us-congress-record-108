# APPOINTMENT OF HONORABLE ROSCOE G. BARTLETT OF MARYLAND TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS ON TODAY

```
   APPOINTMENT OF HONORABLE ROSCOE G. BARTLETT OF MARYLAND TO ACT AS 
  SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS ON 
                                 TODAY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
Communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                November 25, 2003.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Roscoe G. Bartlett to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions on this day.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
