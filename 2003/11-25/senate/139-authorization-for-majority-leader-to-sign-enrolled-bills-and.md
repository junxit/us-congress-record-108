# AUTHORIZATION FOR MAJORITY LEADER TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS DURING SENATE'S ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
  AUTHORIZATION FOR MAJORITY LEADER TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT 
                RESOLUTIONS DURING SENATE'S ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that, during 
the Senate's adjournment, the majority leader be authorized to sign 
enrolled bills and joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
