# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION AND THE WORKFORCE

**Speaking:** Doolittle, John T. (D000429) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 70

```
     ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION AND THE WORKFORCE

  Mr. DOOLITTLE. Madam Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 70) and 
ask unanimous consent for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 70

       Resolved, That the following Member be and is hereby 
     elected to the following standing committee of the House of 
     Representatives: Education and the Workforce: Mr. Burns.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?

[[Page H417]]

  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
