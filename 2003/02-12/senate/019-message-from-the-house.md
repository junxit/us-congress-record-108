# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:26 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, without amendment:

       S. 141. An act to improve the calculation of the Federal 
     subsidy rate with respect to certain small business loans, 
     and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the House has passed the following 
bills and joint resolution, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 337. An act to extend certain hydro-electric licenses 
     in the State of Alaska.
       H.R. 397. An act to reinstate and extend the deadline for 
     commencement of construction of a hydroelectric project in 
     the State of Illinois.
       H.J. Res. 19. A joint resolution recognizing the 92nd 
     birthday of Ronald Reagan.

  The message also announced that the House has agreed to the following 
concurrent resolutions, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 22. Concurrent Resolution honoring Czech 
     Republic President Vaclav Havel.
       H. Con. Res. 27. Concurrent Resolution condemning the 
     selection of Libya to chair the United Nations Commission on 
     Human Rights, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
