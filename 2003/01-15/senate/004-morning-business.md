# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m., with the time equally divided and 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
```
