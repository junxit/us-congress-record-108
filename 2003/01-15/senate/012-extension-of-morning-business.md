# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the time for 
morning business be extended until noon and the time be equally divided 
in the usual form with Senators allowed to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each.
```
