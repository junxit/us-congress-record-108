 HONORING THE GIRL SCOUTS WHO HAVE RECEIVED THE SILVER AND GOLD AWARDS

● Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, I rise today to honor the Girl 
Scouts in Rhode Island who have received the Silver and Gold Awards for 
2002.
  I praise all of the hard work the girls have done throughout the year 
to receive their respective awards.
  The Girl Scout Gold Award is the highest and most prestigious award a 
girl can earn in girl scouting. A girl who has earned the Girl Scout 
Gold Award can look forward to greater access to college scholarships, 
paid internships, and community awards.
  I ask that the list of the girls receiving the awards be printed in 
the Record.
