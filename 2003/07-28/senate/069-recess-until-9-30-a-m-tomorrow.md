# RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** S.Res. 203

```
                    RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate recess under 
the provisions of S. Res. 203, as a further mark of respect to our 
distinguished and late former colleague, Senator Vance Hartke.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:45 p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, July 29, 2003, at 9:30 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
