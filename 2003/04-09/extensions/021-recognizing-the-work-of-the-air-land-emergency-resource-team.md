# RECOGNIZING THE WORK OF THE AIR LAND EMERGENCY RESOURCE TEAM ``COLUMBIA'' SHUTTLE DISASTER CREW

```

                        Wednesday, April 9, 2003

  Mr. SAM JOHNSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I want to commend 158 men of 
the International ALERT Academy who selflessly spent 11 days in San 
Augustine County, Texas, helping with recovery efforts following the 
disastrous crash of the space shuttle Columbia. From February 2-12, 
2003, these men served our country and the families of the crew at 
their own expense, searching through the dense forests and briars of 
that region. Through their thoroughness, hard work, and willing 
attitudes these men brought encouragement to the leadership of the 
incident command structure, and every fellow worker involved.
```
