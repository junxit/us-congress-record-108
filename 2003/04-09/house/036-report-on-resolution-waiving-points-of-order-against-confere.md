# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 151, PROSECUTORIAL REMEDIES AND TOOLS AGAINST THE EXPLOITATION OF CHILDREN TODAY ACT OF 2003

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 141, S. 151, H.Res. 188

```
       REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST 
     CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 151, PROSECUTORIAL REMEDIES AND TOOLS 
     AGAINST THE EXPLOITATION OF CHILDREN TODAY ACT OF 2003

  Mrs. MYRICK (during consideration of H. Con. Res. 141), from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 108-68) on 
the resolution (H. Res. 188) waiving points of order against the 
conference report to accompany the Senate bill (S. 151) to amend title 
18, United States Code, with respect to the sexual exploitation of 
children, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
