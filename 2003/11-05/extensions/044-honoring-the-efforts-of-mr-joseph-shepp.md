# HONORING THE EFFORTS OF MR. JOSEPH SHEPP

```
                HONORING THE EFFORTS OF MR. JOSEPH SHEPP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JON C. PORTER

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 5, 2003

  Mr. PORTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to Mr. Joseph 
Shepp, who has been awarded the AARP Andrus Award for Community Service 
in the State of Nevada for 2003. The Andrus Award honors the 
outstanding volunteering efforts of AARP volunteers and members in 
every state.
  Mr. Shepp provides yet another example of service and volunteerism to 
the Southern Nevada community. He has demonstrated his service to the 
community through his service and commitment to organizations like 
Seniors and Law Enforcement Together and the Senior Assisted Living 
Project. We in Southern Nevada are fortunate to find yet another role 
model in Mr. Shepp. I wish him all the best in his future endeavors and 
congratulate him on this well-deserved award.

                          ____________________
```
