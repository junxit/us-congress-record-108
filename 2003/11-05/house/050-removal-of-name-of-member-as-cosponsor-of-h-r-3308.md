# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3308

**Speaking:** Dooley, Calvin M. (D000424) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3242, H.R. 3308

```
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3308

  Mr. DOOLEY of California. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
the gentleman from Texas (Mr. Rodriguez) be removed as a cosponsor from 
my bill, H.R. 3308. The gentleman from Texas had asked me to be a 
cosponsor of H.R. 3242, which I introduced, and was mistakenly added as 
a cosponsor of H.R. 3308.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
