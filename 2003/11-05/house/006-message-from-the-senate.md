# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with amendments in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 3365. An act to amend title 10, United States Code, 
     and the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase the death 
     gratuity payable with respect to deceased members of the 
     Armed Forces and to exclude such gratuity from gross income.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1720. An act to provide for Federal court proceedings in 
     Plano, Texas.

                          ____________________
```
