# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 1, H.Con.Res. 274, H.Res. 355, H.Res. 372

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on one motion to suspend the rules previously postponed and 
on the motions to instruct postponed Thursday, October 2.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H. Con. Res. 274, by the yeas and nays;
  Motion to instruct on H.R. 1 by Mr. Bishop of New York, by the yeas 
and nays;
  Motion to instruct on H.R. 1 by Mr. Flake, by the yeas and nays.
  The votes on H. Res. 355 and H. Res. 372 will be taken tomorrow.
  The first and third electronic votes will be conducted as 15-minute 
votes. The second vote in this series will be a 5-minute vote.

                          ____________________
```
