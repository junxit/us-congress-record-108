# HONORING ESTHER SHIPP ON HER 90TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Porter, Jon C. (P000589) [R-NV]

```
               HONORING ESTHER SHIPP ON HER 90TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JON C. PORTER

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 7, 2003

  Mr. PORTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Esther Shipp on 
her 90th Birthday. Born September 7, 1913, Esther has been a resident 
of Boulder City since 1941. I am pleased to join Esther's friends, her 
five generations of relatives, and all of Boulder City in 
congratulating her on reaching this milestone.
```
