# HONORING ALICE MAY LARSON ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

```
            HONORING ALICE MAY LARSON ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JON C. PORTER

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 7, 2003

  Mr. PORTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Alice May 
Larson on her 100th birthday. Alice was born in Duluth, Minnesota, on 
October 4, 1903, and currently resides in Boulder City. I am pleased to 
join Alice's friends, relatives, and all of Boulder City in 
congratulating her on reaching this milestone.

                          ____________________
```
