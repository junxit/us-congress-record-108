# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the period for 
morning business be extended to 2 p.m., with the time equally divided, 
and that Senators be permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I further ask unanimous consent that 
Senator Nelson of Florida be recognized at the conclusion of my 
remarks.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
