**Measures:** H.R. 1350

```
     TO REAUTHORIZE THE INDIVIDUALS WITH DISABILITIES EDUCATION ACT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate having 
received from the House a message, the Senate agrees to a request for a 
conference on H.R. 1350, the Senate agrees to the request for a 
conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses, and the Chair

Mr. Enzi, Mr. Alexander, Mr. Bond, Mr. DeWine, Mr. Roberts, Mr. 
Sessions, Mr. Ensign, Mr. Graham of South Carolina, Mr. Warner, Mr. 
Kennedy, Mr. Dodd, Mr. Harkin, Ms. Mikulski, Mr. Jeffords, Mr. 
Bingaman, Mrs. Murray, Mr. Reed of Rhode Island, Mr. Edwards, and Mrs. 
Clinton conferees on the part of the Senate.
```
