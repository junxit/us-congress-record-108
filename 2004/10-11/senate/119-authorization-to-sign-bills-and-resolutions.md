# AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
              AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that during the adjournment of the 
Senate, the majority leader, the junior Senator from Alabama, and the 
senior Senator from Virginia be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills 
on joint resolutions.
```
