# PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

**Speaking:** Conyers, John, Jr. (C000714) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 3193

```
                         PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

  Mr. CONYERS. Mr. Speaker, parliamentary inquiry.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Fossella). The gentleman will state his 
parliamentary inquiry.
  Mr. CONYERS. Mr. Speaker, under the provisions of the bill just 
passed, H.R. 3193, will the Members of the House of Representatives be 
allowed to bring concealed weapons to the floor of this body?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The gentleman's question is not a proper 
parliamentary inquiry.
  The rules of the House would govern that question as a matter of 
decorum.

                          ____________________
```
