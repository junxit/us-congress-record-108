# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Pence, Mike (P000587) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.Res. 806

```
    ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. PENCE. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 806) and I ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The SPEAKER. The Clerk will report the resolution.
  The Clerk read as follows:

                              H. Res. 806

       Resolved, That the following Members be and are hereby 
     elected to the following standing committees of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on Government Reform: Mr. Cantor.
       Committee on Rules: Mr. Putnam.

  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Indiana?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
