# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Allard, Wayne (A000109) [R-CO]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                          committee on finance

  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Finance be authorized to

[[Page S2314]]

meet during the session on Monday, March 8, 2004, at 2 p.m., to 
consider the nomination of Mark B. McClellen to be Administrator of the 
Center for Medicare and Medicaid Services; Brian Rosoboro to be Under 
Secretary of the Department; Donald Korb, to be Chief Counsel for the 
Internal Revenue Service and Assistant General Counsel in the 
Department of the Treasury; and Mark J. Warshawsky, to be Assistant 
Secretary, U.S. Department of Treasury.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
