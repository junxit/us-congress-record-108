# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3783

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3783

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk due its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the title of the bill for 
the second time.

[[Page 2517]]

  The assistant journal clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3783) to provide an extension of highway, 
     highway safety, motor carrier safety, transit, and other 
     programs funded out of the Highway Trust Fund pending 
     enactment of a law reauthorizing the Transportation Equity 
     Act for the 21st Century.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I object to further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be placed on the Calendar.
```
