# MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 3108, PENSION FUNDING EQUITY ACT OF 2004

```
MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 
                3108, PENSION FUNDING EQUITY ACT OF 2004

  Mr. DUNCAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it be in order 
at any time to consider a conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 
3108) to amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 and 
the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to temporarily replace the 30-year 
Treasury rate with a rate based on long-term corporate bonds for 
certain pension plan funding requirements, and for other purposes, that 
the conference report be considered as read, and that all points of 
order against the conference report and against its consideration be 
waived.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Tennessee?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
