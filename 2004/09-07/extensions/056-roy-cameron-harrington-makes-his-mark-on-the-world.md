# ROY CAMERON HARRINGTON MAKES HIS MARK ON THE WORLD

```
           ROY CAMERON HARRINGTON MAKES HIS MARK ON THE WORLD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BOB ETHERIDGE

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 7, 2004

  Mr. ETHERIDGE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Roy and 
Rhonda Harrington on the birth of their son, Mr. Roy Cameron 
Harrington. Roy was born on Thursday, July 1, 2004 and he weighed 7 
pounds and 5 ounces. Faye joins me in wishing Roy and Rhonda great 
happiness during this very special time in their lives.
  As a father of three, I know the immeasurable pride and rewarding 
challenge that children bring into your life. The birth of a child 
changes your perspective on life and opens the world to you in a fresh, 
new way. Their innocence keeps you young-at-heart. A little miracle, a 
new baby holds all the potential of what human beings can achieve.
  With great happiness, I welcome young Roy into the world and wish Roy 
and Rhonda all the best as they raise him.

                          ____________________
```
