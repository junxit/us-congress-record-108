```

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will be a 
period for the transaction of morning business for statements only 
until 5 p.m., with the time equally divided in the usual form.
  The Senator from Mississippi.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, my apologies; what is the time divide?

manner.
```
