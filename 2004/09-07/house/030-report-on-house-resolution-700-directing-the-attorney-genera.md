# REPORT ON HOUSE RESOLUTION 700, DIRECTING THE ATTORNEY GENERAL TO TRANSMIT DOCUMENTS IN THE POSSESSION OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL RELATING TO TREATMENT OF PRISONERS AND DETAINEES IN IRAQ, AFGHANISTAN, AND GUANTANAMO BAY

```
   REPORT ON HOUSE RESOLUTION 700, DIRECTING THE ATTORNEY GENERAL TO 
 TRANSMIT DOCUMENTS IN THE POSSESSION OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL RELATING 
   TO TREATMENT OF PRISONERS AND DETAINEES IN IRAQ, AFGHANISTAN, AND 
                             GUANTANAMO BAY

Representatives documents in the possession of the Attorney General 
relating to the treatment of prisoners and detainees in Iraq, 
Afghanistan, and Guantanamo Bay, which was referred to the House
```
