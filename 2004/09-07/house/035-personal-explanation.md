# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mrs. JONES of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, on Tuesday September 7, 2004, I was 
unavoidably detained and missed rollcall votes Nos. 422 and 423. Had I 
been present for the votes, I would have voted ``Aye'' for both 
measures to name The General William Carey Lee Post Office Building, 
and to name The Harvey and Bernice Jones Post Office Building, 
respectively.
  I ask unanimous consent that my vote statement appear in the 
appropriate place in the Congressional Record behind the day's recorded 
votes.
```
