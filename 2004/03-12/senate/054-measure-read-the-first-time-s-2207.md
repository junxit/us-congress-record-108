# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2207

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2207

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand that S. 2207 is at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2207) to improve women's access to health care 
     services, and the access of all individuals to emergency and 
     trauma care services, by reducing the excessive burden the 
     liability system places on the delivery of such services.

  Mr. FRIST. I now ask for its second reading in order to place the 
bill on the Calendar under provisions of rule XIV and object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be read the second time on the 
next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
