# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 22, 2004

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 22, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 12 noon on 
Monday, March 22. I further ask that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then begin a period for morning 
business until 2 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 
minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
