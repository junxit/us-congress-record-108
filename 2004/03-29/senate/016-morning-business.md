# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. GRASSLEY. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now proceed to 
a period for morning business with Senators permitted to speak up to 10 
minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  The Senator from Michigan.
  Mr. LEVIN. Reserving the right to object, I wonder if as part of that 
agreement we could line up speakers as follows: That Senator Durbin be 
recognized in morning business for 15 minutes; followed by Senator 
Bennett for 20 minutes; followed by myself for 15 minutes; followed by 
the Senator from Minnesota, Mr. Dayton, for 15 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.
```
