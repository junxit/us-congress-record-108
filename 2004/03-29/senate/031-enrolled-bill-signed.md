# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 254

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  The message also announced that the Speaker of the House of 
Representatives has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 254. An act to authorize the President of the United 
     States to agree to certain amendments to the Agreement 
     between the Government of the United States of America and 
     the Government of the United Mexican States concerning the 
     establishment of a Border Environment Cooperation Commission 
     and a North American Development Bank, and for other 
     purposes.

  The enrolled bill was signed subsequently by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Stevens).
```
