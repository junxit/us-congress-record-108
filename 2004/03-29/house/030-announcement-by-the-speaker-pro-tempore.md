# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 2584, H.R. 3723

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on motions to suspend the rules previously postponed. These 
votes will be taken in the following order:
  Motion to concur in Senate amendments to H.R. 2584, by the yeas and 
nays;
  H.R. 3723, by the yeas and nays.
  Both of these will be 15-minute votes.
```
