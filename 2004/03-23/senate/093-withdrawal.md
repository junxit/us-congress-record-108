# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on March 
23, 2004, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       THOMAS HILL MOORE, OF FLORIDA, TO BE A COMMISSIONER OF THE 
     CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY COMMISSION FOR A TERM OF SEVEN YEARS 
     FROM OCTOBER 27, 2002, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON MARCH 
     11, 2004.
```
