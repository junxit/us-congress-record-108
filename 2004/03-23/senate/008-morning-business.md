# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business until 11 a.m., with 
the majority leader in control of the first half of the time, and the 
Democratic leader or his designee in control of the remaining time.
  Does the minority leader seek recognition?
  Mr. DASCHLE. I do, Mr. President.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The minority leader is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
