# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 4, 2004

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 4, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Monday, October 4. I further ask consent that following the prayer and 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved, and that 
there then be a period of morning business for up to 60 minutes, with 
the first 30 minutes under the control of the minority leader or his 
designee and the second 30 minutes under the control of the majority 
leader or his designee; provided further that following morning 
business, the Senate resume consideration of S. 2845, the intelligence 
reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
