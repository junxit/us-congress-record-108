# HONORING CALHOUN MIDDLE SCHOOL'S AMERICAN SPIRIT TOWARD THE VICTIMS OF THE BESLAN SCHOOL TRAGEDY

**Speaking:** Burgess, Michael C. (B001248) [R-TX]

```
HONORING CALHOUN MIDDLE SCHOOL'S AMERICAN SPIRIT TOWARD THE VICTIMS OF 
                       THE BESLAN SCHOOL TRAGEDY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MICHAEL C. BURGESS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 30, 2004

  Mr. BURGESS. Mr. Speaker, today, I would like to say thank you to one 
of my hometown schools, Calhoun Middle School in Denton, Texas. The 
students of Calhoun Middle School enthusiastically participated in a 
card giving program for the victims of the Beslan school tragedy.
  Before departing for my mission to Russia, I asked several local 
schools if they would like to create, draw, color or write greeting 
cards to the survivors and grieving families. The response was 
overwhelming. I was honored to be able to present hundreds of colorful 
cards to patients and families.
  During my time in Russia, I had the opportunity to visit survivors 
recuperating at two hospitals. The visits were moving and tearful,

[[Page E1764]]

but you could see the hearts of American students truly shining in the 
eyes of the Russian children. I was truly privileged to represent the 
United States, and especially grateful to be able to share wonderful 
cards full of encouragement and joy.
  Again, thank you, the students of Calhoun Middle School for your 
kindheartedness and thoughtfulness. Your words strongly conveyed the 
spirit of the American people and our commitment to build relations 
with countries dedicated to combating global terrorism.

                          ____________________
```
