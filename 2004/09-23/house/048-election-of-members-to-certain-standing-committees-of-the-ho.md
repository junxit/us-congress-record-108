# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 785, H.Res. 795

```
    ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 795) and I ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the resolution.
  The Clerk read as follows:

                              H. Res. 785

       Resolved, That the following Members be and are hereby 
     elected to the following standing committees of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on Financial Services: Mr. Gerlach.
       Committee on Government Reform: Mr. Burgess.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
