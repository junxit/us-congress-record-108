**Speaking:** Nelson, Bill (N000032) [D-FL]

recess until 2 p.m.

reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. 
Alexander).

  Mr. NELSON of Florida. I ask unanimous consent that I be permitted to 
speak for 7 minutes as if in morning business.
