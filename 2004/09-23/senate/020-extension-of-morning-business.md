# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I am wondering if we should extend the time 
until 12:30. I ask unanimous consent that be the agreement, and that it 
not be evenly split. Whoever comes here should be able to speak.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to extending morning 
business until 12:30?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
