# NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                      NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS


               Committee on Energy and Natural Resources

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that the following hearing has 
been scheduled before the committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
  The hearing will be held on Thursday, September 30 at 10:30 a.m., in 
room SD-366.
  The purpose of this hearing is to receive testimony regarding issues 
related to low level radioactive waste.
  Because of the limited time available for the hearing, witnesses may 
testify by invitation only. However, those wishing to submit written 
testimony for the hearing record should send two copies of their 
testimony to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, United 
States Senate, SD-364 Dirksen Senate Office Building, Washington, DC 
20510-6150.
  For further information, please contact: Clint Williamson at 202-224-
7556, Dr. Pete Lyons at 202-224-5861 or Shane Perkins at 202-224-7555.

                          ____________________
```
