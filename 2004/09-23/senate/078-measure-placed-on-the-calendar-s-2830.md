# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2830

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2830

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the second 
time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2830) to amend part A of title IV of the Social 
     Security Act to promote healthy marriages and responsible 
     fatherhood, and for other purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I object to further proceedings on the measure at this 
time in order to place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of 
rule XIV.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be placed on 
the calendar under rule XIV.

                          ____________________
```
