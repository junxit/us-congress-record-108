# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 24, 2004

```
        CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 24, 2004

  Mr. EHLERS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Wednesday, November 24, 
2004, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 529, in 
which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Michigan?
  There was no objection.
```
