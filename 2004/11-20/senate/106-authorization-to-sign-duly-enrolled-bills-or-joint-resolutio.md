# AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
     AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during this 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader, the assistant majority 
leader, and the senior Senator from Virginia be authorized to sign duly 
enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
