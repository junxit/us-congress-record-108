# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S. 714, H.R. 2707

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on the Speaker's approval of the Journal and motions to 
suspend the rules previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  Approval of the Journal, de novo;
  H.R. 2707, by the yeas and nays; and
  S. 714, by the yeas and nays.
  The first and third electronic votes will be conducted as 15-minute 
votes. The second vote in this series will be a 5-minute vote, without 
objection.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
