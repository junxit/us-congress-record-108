# COMMUNICATION FROM CHIEF OF STAFF OF THE HONORABLE SHELLEY MOORE CAPITO, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM CHIEF OF STAFF OF THE HONORABLE SHELLEY MOORE 
                       CAPITO, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Mark Johnson, Chief of Staff of the Honorable 
Shelley Moore Capito, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, February 17, 2004.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to notify you formally, pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives, 
     that I have been served with a grand jury subpoena, issued by 
     the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of West 
     Virginia, for the production of documents.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the precedents and privileges of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Mark Johnson,
     Chief of Staff.

                          ____________________
```
