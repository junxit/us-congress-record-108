# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S. 523, H.R. 743

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, the Speaker 
pro tempore signed the following enrolled bills on Thursday, February 
19, 2004:
  H.R. 743, to amend the Social Security Act and the Internal Revenue 
Code of 1986 to provide additional safeguards for Social Security and 
Supplemental Security Income beneficiaries with representative payees, 
to enhance program protections, and for other purposes;
  S. 523, to make technical corrections to laws relating to Native
```
