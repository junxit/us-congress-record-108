# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 60 
minutes, with the first half of the time under the control of the 
Democratic leader or his designee and the second half of the time under 
the control of the majority leader or his designee.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Minnesota is recognized.
```
