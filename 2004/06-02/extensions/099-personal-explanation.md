# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gutierrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. LUIS V. GUTIERREZ

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 2, 2004

  Mr. GUTIERREZ. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably absent for votes in 
this Chamber on April 2, 2004. I would like the record to show that, 
had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall votes 115, 
116, and 117.
  In addition, I was inadvertently delayed and missed votes on June 1, 
2004. I would like the record to show that, had I been present, I would 
have voted ``yea'' on rollcall votes 210, 211, and 212.
```
