# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, June 23, 2004, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 1848. An act to amend the Bend Pine Nursery Land 
     Conveyance Act to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to sell 
     the Bend Pine Nursery Administration Site in the State of 
     Oregon.
       S. 2238. An act to amend the National Flood Insurance Act 
     of 1968 to reduce losses to properties for which repetitive 
     flood insurance claim payments have been made.
```
