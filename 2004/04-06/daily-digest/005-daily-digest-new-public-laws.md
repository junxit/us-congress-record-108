# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 254, H.R. 3926, H.R. 4062

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D347)

United States of America and the Government of the United Mexican

108-217).
```
