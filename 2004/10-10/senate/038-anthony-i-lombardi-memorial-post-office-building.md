# ANTHONY I. LOMBARDI MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
           ANTHONY I. LOMBARDI MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4618) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 10 West Prospect Street in Nanuet, New York, 
as the ``Anthony I. Lombardi Memorial Post Office Building'' was 
considered, read the third time, and passed.
```
