# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, finally, if there is no further business to 
come before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand 
in adjournment under the previous order, following up to 20 minutes to 
be used in morning business by our colleague from the great State of 
Missouri.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Missouri.

                          ____________________
```
