# McINNIS CANYONS NATIONAL CONSERVATION AREA

```
               McINNIS CANYONS NATIONAL CONSERVATION AREA

  The bill (H.R. 4827) to amend the Colorado Canyons National 
Conservation Area and Black Ridge Canyons Wilderness Act of 2000 to 
rename the Colorado Canyons National Conservation Area as the McInnis 
Canyons National Conservation Area, was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
