# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 2845

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 2845

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 2845

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate receives from the House a message regarding S. 2845, the Senate 
disagree to the amendment from the House, agree to the request for a 
conference if one is requested, and that the Chair be authorized to 
appoint conferees on the part of the Senate in a ratio of either 7 to 6 
or 6 to 5.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
