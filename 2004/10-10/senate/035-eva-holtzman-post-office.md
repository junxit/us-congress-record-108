# EVA HOLTZMAN POST OFFICE

```
                        EVA HOLTZMAN POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 5039) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at United States Route 1 in Ridgeway, North 
Carolina, as the ``Eva Holtzman Post Office'' was considered, read the 
third time, and passed.
```
