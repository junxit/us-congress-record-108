# HARVEY AND BERNICE JONES POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
             HARVEY AND BERNICE JONES POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4381) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2811 Springdale Avenue in Springdale, 
Arkansas, as the ``Harvey and Bernice Jones Post Office Building'' was 
considered, read the third time, and passed.
```
