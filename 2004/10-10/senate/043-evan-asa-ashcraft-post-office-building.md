# EVAN ASA ASHCRAFT POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 EVAN ASA ASHCRAFT POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5147) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 23055 Sherman Way in West Hills, California, 
as the ``Evan Asa Ashcraft Post Office Building'' was considered, read 
the third time, and passed.
```
