# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2974, S. 2975

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. DeWINE (for himself, Mr. Kennedy, and Mr. 
             Jeffords):
       S. 2974. A bill to protect the public health by providing 
     the Food and Drug Administration with certain authority to 
     regulate tobacco products; considered and passed.
           By Mr. HARKIN (for himself and Mr. Kennedy):
       S. 2975. A bill to amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of
```
