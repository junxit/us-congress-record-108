# LEONARD C. BURCH POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 LEONARD C. BURCH POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5051) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1001 Williams Street in Ignacio, Colorado, as 
the ``Leonard C. Burch Post Office Building'' was considered, read the 
third time, and passed.
```
