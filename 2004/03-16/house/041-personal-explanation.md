# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Carson, Julia (C000191) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 339

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. CARSON of Indiana. Mr. Speaker, on March 10 during the vote on 
the Personal Responsibility in Food Consumption Act, H.R. 339, I was 
present on the floor of the House of Representatives. However, my vote 
was not registered due to, I guess, my mistake in terms of leaving my 
card in the machine from the previous vote.
  Had this malfunction not occurred, I would have voted ``nay'' on this 
vote, rollcall vote No. 54.
```
