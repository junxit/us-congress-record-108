# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow morning, following morning 
business, the Senate will begin consideration of the conference report 
to accompany the pension reform bill. Under the unanimous consent 
agreement, there will be up to 4 hours for debate equally divided. 
Following the use or yielding back of time, the Senate will vote on the 
conference report. In addition to the pension reform conference report, 
the Senate may resume consideration of the FSC/ETI or JOBS bill.

                          ____________________
```
