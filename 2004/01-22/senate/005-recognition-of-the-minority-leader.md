# RECOGNITION OF THE MINORITY LEADER

```
                   RECOGNITION OF THE MINORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The minority leader is recognized.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask the assistant majority leader if he 
can advise Members with regard to the schedule tomorrow, whether any 
rollcall votes are expected.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I say to my friend and Democratic leader, I should be 
able to advise him later in the day, but I need to consult first with 
the majority leader.
```
