# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2004

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4567

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its

[[Page S9045]]

business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Friday, September 10. I 
further ask that following the prayer and the pledge, the morning hour 
be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, and 
following the time for the two leaders, the Senate then resume 
consideration of Calendar No. 588, H.R. 4567, the Homeland Security 
appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
