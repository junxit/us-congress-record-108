# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:12 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hanrahan, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
has passed the following bill, with an amendment:

       S. 2634. An act to amend the Public Health Services Act to 
     support the planning, implementation, and evaluation of 
     organized activities involving statewide youth suicide early 
     intervention and prevention strategies, to provide funds for 
     campus mental and behavioral health service centers, and for 
     other purposes.
```
