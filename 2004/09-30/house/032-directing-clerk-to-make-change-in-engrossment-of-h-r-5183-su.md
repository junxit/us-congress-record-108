# DIRECTING CLERK TO MAKE CHANGE IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 5183, SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2004, PART V

```
  DIRECTING CLERK TO MAKE CHANGE IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 5183, SURFACE 
              TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2004, PART V

  Mr. YOUNG of Alaska. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Clerk be directed to make the change in the engrossment of H.R. 5183 
that I have placed at the desk.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the change.
  The Clerk read as follows:

       In subsection (l)(1) that is proposed to be added at the 
     end of section 1101 of the Transportation Act of the 21st 
     Century by section 2(d) of the bill (H.R. 5183), strike 
     ``$21,311,774,667'' and insert ``$22,685,936,000''.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the change is agreed to.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
