                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations on 
the Executive Calendar, and for the information of Members, these are 
uniformed military promotions that were reported today by the Armed 
Services Committee: 867 through 900, and all nominations on the 
Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Marine Corps, and Navy.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nominations be confirmed, 
the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table, the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the Senate then resume 
legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nominations were considered and confirmed, as follows:
