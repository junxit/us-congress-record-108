                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                     HON. GEORGE R. NETHERCUTT, JR.

4568; ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 262, agreeing to the Sanders 
amendment to H.R. 4568; ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 263, agreeing to 
the Holt amendment to H.R. 4568; ``yea'' on rollcall vote No. 264, on 
passage of H.R. 4568, making appropriations for the Department of the 
Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2005; ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 265, on agreeing to the DeFazio 
amendment to H.R. 4567; and ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 266, on 
agreeing to the Sweeney amendment to H.R. 4567.
