# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 21, 2004

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2400

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 21, 2004

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 1 p.m. on Monday, 
June 21. I further ask that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of Calendar No. 
503, S. 2400, the DOD authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
