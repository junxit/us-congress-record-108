# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JANUARY 26, 2004

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 3108

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JANUARY 26, 2004

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
adjourn until 1 p.m., Monday, January 26. I further ask consent that 
following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then 
resume consideration of H.R. 3108, the pension bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
