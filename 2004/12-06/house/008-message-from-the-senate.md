# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 4012

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with amendments in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 4012. An act to amend the District of Columbia College 
     Access Act of 1999 to reauthorize for 5 additional years the 
     public school and private school tuition assistance programs 
     established under the Act.
```
