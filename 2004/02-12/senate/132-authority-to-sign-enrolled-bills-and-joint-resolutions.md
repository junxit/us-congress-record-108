# AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
         AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during this 
adjournment of the Senate the majority leader or the assistant majority 
leader be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
