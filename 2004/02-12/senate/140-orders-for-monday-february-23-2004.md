# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 23, 2004

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 2061

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 23, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 12 noon, Monday, 
February 23; I further ask that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and Senator Breaux then be recognized to deliver George 
Washington's Farewell Address, as provided under the previous order; 
provided that upon the conclusion of the address, the Senate then 
resume debate on the motion to proceed to Calendar No. 429, S. 2061, 
the medical malpractice bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
