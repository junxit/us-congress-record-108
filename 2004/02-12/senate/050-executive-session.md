# EXECUTIVE SESSION

```
                           EXECUTIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

 NOMINATION OF SAMUEL W. BODMAN TO BE DEPUTY SECRETARY OF THE TREASURY

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to executive session to consider the nomination of 
Samuel W. Bodman to be Deputy Secretary of

[[Page 2131]]

the Treasury, which was reported by the Finance Committee today. I 
further ask unanimous consent that the nomination be confirmed, the 
motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the Senate then return 
to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nomination considered and confirmed is as follows:


                         department of treasury

       Samuel W. Bodman, of Massachusetts, to be Deputy Secretary 
     of the Treasury.
```
