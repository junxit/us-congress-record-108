# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that morning 
business be extended by 5 minutes on each side.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. REID. I have no objection at all. I know Senator Kennedy has been 
waiting a long time, but that is fine. Five minutes won't hurt him. I 
have no objection.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Mississippi is recognized.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, if I could, I will yield an additional 5 
minutes to Senator Durbin and an additional 5 minutes to Senator 
Kennedy. That uses our entire 35 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Mississippi.

                          ____________________
```
