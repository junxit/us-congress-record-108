# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, tomorrow, following morning business, 
the Senate will resume consideration of the class action bill. The 
majority leader stated this morning that it is his desire to consider 
related amendments to the pending class action bill and finish the bill 
in a reasonable timeframe. It is our hope that progress can be made on 
the bill during tomorrow's session.
  Again, to reiterate, this is a bipartisan bill, and I would encourage 
Senators to show restraint in offering nonrelevant amendments.
```
