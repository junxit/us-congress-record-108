# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Paul, Ron (P000583) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 4754

```
H.R. 4754

                          Offered By: Mr. Paul

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for the American Community Survey.
```
