# Text of House Amendment 3

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 4754

```
H.R. 4754

                        Offered By: Mr. Sanders

       Amendment No. 3: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new title:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to make an application under section 501 of the 
     Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 (50 U.S.C. 
     1861) for an order requiring the production of library 
     circulation records, library patron lists, library Internet 
     records, bookseller sales records, or bookseller customer 
     lists.
```
