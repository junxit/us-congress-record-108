# Text of House Amendment 9

**Speaking:** Paul, Ron (P000583) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 4754

```
H.R. 4754

                          Offered By: Mr. Paul

       Amendment No. 9: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to pay expenses for any United States contribution to 
     the United Nations Educational, Scientific, and Cultural 
     Organization (UNESCO).
```
