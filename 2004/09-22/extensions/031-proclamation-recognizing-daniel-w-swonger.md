# PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING DANIEL W. SWONGER

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
               PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING DANIEL W. SWONGER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 22, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Daniel W. Swonger has devoted himself to serving others 
through his membership in the Boy Scouts of America; and
  Whereas, Daniel W. Swonger has shared his time and talent with the 
community in which he resides; and
  Whereas, Daniel W. Swonger has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, Daniel W. Swonger must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication he put forth in earning the Eagle Scout Award;
  Therefore, I join with Troop 86 and Daniel's family and friends in 
congratulating Daniel W. Swonger as he receives the Eagle Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```
