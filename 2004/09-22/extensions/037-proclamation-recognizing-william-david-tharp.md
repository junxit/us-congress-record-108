# PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING WILLIAM DAVID THARP

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
              PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING WILLIAM DAVID THARP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 22, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, William David Tharp has devoted himself to serving others 
through his membership in the Boy Scouts of America; and
  Whereas, William David Tharp has shared his time and talent with the 
community in which he resides; and
  Whereas, William David Tharp has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, William David Tharp must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication he put forth in earning the Eagle Scout Award;
  Therefore, I join with William's Boy Scout Troup and his family and 
friends in congratulating William David Tharp as he receives the Eagle 
Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```
