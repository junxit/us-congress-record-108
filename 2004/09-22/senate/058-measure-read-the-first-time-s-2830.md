# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2830

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2830

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2830

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand that S. 2830 is at the 
desk. I ask its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the first 
time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2830) to amend part A of title IV of the Social 
     Security Act to promote healthy marriages and responsible 
     fatherhood, and for other purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask for its second reading, and in order to place 
the bill on the calendar in accordance with rule XIV, I object to my 
own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be read the second time on the 
next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
