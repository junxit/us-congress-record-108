# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 4766

```
H.R. 4766

                         Offered By: Ms. Kaptur

       Amendment No. 1: Add at the end (before the short title), 
     the following new section:
       Sec. 7__. The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by reducing the amount made available under title I 
     for ``Common Computing Environment'' and by increasing the 
     amounts made available under title I for ``Marketing 
     Services'' and ``limitation on administrative expenses'' 
     under the heading ``AGRICULTURAL MARKETING SERVICE'' (for the 
     Farmers Market Promotion Program and administrative expenses 
     related to such program), by $6,000,000, $6,000,000, and 
     $250,000, respectively.
```
