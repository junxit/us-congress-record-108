# Text of House Amendment 33

**Speaking:** Weiner, Anthony D. (W000792) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4754

```
H.R. 4754

                         Offered By: Mr. Weiner

       Amendment No. 33: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used in contravention of the provisions of section 214(d) 
     of the Foreign Relations Authorization Act, Fiscal Year 2003 
     (Public Law 107-228).
```
