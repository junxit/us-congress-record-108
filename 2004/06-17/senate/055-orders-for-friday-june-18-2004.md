# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 18, 2004

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 18, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Friday, June 18. I 
further ask

[[Page 12744]]

that following the prayer and the pledge, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume consideration of Calendar No. 503, S. 2400, the 
Department of Defense authorization bill. I further ask consent that 
the cloture vote be vitiated. I further ask consent that the Brownback 
recognition request be vitiated.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
