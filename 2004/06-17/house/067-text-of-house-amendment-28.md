# Text of House Amendment 28

**Speaking:** Weiner, Anthony D. (W000792) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4567

```
H.R. 4567

                         Offered By: Mr. Weiner

       Amendment No. 28: At the end of the bill add the following:
       Sec. __. In making any threat assessment in conjunction 
     with the Urban Area Security Initiative, the Department of 
     Homeland Security shall weigh credible threat more heavily 
     than population concentration, critical infrastructure, or 
     any other consideration.
```
