# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M., MONDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 2004

           ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M., MONDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 2004

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand adjourned until 1 p.m., Monday, February 2, 2004.
  Thereupon, the Senate at 11 o'clock at 10 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Monday, February 2, 2004, at 1 p.m.
