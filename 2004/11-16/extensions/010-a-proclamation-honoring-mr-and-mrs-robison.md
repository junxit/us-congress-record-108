# A PROCLAMATION HONORING MR. AND MRS. ROBISON

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
                        A PROCLAMATION HONORING 
                          MR. AND MRS. ROBISON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 16, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Walter and Virginia Robison were united in marriage on 
August 26, 1944; and
  Whereas, Walter and Virginia Robison are celebrating 60 years of 
marriage; and
  Whereas, Walter and Virginia Robison have demonstrated love and 
commitment to each other; and
  Whereas, Walter and Virginia Robison must be commended for their 
loyalty and dedication to their family; and
  Whereas, Walter and Virginia Robison have proven, by their example, 
to be a model for all married couples;
  Therefore, I join with Walter and Virginia Robison's family, friends, 
and the residents of the 18th Congressional District of Ohio in 
congratulating Walter and Virginia Robison as they celebrate their 60th 
wedding anniversary.

                          ____________________
```
