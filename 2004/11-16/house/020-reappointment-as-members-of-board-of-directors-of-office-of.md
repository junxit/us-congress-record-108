# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF OFFICE OF COMPLIANCE

```
 REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF OFFICE OF COMPLIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 301 of the Congressional 
Accountability Act of 1995 (2 USC 1381), amended by Public Law 108-329, 
and the order of House of December 8, 2003, the Chair announces on 
behalf of the Speaker and minority leader of House of Representatives 
and the majority and minority leaders of the United States Senate their 
joint reappointment on October 27, 2004, of the following individuals 
to a 5-year term to the Board of Directors of the Office of Compliance:
  Mr. Allen V. Friedman, Los Angeles California.
  Ms. Susan S. Robfogel, Rochester, New York.
  Ms. Barbara Childs Wallace, Ridgeland, Mississippi.

                          ____________________
```
