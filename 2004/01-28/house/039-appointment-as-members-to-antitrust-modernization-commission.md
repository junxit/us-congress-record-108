# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO ANTITRUST MODERNIZATION COMMISSION

```
      APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO ANTITRUST MODERNIZATION COMMISSION

Modernization Commission Act of 2002 (15 U.S.C. 1 Note), and the order
```
