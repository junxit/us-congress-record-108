# RECOGNITION OF THE MINORITY LEADER

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```
                   RECOGNITION OF THE MINORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Democratic leader is recognized.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I hope we can complete our work on the 
pension bill today. We have been on it now for, I think, 6 days. We 
have been getting good cooperation on both sides of the aisle. I don't 
see any reason why before we conclude our work this afternoon we cannot 
finish this bill. I appreciate very much the report of the assistant 
majority leader this morning.

                          ____________________
```
