# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Tomorrow morning, the Senate will convene a pro forma 
session. No business will be transacted during Thursday's session. The 
Senate will then reconvene on Monday, February 2 at 1 p.m. At 2 p.m. we 
will resume debate on the motion to proceed. Under the order, the 
Senate will vote on invoking cloture on the motion to proceed to the 
highway bill at 5:45 p.m. Monday. If cloture is invoked, we will stay 
on that motion until it is disposed of. I encourage Members to come to 
the floor on Monday to begin their opening statements on the highway 
legislation.

                          ____________________
```
