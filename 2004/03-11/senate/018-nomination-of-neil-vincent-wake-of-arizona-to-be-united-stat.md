# NOMINATION OF NEIL VINCENT WAKE, OF ARIZONA, TO BE UNITED STATES DISTRICT JUDGE FOR THE DISTRICT OF ARIZONA

```
   NOMINATION OF NEIL VINCENT WAKE, OF ARIZONA, TO BE UNITED STATES 
               DISTRICT JUDGE FOR THE DISTRICT OF ARIZONA

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
move to the nomination of Neil Vincent Wake to be United States 
District Judge for the District of Arizona.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination 
of Neil Vincent Wake, of Arizona, to be United States District Judge 
for the District of Arizona?
  The nomination was considered and confirmed.

[[Page S2700]]
```
