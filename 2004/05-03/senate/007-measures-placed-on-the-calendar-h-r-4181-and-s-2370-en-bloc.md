# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 4181 AND S. 2370, EN BLOC

```
    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 4181 AND S. 2370, EN BLOC

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand that there are two bills 
at the desk due a second reading. I ask unanimous consent that the 
clerk read the titles of the bills for a second time en bloc.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 4181) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to permanently extend the marriage penalty relief 
     provided under the Economic Growth and Tax Relief 
     Reconciliation Act of 2001.
       A bill (S. 2370) to amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 
     1938 to provide for an increase in the Federal minimum wage, 
     and for other purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I object to further proceeding en bloc.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. The bills will 
be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
