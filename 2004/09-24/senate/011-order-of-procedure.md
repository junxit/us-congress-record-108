```

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we have two Senators on our side who wish to 
speak: Senator Wyden for 10 minutes and Senator Dorgan. On behalf of 
Senator Dorgan, I ask unanimous consent that he be allowed to speak for 
up to 40 minutes.

ordered.

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the order for the 
quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Dole). Without objection, it is so
```
