# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, tomorrow, following morning business, the 
Senate will resume debate on the motion to proceed to the asbestos 
bill. The majority leader is hoping to find a way to begin 
consideration of the asbestos litigation. However, the cloture vote on 
the motion to proceed to the bill will occur Thursday, unless an 
agreement is reached during the interim.
  Also, as a reminder, the Senate will conduct a cloture vote on the 
motion to proceed to the victims' rights amendment this week as well. 
Again, the majority leader has been working on an agreement to begin 
consideration of the victims' rights amendment. However, this 
procedural vote will be necessary unless that consent is granted.
```
