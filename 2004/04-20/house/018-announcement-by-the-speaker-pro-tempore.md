# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 3108

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, Speaker Pro 
Tempore Wolf signed the following enrolled bill on Friday, April 9, 
2004:
  H.R. 3108, to amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 
1974 and the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to temporarily replace the 
30-year Treasury rate with a rate based on long-term corporate bonds 
for certain pension plan funding requirements and other provisions, and 
for other purposes.
```
