# RECOGNIZING OLYMPIAD PAUL FOERSTER

```
                   RECOGNIZING OLYMPIAD PAUL FOERSTER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RALPH M. HALL

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 8, 2004

  Mr. HALL. Mr. Speaker, today I am honored to recognize Paul Foerster, 
resident of Rockwall, Texas and recipient of a gold medal in the Games 
of the XXVIIIth Olympiad in Athens, Greece.
  On August 21, 2004, Paul Foerster, and his teammate, Kevin Burnham, 
won the men's team gold medal for the United States in the 2004 Olympic 
Regatta for sailing in the 470 Class.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the entire 4th District of Texas, I would 
like to take this opportunity to commend and congratulate Paul for his 
outstanding performance and representation of the United States in the 
Games of the XXVIIIth Olympiad.

                          ____________________
```
