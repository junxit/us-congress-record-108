# REQUESTING THE SENATE TO RETURN TO THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES S. 1301

```
REQUESTING THE SENATE TO RETURN TO THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES S. 1301

  Mr. HUNTER. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 
842) requesting return of official papers on S. 1301, and ask for its 
immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 842

       Resolved, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives 
     request the Senate to return to the House the bill (S. 1301), 
     an Act to amend title 18, United States Code, to prohibit 
     video voyeurism in the special maritime and territorial 
     jurisdiction of the United States, and for other purposes.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
