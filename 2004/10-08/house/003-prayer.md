# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, You are our hope and our salvation. Your word guides our 
every step of the way. Your spirit of truth penetrates our very being 
and becomes the judgment of every word and deed of ours.
  Your truth shall set us free, O Lord. Sift through every complexity 
before us. Wherever You lead us may we find solace and peace. Bring us 
at last to that place where our hearts will rest in You, forever and 
ever. Amen.
```
