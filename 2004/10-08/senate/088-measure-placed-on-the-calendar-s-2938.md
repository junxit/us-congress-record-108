# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2938

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 2938

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2938

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the title of the bill 
for a second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2938) to grant a Federal charter to the National 
     American Indian Veterans, Incorporated.

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings on the 
measure at this time in order to place the bill on the calendar under 
the provisions of rule XIV.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
