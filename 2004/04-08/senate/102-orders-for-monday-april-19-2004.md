# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 19, 2004

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 19, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 1 p.m., Monday, 
April 19. I

[[Page S4072]]

further ask that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate begin a period of morning business with Senators limited 
to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
