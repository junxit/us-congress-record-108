# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 3108

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  At 4:01 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by one of its clerks, announced that the Speaker has signed the 
following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 3108. An act to amend the Employee Retirement Income 
     Security Act of 1974 and the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
     temporarily replace the 30-year Treasury rate with a rate 
     based on long-term corporate bonds for certain pension plan 
     funding requirements and other provisions, and for other 
     purposes.

  The enrolled bill was signed subsequently by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Stevens).

                          ____________________
```
