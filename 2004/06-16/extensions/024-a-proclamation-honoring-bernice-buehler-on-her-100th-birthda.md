# A PROCLAMATION HONORING BERNICE BUEHLER ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

```
     A PROCLAMATION HONORING BERNICE BUEHLER ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 16, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Bernice Buehler is celebrating her 100th birthday today; and
  Whereas, Bernice Buehler is a long-time active participant in the 
social and civic life of the community; and
  Whereas, Bernice Buehler has exemplified a love for her family and 
friends and must be commended for her life-long dedication to helping 
others in her community.
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District of Ohio in wishing Bernice Buehler a very happy 100th 
birthday.

                          ____________________
```
