# Text of House Amendment 16

**Speaking:** Weiner, Anthony D. (W000792) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4568

```
H.R. 4568

                         Offered By: Mr. Weiner

       Amendment No. 16: At the end of title III (page 136, after 
     line 4) add the following:

       Sec. __. The Secretary of the Interior shall, on and after 
     the commencement of the Republican National Convention in 
     2004, provide public access to the Statue of Liberty 
     substantially equivalent to the access provided before 
     September 11, 2001.
```
