# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4567

```
H.R. 4567

                        Offered By: Mr. Sherman

       Amendment No. 14: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for processing the importation of any article which 
     is the product of Iran and for which there is no duty.
```
