# Text of House Amendment 20

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 4567

```
H.R. 4567

                        Offered By: Mr. Sanders

       Amendment No. 20: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. __. The amounts made available under title I, and the 
     amount made available in title III under the item relating to 
     ``Office for State and Local Government-firefighter 
     assistance grants'' are reduced, on a prorated basis, and 
     increased by $146,000,000 and $146,000,000, respectively.
```
