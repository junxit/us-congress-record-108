# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY REGARDING DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE INVESTIGATIONS RELATING TO ALLEGATIONS OF VIOLATIONS OF THE GENEVA CONVENTIONS AT ABU GHRAIB PRISON IN IRAQ

```
  PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY REGARDING DEPARTMENT OF 
  DEFENSE INVESTIGATIONS RELATING TO ALLEGATIONS OF VIOLATIONS OF THE 
            GENEVA CONVENTIONS AT ABU GHRAIB PRISON IN IRAQ

  Mr. HUNTER, from the Committee on Armed Services, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 108-547) on the resolution (H. Res. 640) 
of inquiry requesting that the Secretary of Defense transmit to the 
House of Representatives before the expiration of the 14-day period 
beginning on the date of the adoption of this resolution any picture, 
photograph, video, communication, or report produced in conjunction 
with any completed Department of Defense investigation conducted by 
Major General Antonio M. Taguba relating to allegations of torture or 
allegations of violations of the Geneva Conventions of 1949 at Abu 
Ghraib prison in Iraq or any completed Department of Defense 
investigation relating to the abuse or alleged abuse of a prisoner of 
war or detainee by any civilian contractor working in Iraq who is 
employed on behalf of the Department of Defense, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
