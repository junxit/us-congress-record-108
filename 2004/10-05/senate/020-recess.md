# RECESS

**Speaking:** Mikulski, Barbara A. (M000702) [D-MD]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
having arrived, the Senate will stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 
p.m, with time to run against cloture.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:28, recessed until 2:16 p.m., and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Sununu).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
New Hampshire, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Ms. MIKULSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Voinovich). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
