# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 2273. An act to provide increased rail transportation 
     security; to the Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure.
       S. 2435. An act to permit Inspectors General to authorize

     Missing and Exploited Children, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Government Reform.

     the period of authorization for certain coastal wetland 
     conservation projects; to the Committee on Resources.
       S. 2882. An act to make the program for national criminal 
     history background checks for volunteer groups permanent; to 
     the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
