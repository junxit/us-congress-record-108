# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 3, H.R. 9, H.R. 10

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the numbers H.R. 3, H.R. 
9, and H.R. 10 shall be available during the second session of the 
108th Congress for assignment by the Speaker to such bills as he may 
designate.
```
