# COMMUNICATION FROM HON. NANCY PELOSI, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
[[Page H7949]]
        COMMUNICATION FROM HON. NANCY PELOSI, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                                  October 4, 2004.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker of the House, House of Representatives, Washington, 
         DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to section 214(a) of the Help 
     America Vote Act of 2002 (42 U.S.C. 15344), I hereby appoint 
     Douglas H. Palmer of Trenton, New Jersey to the Election 
     Assistance Commission Board of Advisors. Mr. Palmer will fill 
     the remainder of the term of Willie L. Brown, Jr.
           Best regards,
     Nancy Pelosi.

                          ____________________
```
