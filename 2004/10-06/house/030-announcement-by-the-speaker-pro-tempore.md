# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Isakson). The Chair must remind all 
Members that remarks in debate may not engage in personalities toward 
the President and the Vice President or the acknowledged candidates for 
those offices. Policies may be addressed in critical terms, but 
personal references of an offensive or accusatory nature are not 
proper.

                          ____________________
```
