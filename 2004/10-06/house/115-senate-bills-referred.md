# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 2796, S. 2864

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 2796. An act to clarify that service marks, collective 
     marks, and certification marks are entitled to the same 
     protections, rights, and privileges of trademarks; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
       S. 2864. An act to extend for eighteen months the period 
     for which chapter 12 of title 11, United States Code, is 
     reenacted; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
