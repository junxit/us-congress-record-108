# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BARBARA LEE

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 20, 2004

  Ms. LEE. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall Nos. 391, 392, and 393 on Monday, 
July 19, 2004, I was unavoidably detained due to inclement weather and 
delayed air service at Dulles International Airport, and unable to cast 
my vote.
  Had I been present, I would have voted the following: On rollcall 
391, the Viet Nam Human Rights Act of 2003, I would have voted ``no;'' 
on rollcall 392, concerning the importance of the distribution of food 
in schools to hungry or malnourished children around the world, I would 
have voted ``yea;'' and on rollcall 393, the Northern Uganda Crisis 
Response Act, I would have voted ``yea.''
```
