# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on March 23, 2004 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bill.

       H.R. 3724. To amend section 220 of the National Housing Act 
     to make a technical correction to restore allowable increases 
     in the maximum mortgage limits for FHA-insured mortgages for 
     multifamily housing projects to cover increased costs of 
     installing a solar energy system or residential energy 
     conservation measures.
```
