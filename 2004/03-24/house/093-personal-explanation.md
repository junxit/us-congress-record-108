                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                       Wednesday, March 24, 2004

  Mr. ROGERS of Michigan. Mr. Speaker, on the legislative day of 
Thursday, March 18, 2004, the House had a vote on H. Con. Res. 364, a 
resolution that would express Congress' recognition of more than 50 
years of strategic partnership between the United States and the 
Marshall Islands. On House rollcall vote No. 71, I was unavoidably 
detained. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea.''
