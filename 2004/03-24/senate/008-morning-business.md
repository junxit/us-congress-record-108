# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will be a 
period for the transaction of morning business for a full hour until 
10:30 a.m. or such time that may expire, with the Democratic leader or 
his designee in control of the first half of the time, and the majority 
leader or his designee in control of the remaining time.
  Who yields time?
  The Democratic leader is recognized. Does the Senator use his 
leadership time at this time?
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, that is my intention.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
