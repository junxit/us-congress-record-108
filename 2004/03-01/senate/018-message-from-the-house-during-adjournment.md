# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
               MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of January 7, 2003, the Secretary of 
the Senate, on February 27, 2004, during the adjournment of the Senate, 
received a message from the House of Representatives announcing that 
the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 3850. An act to provide an extension of highway, 
     highway safety, motor carrier safety, transit, and other 
     programs funded out of the Highway Trust Fund pending 
     enactment of a law reauthorizing the Transportation Equity 
     Act for the 21st Century.

  Under the authority of the order of January 7, 2003, the enrolled 
bill was signed by the President pro tempore (Mr. Stevens) on February 
27, 2004.

                          ____________________
```
