# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 93, S.Con.Res. 94

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed concurrent resolutions of the 
following titles in which the concurrence of the House is requested.

       S. Con. Res. 93. Concurrent resolution authorizing the use 
     of the rotunda of the Capitol by the Joint Congressional 
     Committee on Inaugural Ceremonies.
       S. Con. Res. 94. Concurrent resolution establishing the 
     Joint Congressional Committee on Inaugural Ceremonies.
```
