# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 339, PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY IN FOOD CONSUMPTION ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 339, H.Res. 552

```
[[Page H896]]
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 339, PERSONAL 
                 RESPONSIBILITY IN FOOD CONSUMPTION ACT

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 108-435) on the resolution (H. Res. 552) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 339) to prevent frivolous lawsuits 
against the manufacturers, distributors, or sellers of food or non-
alcoholic beverage products that comply with applicable statutory and 
regulatory requirements, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
