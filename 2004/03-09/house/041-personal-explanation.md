**Speaking:** Weldon, Dave (W000267) [R-FL]

                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. WELDON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, unfortunately, I was unable to be 
present for today's votes. Had I been present I would have voted

occurred in San Luis Obispo County, CA, on December 22, 2003 
(Representative Thomas); H. Res. 392--Congratulating the Detroit Shock 
for winning the 2003 Women's National Basketball Association

the San Jose Earthquakes for winning the 2003 Major League Soccer Cup 
(Representative Honda).
