# 100 HOURS AS PRESIDING OFFICER

```
                     100 HOURS AS PRESIDING OFFICER

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, on Thursday, March 4, 2004, Senator Saxby 
Chambliss reached his 100th hour of presiding over the U.S. Senate. As 
a presiding officer, his dedication and dependability are to be 
commended. It is with sincere appreciation that I announce Senator 
Chambliss as the most recent recipient of the Golden Gavel Award for 
the 108th Congress.

                          ____________________
```
