# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 414

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. SENSENBRENNER: Committee on the Judiciary. House 
     Concurrent Resolution 414. Resolution expressing the sense of 
     the Congress that, as Congress recognizes the 50th 
     anniversary of the Brown v. Board of Education decision, all 
     Americans are encouraged to observe this anniversary with a 
     commitment to continuing and building on the legacy of Brown 
     (Rept. 108-485). Referred to the House Calendar.

                          ____________________
```
