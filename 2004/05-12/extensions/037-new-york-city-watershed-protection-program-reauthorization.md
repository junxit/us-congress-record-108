# NEW YORK CITY WATERSHED PROTECTION PROGRAM REAUTHORIZATION

**Speaking:** Stupak, Bart (S001045) [D-MI]

```
       NEW YORK CITY WATERSHED PROTECTION PROGRAM REAUTHORIZATION

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                            HON. BART STUPAK

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Wednesday, May 5, 2004

  Mr. STUPAK. Mr. Speaker, I misspoke when on May 5, 2004, I identified 
perchlorate as the contaminant found at Camp Lejeune, North Carolina.

trichloroethylene and perchloroethylene.
```
