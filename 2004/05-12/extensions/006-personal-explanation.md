# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Peterson, John E. (P000263) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 398

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOHN E. PETERSON

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 11, 2004

  Mr. PETERSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, on Thursday, May 6, 2004, 
I was unavoidably detained and missed House rollcall vote No. 152 on H. 
Con. Res. 398, a bill expressing the concern of Congress over Iran's 
development of the means to produce nuclear weapons.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on H. Con. Res. 398, 
and ask unanimous consent that this be reflected in the appropriate 
place in the Record.

                          ____________________
```
