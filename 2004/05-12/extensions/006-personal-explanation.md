# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 12, 2004

  Mrs. MALONEY. Mr. Speaker, on May 11, 2004, I was unavoidably 
detained and missed rollcall votes numbered 153 and 154. Rollcall vote 
153 was on the motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 4299, a bill 
to designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located 
at 410 South Jackson Road in Edinburg, Texas, as the ``Dr. Miguel A. 
Nevarez Post Office Building.'' Rollcall vote 154 was on the motion to 
suspend the rules and pass H. Res. 622, a resolution supporting the 
goals and ideals of Peace Officers Memorial Day.
  Had I been present I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall votes 
numbered 153 and 154.

                          ____________________
```
