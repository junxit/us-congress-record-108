# A PROCLAMATION HONORING CARRIE MYTINGER

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
                A PROCLAMATION HONORING CARRIE MYTINGER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 2, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Carrie Mytinger was presented the 2003 Rookie of the Year 
Award by the Chillicothe Jaycees; and
  Whereas, Carrie Mytinger must be commended for her work with the 
Chillicothe community; and
  Whereas, Carrie Mytinger has demonstrated a commitment to community 
service through her work with various organizations, meeting challenges 
with enthusiasm and confidence;
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District in congratulating Carrie Mytinger for her outstanding 
accomplishment.

                          ____________________
```
