# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 29, 2004

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 29, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until 1 p.m. on Monday, March 29. I further 
ask, following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then begin consideration of H.R. 4, the welfare reform 
reauthorization bill as provided under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
