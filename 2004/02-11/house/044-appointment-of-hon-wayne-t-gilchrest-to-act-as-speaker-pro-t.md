# APPOINTMENT OF HON. WAYNE T. GILCHREST TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION THROUGH FEBRUARY 24, 2004

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. WAYNE T. GILCHREST TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO 
   SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION THROUGH FEBRUARY 24, 2004

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:


                                               Washington, DC,

                                                February 11, 2004.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Wayne T. Gilchrest to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through February 24, 2004.
                                                   Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
