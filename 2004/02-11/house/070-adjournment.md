# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Strickland, Ted (S001004) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 361

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. STRICKLAND. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Accordingly, pursuant to the previous order of the House of today, 
the House stands adjourned until 4 p.m. on Saturday, February 14, 2004, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 361, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  Thereupon (at 9 o'clock and 50 minutes p.m.), pursuant to the 
previous order of the House of today, the House adjourned until 4 p.m. 
on Saturday, February 14, 2004, unless it sooner has received a message 
from the Senate transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent 
Resolution 361, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant 
to that concurrent resolution.

                          ____________________
```
