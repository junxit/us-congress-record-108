# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE TO SATURDAY, FEBRUARY 14, 2004

**Speaking:** Hoekstra, Peter (H000676) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 361

```
  CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE TO SATURDAY, FEBRUARY 14, 2004

  Mr. HOEKSTRA. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 4 p.m. on Saturday, 
February 14, 2004, unless it sooner has received a message from the 
Senate transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 361, 
in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that 
concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Beauprez). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Michigan?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
