# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3575

```
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3575

  Mr. ROSS. Mr. Speaker, today I learned that I have been listed as a 
cosponsor of H.R. 3575, something I was not aware of and I did not ask

as a cosponsor of H.R. 3575.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Arkansas?
```
