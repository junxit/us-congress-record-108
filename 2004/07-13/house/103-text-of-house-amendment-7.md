# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Hefley, Joel (H000444) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 4818

```
H.R. 4818

                         Offered By: Mr. Hefley

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:


               reduction of discretionary appropriations

       Sec. __. Total appropriations made in this Act (other than 
     appropriations required to be made by a provision of law) are 
     hereby reduced by $193,860,000.
```
