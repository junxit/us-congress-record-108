# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Deal, Nathan (D000168) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4818

```
H.R. 4818

                    Offered By: Mr. Deal of Georgia

       Amendment No. 1: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:


          governments that do not permit certain extraditions

       Sec. 576. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to provide assistance to the government of any 
     country that does not permit the extradition to the United 
     States, for trial or sentencing in the United States, of 
     individuals suspected of committing criminal offenses for 
     which the maximum penalty is life imprisonment without the 
     possibility of parole, or a lesser term of imprisonment.
```
