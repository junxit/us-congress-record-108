# Text of House Amendment 15

**Speaking:** Tancredo, Thomas G. (T000458) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 4766

```
H.R. 4766

                        Offered By Mr. Tancredo

       Amendment No. 15: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act under 
     the Heading ``Food Stamp Program'' may be expended in 
     contravention of 8 U.S.C. 1183a.
```
