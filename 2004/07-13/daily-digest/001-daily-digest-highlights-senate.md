  A unanimous-consent agreement was reached providing for further

resolution at approximately 10 a.m., on Wednesday, July 14, 2004; that

  E-Government Act Amendment--Agreement: A unanimous-consent agreement 
was reached providing that the Senate request the return from the House 
of Representatives the papers with respect to H.R. 1303, to amend the

Judicial Conference, and that the Senate action of July 9, 2004 on 
passage of the measure be vitiated, and the bill returned to the 
Committee on Governmental Affairs for appropriate action.
