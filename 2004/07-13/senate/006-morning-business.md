# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business, with the first 
half of the time under the control of the minority leader or his 
designee and the second half of the time under the control of the 
majority leader or his designee.
```
