# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 1303

**Speaking:** Allard, Wayne (A000109) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 1303

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 1303

  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
request the return from the House of Representatives the papers with 
respect to H.R. 1303, that the Senate action on that measure be 
vitiated, and that the bill be returned to the Committee on 
Governmental Affairs for appropriate action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
