# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, APRIL 26, 2004, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, APRIL 27, 2004

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

```
ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, APRIL 26, 2004, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, 
                             APRIL 27, 2004

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today it adjourn to meet at noon on Monday, April 26, 2004; 
and further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 
12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, April 27, for morning hour debate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
