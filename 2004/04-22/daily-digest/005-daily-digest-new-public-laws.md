# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** S. 2057

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D373)
  S. 2057, to require the Secretary of Defense to reimburse members of 
the United States Armed Forces for certain transportation expenses 
incurred by the members in connection with leave under the Central 
Command Rest and Recuperation Leave Program before the program was 
expanded to include domestic travel. Signed on April 22, 2004. (Public 
Law 108-220)
```
