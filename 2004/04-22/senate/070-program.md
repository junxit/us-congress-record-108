# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. On Monday at 2 p.m. the Senate will resume consideration 
of the motion to proceed to the Internet tax bill. This is a piece of 
legislation that was on the floor for debate only last November. 
However, minutes ago I was forced to file cloture in order to bring the 
bill back for consideration.
  The cloture vote on the motion to proceed will occur at 5:30 p.m. on 
Monday, and that will be the next rollcall vote. It is my hope cloture 
will be invoked and we can move forward with debate on the bill.
```
