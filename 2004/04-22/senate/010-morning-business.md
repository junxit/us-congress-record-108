# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there is 
a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 minutes, with 
the first 30 minutes under the time of the majority leader or his 
designee and the final 30 minutes under the time of the Democratic 
leader or his designee.
```
