# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to a period for morning business with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from New Mexico.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, will the Senator from New Mexico yield to me 
for a question?
  Mr. DOMENICI. Yes, I yield.
  Mr. REID. Senator Landrieu has been waiting for the vote to end. She 
has to pick up her children, but she first wants to speak about her 
children for a couple of minutes. Would the Senator allow her 2 minutes 
prior to beginning his speech?
  Mr. DOMENICI. Of course.
  Ms. LANDRIEU. I thank the Senator from New Mexico, and I thank my 
colleague from Nevada.

                          ____________________
```
