# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on April 22, 2004, he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.R. 1274. To direct the Administrator of General Services 
     to convey to Fresno County, California, the existing Federal 
     courthouse in that county.
       H.R. 2489. To provide for the distribution of judgment 
     funds to the Cowlitz Indian Tribe.
       H.R. 3118. To designate the Orville Wright Federal Building 
     and the Wilbur Wright Federal Building in Washington, 
     District of Columbia.

                          ____________________
```
