# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON STUDENT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

```
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON STUDENT FINANCIAL 
                               ASSISTANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Bishop of Utah). Pursuant to section 491 
of the Higher Education Act, the order of the House on December 8, 
2003, and upon the recommendation of the minority leader, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following member on the part 
of the House to the Advisory Committee on Student Financial Assistance 
for a 3-year term:
  Mr. Robert Shireman, Oakland, California.
```
