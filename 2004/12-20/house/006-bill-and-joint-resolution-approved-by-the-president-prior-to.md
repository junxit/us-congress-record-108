# BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT PRIOR TO SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

```
 BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT PRIOR TO SINE DIE 
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The President, prior to sine die adjournment of the 2d Session, 108th 
Congress, notified the Clerk of the House that on the following date, 
he had approved and signed a bill and joint resolution of the following 
titles:

           December 3, 2004:
       H.R. 1350. An act to reauthorize the Individuals with 
     Disabilities Education Act, and for other purposes.
       H.J. Res. 115. Joint resolution making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2005, and for other 
     purposes.

                          ____________________
```
