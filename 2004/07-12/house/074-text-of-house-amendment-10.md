# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 4766

```
H.R. 4766

                         Offered By: Ms. Kaptur

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec.__. None of the funds made available in this Act may be 
     used to provide credits or credit guarantees for agricultural 
     commodities provided for use in Iraq in violation of 
     subsection (e) or (f) of section 202 of the Agricultural 
     Trade Act of 1978 (7 U.S.C. 5622).
```
