                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. EDDIE BERNICE JOHNSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, unfortunately, I

I been in attendance I would have made the following votes:

Appropriations Act for FY05. Had I been in attendance, I would have 
vote ``aye.''
  Vote on the Hefley amendment to H.R. 4755--Legislative Branch 
Appropriations Act for FY05. Had I been in attendance, I would have 
voted ``no.''
  Vote on the Motion to Recommit--4755--Legislative Branch 
Appropriations Act for FY05. Had I been in attendance, I would have 
vote ``aye.''
  Vote on passage of H.R. 4755--Legislative Branch Appropriations Act 
for FY05. Had I been in attendance, I would have vote ``aye.''
