# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 40

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, today the Senate will resume 
consideration of the motion to proceed to S.J. Res. 40, the Federal 
marriage amendment. Discussions continue as to how best to proceed to 
the consideration of this constitutional amendment. While those 
negotiations continue, Senators are encouraged to come to the floor to 
speak on the amendment.
  Friday, a number of Members came to the floor to talk on this issue, 
and we expect to resume the robust debate today. There will be no 
rollcall votes during today's session.
```
