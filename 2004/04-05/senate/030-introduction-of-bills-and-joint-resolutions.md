# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2280, S. 2281, S. 2282, S. 2283

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. STEVENS:
       S. 2280. A bill to establish a coordinated national ocean 
     exploration program within the National Oceanic and 
     Atmospheric Administration; to the Committee on Commerce, 
     Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. SUNUNU:
       S. 2281. A bill to provide a clear and unambiguous 
     structure for the jurisdictional and regulatory treatment for 
     the offering or provision of voice-over-Internet-protocol 
     applications, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. KENNEDY (for himself, Mr. Baucus, and Mr. 
             Daschle):
       S. 2282. A bill to amend the Employee Retirement Income 
     Security Act of 1974 and the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
     temporarily replace the 30-year Treasury rate with a rate 
     based on long-term corporate bonds for certain pension plan 
     funding requirements and other provisions, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. GREGG (for himself and Mr. Baucus):
       S. 2283. A bill to extend Federal funding for operation of 
     State high risk health insurance pools; to the Committee on 
     Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
```
