# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      committee on armed services

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Armed Services be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on May 7, 2004, at 11:45 a.m., in open session, to receive 
testimony on allegations of mistreatment of Iraqi prisoners.
```
