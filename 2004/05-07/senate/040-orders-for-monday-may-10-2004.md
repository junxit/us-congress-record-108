# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 10, 2004

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 10, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, 
May 10. I further ask that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed to have expired, the Journal of the proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, the Senate then begin a period of morning 
business, with Senators to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
