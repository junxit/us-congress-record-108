# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Rahall, Nick J., II (R000011) [D-WV]

**Measures:** H.R. 4568

```
H.R. 4568

                         Offered By: Mr. Rahall

       Amendment No. 1: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new title:

                 TITLE V--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 501. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to adversely affect

[[Page H4173]]

     the physical integrity of Indian Sacred Sites on Federal 
     lands (as such terms are defined in Executive Order 13007, 
     dated May 24, 1996).
```
