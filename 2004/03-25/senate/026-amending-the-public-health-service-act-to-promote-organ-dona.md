# AMENDING THE PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICE ACT TO PROMOTE ORGAN DONATION

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3926

```
    AMENDING THE PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICE ACT TO PROMOTE ORGAN DONATION

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 3926, which is at the 
desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3926) to amend the Public Health Service Act 
     to promote organ donation, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
read the third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon 
the table, and that any statements related to the bill be printed in 
the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 3926) was read the third time and passed.

                          ____________________
```
