# AMENDMENT PROCESS FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3550, TRANSPORTATION EQUITY ACT: A LEGACY FOR USERS

```
AMENDMENT PROCESS FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3550, TRANSPORTATION EQUITY 
                        ACT: A LEGACY FOR USERS

  Mr. DREIER. Mr. Speaker, the Committee on Rules may meet the week of 
March 29 to grant a rule which could limit the amendment process for 
floor consideration of H.R. 3550, the Transportation Equity Act: A 
Legacy For Users. The Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure 
ordered the bill reported on March 24.
  Any Member wishing to offer an amendment should submit 55 copies of 
the amendment and one copy of a brief explanation of the amendment to 
the Rules Committee in room H-312 of the Capitol by 10 a.m. on Tuesday, 
March 30. Members should draft their amendments to the text of the bill 
as reported by Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure which is 
available for their review on the Web sites of both the Committee on 
Transportation and Infrastructure as well as the Committee on Rules.
  Members should use the Office of Legislative Counsel to ensure that 
their amendments are drafted in the most appropriate format. Members 
are also advised to check with the Office of the Parliamentarian to be 
certain that their amendments comply with the rules of the House.
```
