# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Res. 23, S.Res. 110

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       261. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of Tennessee, relative to Senate Resolution No. 110 
     urging the United States Congress and the President of the 
     United States to restore funding for the Juvenile 
     Accountability Block Grant; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
       262. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of New Hampshire, relative to House Resolution 23 
     memorializing the President of the United States and the 
     United States Congress to amend federal selective service and 
     immigration laws to grant the right of citizenship to all 
     immigrants honorably discharged from the United States 
     military; jointly to the Committees on the Judiciary and 
     Armed Services.

                          ____________________
```
