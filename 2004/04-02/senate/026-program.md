# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. On Monday, the Senate will be in a period for the 
transaction of morning business throughout the day. There will be no 
rollcall votes on Monday, but Senators are encouraged to come to the 
floor to deliver morning business statements if they have any.
  As a reminder, earlier today the majority leader propounded a 
unanimous consent request that would have allowed us to take up and 
begin debate on S. 2207, the Pregnancy and Trauma Care Access 
Protection Act of 2004. There was an objection to that request, and the 
majority leader was forced to file cloture on the motion to proceed.
  The cloture vote on the motion to proceed to S. 2207 will occur on 
Wednesday of next week at 2:15, and that vote will be the next rollcall 
vote.

                          ____________________
```
