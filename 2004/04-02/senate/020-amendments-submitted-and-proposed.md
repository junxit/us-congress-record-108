# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

```
                   AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

     Leahy, Mr. DeWine, and Mr. Kohl)) proposed an amendment to 
     the bill H.R. 1086, to encourage the development and
```
