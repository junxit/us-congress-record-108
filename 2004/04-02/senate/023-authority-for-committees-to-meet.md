# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


           subcommittee on emerging threats and capabilities

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and Capabilities of the Committee on 
Armed Services be authorized to meet during the session of the Senate 
on April 2, 2004, at 9:30 a.m., in open and closed session to receive 
testimony on the Department of Defense Counter Narcotics Program in 
review of the Defense authorization request for fiscal year 2005.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
