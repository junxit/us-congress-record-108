# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. TANCREDO. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Accordingly, pursuant to the previous order of the House of today, 
the House stands adjourned until 4 p.m. on Tuesday, April 6, 2004, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 404, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  Thereupon (at 4 o'clock and 11 minutes p.m.), pursuant to the 
previous order of the House of today, the House adjourned until 4 p.m. 
on Tuesday, April 6, 2004, unless it sooner has received a message from 
the Senate transmitting its adoption of the House Concurrent Resolution 
404, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that 
concurrent resolution.
```
