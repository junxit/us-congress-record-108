# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE TO TUESDAY, APRIL 6, 2004

```
     CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE TO TUESDAY, APRIL 6, 2004

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 4 p.m. on Tuesday, April 6, 2004, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 404, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
```
