# 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FLORENCE WOMAN'S CLUB

```
             50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FLORENCE WOMAN'S CLUB

● Mr. BUNNING. Mr. President, I rise to congratulate the 
Florence Woman's Club of Florence, KY on the recent celebration of its 
50th anniversary.
  The Florence Woman's Club was founded on April 20, 1954. Its goal was 
simply to make the city of Florence a better place to live. The causes 
that the club has lent its time to are almost too numerous to mention. 
They have been involved in raising money to fight cancer, working at 
local veterans' hospitals, and helping with the preservation of 
historic buildings in the area, to name just a few.
  The citizens of northern Kentucky are fortunate to have the services 
of the Florence Woman's Club. This organization's example of 
dedication, hard work and compassion should be an inspiration to all 
throughout the Commonwealth of Kentucky.
  They have my most sincere appreciation for this work, and I look 
forward to their continued service to Kentucky. I have no doubt that 
they will be just as productive in their next 50 years as they have 
been in their first 50. Congratulations.●

                          ____________________
```
