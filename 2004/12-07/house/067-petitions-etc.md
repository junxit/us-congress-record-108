# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       130. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Dale W. 
     Hogeland, a Citizen of Norwell, MA, relative to a notice of 
     fraud, and petitioning the United States Congress for redress 
     of grievances; to the Committee on Ways and Means.
       131. Also, a petition of the Legislature of Rockland 
     County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 492 of 2004 
     memorializing the United States Congress to introduce and 
     pass appropriate legislation which seeks to provide 
     comprehensive service to help non-traditional students 
     complete their college degrees; jointly to the Committees on 
     Education and the Workforce and Ways and Means.
```
