# IRMA RANGEL POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                    IRMA RANGEL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4829) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 103 East Kleberg in Kingsville, Texas, as the 
``Irma Rangel Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered to be 
engrossed for a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
