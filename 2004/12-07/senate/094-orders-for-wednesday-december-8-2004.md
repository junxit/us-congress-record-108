# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 8, 2004

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 8, 2004

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Wednesday, December 8. I further ask unanimous consent that 
following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, and the Senate then 
proceed to the conference report to accompany S. 2845, the intelligence 
reform bill, if received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
