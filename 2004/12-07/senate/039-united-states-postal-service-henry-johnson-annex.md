# UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE HENRY JOHNSON ANNEX

```
            UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE HENRY JOHNSON ANNEX

  The bill (H.R. 480) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 747 Broadway in Albany, New York, as the 
``United States Postal Service Henry Johnson Annex,'' was considered, 
ordered to be engrossed for a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
