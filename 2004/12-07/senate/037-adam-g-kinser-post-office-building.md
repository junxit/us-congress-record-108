# ADAM G. KINSER POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  ADAM G. KINSER POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4807) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 140 Sacramento Street in Rio Vista, 
California, as the ``Adam G. Kinser Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to be engrossed for a third reading, read the third 
time, and passed.
```
