# EARL B. GILLIAM/IMPERIAL AVENUE POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5364

```
          EARL B. GILLIAM/IMPERIAL AVENUE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5364) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 5505 Stevens Way in San Diego, California, as 
the ``Earl B. Gilliam/Imperial Avenue Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to be engrossed for a third reading, read the third
```
