# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 8, 2004

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 8, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until 9:45 a.m., on Tuesday, June 8. I 
further ask that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate then begin a period for morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each. I further ask that the 
Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 for the weekly policy luncheons to 
meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Burns). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
