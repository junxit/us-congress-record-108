# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:45 TOMORROW

```
                    ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:45 TOMORROW

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the provisions of S. Res. 371, as a further mark of 
respect to the memory of Ronald Wilson Reagan.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 6:03 p.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, June 8, 2004, at 9:45 a.m.
```
