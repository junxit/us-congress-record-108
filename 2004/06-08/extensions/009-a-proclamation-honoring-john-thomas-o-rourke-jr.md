# A PROCLAMATION HONORING JOHN THOMAS O'ROURKE, JR.

```
           A PROCLAMATION HONORING JOHN THOMAS O'ROURKE, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 8, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, John and Carolyn O'Rourke are celebrating the arrival of 
their son, John Thomas O'Rourke, Jr.; and
  Whereas, John Thomas O'Rourke, Jr. was born in the second day of 
April, 2004 and weighed seven pounds and twelve and a half ounces; and
  Whereas, John and Carolyn O'Rourke are proud to welcome their new son 
into their home; and
  Whereas, John Thomas O'Rourke, Jr. will be a blessed addition to his 
family, bringing love, joy, and happiness for many years to come;
  Therefore, I join with members of Congress and Congressional Staff in 
celebrating with John and Carolyn O'Rourke and wishing John Thomas 
O'Rourke, Jr. a very Happy Birthday.

                          ____________________
```
