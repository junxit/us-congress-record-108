         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

     the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. SENSENBRENNER: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 1731. A 
     bill to amend title 18, United States Code, to establish 
     penalties for aggravated identity theft, and for other 
     purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 108-528). Referred to the
