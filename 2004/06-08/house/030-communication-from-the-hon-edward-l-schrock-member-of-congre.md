# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HON. EDWARD L. SCHROCK, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM THE HON. EDWARD L. SCHROCK, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Rohrabacher) laid before the House the 
following communication from the Honorable Edward L. Schrock, Member of 
Congress:


                                Congress of the United States,

                                                     June 3, 2004.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to formally notify you, pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives, 
     that I have been served with a criminal subpoena for 
     documents, issued by the United States District Court for the 
     Southern District of Illinois.
       After consulting with the Office of General Counsel, I have 
     determined that compliance with the subpoena is consistent 
     with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                Edward L. Schrock,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
