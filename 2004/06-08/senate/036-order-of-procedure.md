# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. COLEMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 11:30 
a.m. tomorrow, the Senate proceed to a resolution introduced by Senator 
Frist, the text of which is at the desk, relative to the death of 
former President Reagan. I further ask the Senate then proceed to a 
vote on the resolution's adoption; provided that there be no amendments 
in order to the resolution or preamble, and no intervening action or 
debate prior to the vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
