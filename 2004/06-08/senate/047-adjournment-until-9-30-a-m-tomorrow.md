# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Coleman, Norm (C001057) [R-MN]

**Measures:** S.Res. 371

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. COLEMAN. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the provisions of S. Res. 371 as a mark of further 
respect for President Ronald Wilson Reagan.

Wednesday, June 9, 2004, at 9:30 a.m.
```
