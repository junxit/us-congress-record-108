# A PROCLAMATION HONORING JEFF FREEMAN AND NICOLE PALYA

```
         A PROCLAMATION HONORING JEFF FREEMAN AND NICOLE PALYA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 18, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Jeff Freeman and Nicole Palya have decided to dedicate their 
lives to each other; and
  Whereas, Jeff and Nicole have shown the love and commitment necessary 
to live a long and beautiful life together; and
  Whereas, Jeff and Nicole have chosen to share their special day with 
friends and family;
  Therefore, I join with the Members of Congress and their staff in 
congratulating Jeff Freeman and Nicole Palya on the occasion of their 
marriage.
```
