# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, the quorum call is 
rescinded and the Senate stands in recess subject to the call of the 
Chair.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 7:04 p.m., recessed subject to the call of 
the Chair and reassembled at 7:06 p.m. when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mr. Talent).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
Missouri, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
