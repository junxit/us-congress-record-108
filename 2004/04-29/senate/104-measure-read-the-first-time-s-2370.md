  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand that S. 2370 is at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.

       A bill (S. 2370) to amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of
