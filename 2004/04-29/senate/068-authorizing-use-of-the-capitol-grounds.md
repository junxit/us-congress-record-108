# AUTHORIZING USE OF THE CAPITOL GROUNDS

```
                 AUTHORIZING USE OF THE CAPITOL GROUNDS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 376.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 376) authorizing the 
     use of the Capitol Grounds for the Greater Washington Soap 
     Box Derby.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent 
resolution be agreed to, that the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statement relating to the resolution be printed in 
the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 376) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
