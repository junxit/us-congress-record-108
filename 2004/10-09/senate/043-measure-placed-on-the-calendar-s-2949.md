# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2949

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2949

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
due for its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the second 
time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2949) to amend the Low-Income Home Energy 
     Assistance Act of 1981 to reauthorize the Act, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings on the 
measure at this time in order to place the bill on the calendar under 
the provisions of rule XIV.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be placed on 
the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
