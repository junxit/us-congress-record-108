# APPOINTMENT OF HON. FRANK R. WOLF OR HON. TOM DAVIS OF VIRGINIA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH NOVEMBER 16, 2004

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. FRANK R. WOLF OR HON. TOM DAVIS OF VIRGINIA TO ACT 
  AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS 
                       THROUGH NOVEMBER 16, 2004

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                              The Speaker's Rooms,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, October 9, 2004.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Frank R. Wolf or, if he is 
     not available to perform this duty, the Honorable Tom Davis 
     to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills

[[Page 22931]]

     and joint resolutions through November 16, 2004.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
