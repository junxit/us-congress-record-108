# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 17, 2004, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, MAY 18, 2004

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

```
                              {time}  1700
 ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 17, 2004, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, 
                              MAY 18, 2004

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 12:30 p.m. on Monday, May 17, 
2004, for morning hour debate and further, when the House adjourns on 
that day, it adjourn to meet at 9 a.m. Tuesday, May 18, for morning 
hour debate as though after May 31, 2004, thereafter to resume its 
session at 10 a.m.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaTourette). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
