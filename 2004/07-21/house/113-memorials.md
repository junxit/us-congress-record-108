# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Res. 47, H.Con.Res. 195, H.R. 677, S. 1925, H.R. 3619

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       410. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature of 
     the State of Hawaii, relative to House Concurrent Resolution 
     No. 195 supporting the Employee Free Choice Act (S. 1925 and 
     H.R. 3619) and memorializing the United States Congress to 
     pass this measure; to the Committee on Education and the 
     Workforce.
       411. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Hawaii, relative to House Resolution No. 47 
     memorializing the Congress of the United States to pass 
     federal legislation to provide access to safe, affordable 
     prescription drugs by allowing purchase of prescription drugs 
     from Canada and other countries that meet federal safety 
     requirements and memorializing the Secretary of Health and 
     Human Services to support and implement reimportation; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       412. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Hawaii, relative to House Concurrent Resoultion No. 250 
     memorializing the President and Congress of the United States 
     to support H.R. 677 extending veterans' benefits to Filipino 
     veterans; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
