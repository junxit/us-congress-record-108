# PERMITTING 5-MINUTE VOTES AFTER FIRST VOTE IN NEXT SERIES NOTWITHSTANDING INTERVENING PROCEEDINGS

```
       PERMITTING 5-MINUTE VOTES AFTER FIRST VOTE IN NEXT SERIES 
                NOTWITHSTANDING INTERVENING PROCEEDINGS

  Mr. TIAHRT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during the next 
series of postponed votes, ensuing votes after the first vote may be 5-
minute votes notwithstanding intervening proceedings attending the 
swearing in of the new Member-elect from North Carolina.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Kansas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
