# CONGRATULATING THE TYLER STREET BOYS TRACK TEAM

```
            CONGRATULATING THE TYLER STREET BOYS TRACK TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MARTIN FROST

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 19, 2004

  Mr. FROST. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Tyler Street 
Boys Track Team of Dallas, on their 2-A State Title. The Crusaders 
upset the two-time defending champions, Muenster Sacred Heart, to 
capture the title.
  The Tyler Street Relay Teams won both the 4x200 and 4x400 relays, no 
doubt the result of hard work throughout the season. The Tyler Street 
Track Team also finished in the top three positions in the field 
events, including a first place finish in the shot put.
  This year's championship team includes members: Bobby Lewis, Darrian 
Sanders, Daunte Bornen, Jared Carter, Cshammah Reed, E.J. Rusher, 
Richard Solis, Britt Jones, Austin Alburtis, Freddy Holland, Nicholas

[[Page 10443]]

Wright, Cjabrione Reed, and Joshua Carter. This year's team is coached 
by Walter Favors.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate the Tyler Street Track Team on their 
championship and also on a great season.

                          ____________________
```
