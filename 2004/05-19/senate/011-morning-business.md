# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business up to the hour of 
11:30 a.m., with the first half of the time under the control of the 
Democratic leader or his designee, and the second half of the time 
under the control of the majority leader or his designee.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, on behalf of Senator Daschle, I yield 10 
minutes to Senator Stabenow, 10 minutes to Senator Murray, 10 minutes 
to Senator Durbin, and 10 minutes to Senator Wyden.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Thomas). Without objection, it is so 
ordered. The Senator from Michigan is recognized.
```
