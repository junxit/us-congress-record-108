# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2348

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2348

  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, I understand that S. 2348 is at the 
desk and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for a first time 
by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2348) to extend the Internet Tax Freedom Act.

  Mr. ALEXANDER. I now ask for its second reading, and in order to 
place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
a second time on the next legislative day.
```
