# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 27, 2004

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 27, 2004

  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent when the Senate completes its business today, it 
adjourn until 9:45 a.m. on Tuesday, April 27. I further ask that 
following the prayer and the pledge, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, and following 
the time for the two leaders, the Senate begin a period of morning 
business for up to 60 minutes, with the first half of the time under 
the control of the Democratic leader or his designee and the second 
half of the time under the control of the Republican leader or his 
designee; provided that following that 60-minute period, the Senate 
resume consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 150, the Internet 
tax bill, as under the previous order. I further ask consent that the 
Senate recess from 12:45 until 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party 
luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
