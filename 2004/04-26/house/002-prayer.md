# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, with the right amount of sunlight and rain You provide the 
greening of earth. With just enough daytime and nighttime You measure 
the activity and the rest of Your people.
  As a new week unfolds before us, may each person find an inner 
balance that speaks of health and holiness. May every day be a response 
to Your word and prove to be an opportunity to correct our faults and 
grow in virtue to work together for justice, and sow seeds of peace.
  In us and through us may Your holy will be revealed today, tomorrow 
and always. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
