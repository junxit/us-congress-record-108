# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will reconvene to

[[Page 24285]]

consider the Omnibus appropriations conference report. It is expected 
that the conference report will be filed tonight and that the House 
will begin consideration of that measure Saturday morning. We will then 
return to business at 11 a.m. with the hope that the conference report 
will be available shortly thereafter.
  In addition, the Senate will consider the intelligence reform 
conference report if that is available. Senators should expect votes 
tomorrow, and we will notify Members as to when the first vote is 
expected.
```
