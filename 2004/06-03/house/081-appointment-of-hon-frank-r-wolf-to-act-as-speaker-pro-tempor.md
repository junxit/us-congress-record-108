# APPOINTMENT OF HON. FRANK R. WOLF TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 8, 2004

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. FRANK R. WOLF TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN 
       ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 8, 2004

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, June 3, 2004.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Frank R. Wolf to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through June 8, 2004.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
