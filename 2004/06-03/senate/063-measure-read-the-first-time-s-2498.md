# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2498

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2498

  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, I understand that S. 2498 is at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2498) to provide for a 10-year extension of the 
     assault weapons ban.

  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading, and in 
order to place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule 
XIV, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
