# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. RES. 581, SENSE OF HOUSE REGARDING RULES OF COMPENSATION FOR CIVILIAN EMPLOYEES AND MEMBERS OF THE UNIFORMED SERVICES OF THE UNITED STATES

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. RES. 581, SENSE 
  OF HOUSE REGARDING RULES OF COMPENSATION FOR CIVILIAN EMPLOYEES AND 
         MEMBERS OF THE UNIFORMED SERVICES OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 108-454) on the resolution (H. Res. 585) providing 
for consideration of the resolution (H. Res. 581) expressing the sense 
of the House of Representative regarding rates of compensation for 
civilian employees and members of the uniformed services of the United 
States, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
