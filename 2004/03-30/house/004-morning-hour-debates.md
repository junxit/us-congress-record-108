# MORNING HOUR DEBATES

```
                          MORNING HOUR DEBATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of March 
29, 2004, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists submitted by 
the majority and minority leaders for morning hour debates.
  The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with each 
party limited to 25 minutes and each Member, other than the majority 
and minority leaders and the minority whip, limited to 5 minutes each, 
but in no event shall debate continue beyond 9:50 a.m.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Ohio (Mr. Brown) for 5 
minutes.

                          ____________________
```
