# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2250

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2250

  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk that is due for second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  The clerk will read the title of the bill for the second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2250) to extend the Temporary Extended 
     Unemployment Compensation Act of 2002, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. ALEXANDER. I object to further proceedings on the bill at this 
time on behalf of the leader in order to place the bill on the calendar 
under the provisions of rule XIV.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard. The bill will be 
placed on the calendar pursuant to rule XIV.

                          ____________________
```
