# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 3723, H.R. 3917

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 3723. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 8135 Forest Lane in Dallas, 
     Texas, as the ``Vaughn Gross Post Office Building''; to the 
     Committee on Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 3917. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 695 Marconi Boulevard in 
     Copiague, New York, as the ``Maxine S. Postal United States 
     Post Office''; to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.
```
