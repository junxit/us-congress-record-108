# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 7, 2004

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** S. 2400

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 7, 2004

  Mr. SESSIONS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 1:30 p.m., on 
Monday, June 7. I further ask consent that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then begin a period of morning 
business until 2:30 p.m., with the time equally divided between the two 
leaders or their designees; provided that at 2:30 p.m. the Senate 
resume consideration of Calendar No. 503, S. 2400, the Department of 
Defense authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
