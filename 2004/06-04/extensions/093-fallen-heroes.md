# FALLEN HEROES

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
                             FALLEN HEROES

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 20, 2004

  Mr. CARTER. Mr. Speaker, as we approach Memorial Day, we should take 
a moment to remember the young men and women who are fighting bravely 
for the freedom and democracy of this nation and others. At the same 
time we must remember the families of those men and women who have made 
the ultimate sacrifice.
  All Texans fighting for a just cause will forever be ingrained in my 
mind, especially those who have become our fallen heroes:
  Spc. Zeferino E. Colunga, Belville, Texas.
  Pfc. James D. Parker, Bryan, Texas.
  2nd Lt. Jonathan D. Rozier, Katy, Texas.

                          ____________________
```
