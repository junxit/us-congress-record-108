# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 114, S. 1233, S. 2363

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed a bill and a concurrent resolution 
of the following titles in which the concurrence of the House is 
requested:

       S. 2363. An act to revise and extend the Boys and Girls 
     Clubs of America.
       S. Con. Res. 114. Concurrent resolution concerning the 
     importance of the distribution of food in schools to hungry 
     or malnourished children around the world.

  The message also announced that the Senate agrees to the amendment of 
the House of Representatives to the bill (S. 1233) entitled ``An Act to 
authorize assistance for the National Great Blacks in Wax Museum and 
Justice Learning Center.''.

                          ____________________
```
