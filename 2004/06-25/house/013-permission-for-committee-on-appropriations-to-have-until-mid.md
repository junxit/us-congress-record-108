```
   PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT 
 FRIDAY, JULY 2, 2004, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON LEGISLATIVE BRANCH 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2005
```
