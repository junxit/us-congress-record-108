# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

   Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on June 25, 2004 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

        H.J. Res 97. Approving the renewal of import restrictions 
     contained in the Burmese Freedom and Democracy Act of 2003.
        H.R. 884. To provide for the use and distribution of the 
     funds awarded to the Western Shoshone identifiable group 
     under Indian Claims Commission Docket Numbers 326-A-1, 326-A-
     3, and 326-K, and for other purposes.
        H.R. 2751. To provide new human capital flexibilities with 
     respect to the GAO, and for other purposes.
```
