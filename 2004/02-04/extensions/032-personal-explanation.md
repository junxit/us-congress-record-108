# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gutierrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. LUIS V. GUTIERREZ

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 4, 2004

  Mr. GUTIERREZ. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably absent from this 
Chamber, on January 21, 2004. I would like the record to show that, had 
I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall vote No. 4.
  I was also unavoidably absent from this Chamber on January 28, 2004. 
I would like the record to show that, had I been present, I would have 
voted ``yea'' on rollcall Nos. 8, 9 and 11. I would also like the 
record to show that I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall 10.
```
