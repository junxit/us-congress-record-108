Commercial Space Launch Amendments Act of 2004: The House passed H.R. 3752, 
to promote the development of the emerging commercial human space flight 
industry, to extend the liability indemnification regime for the commercial 
space transportation industry, to authorize appropriations for the Office of 
the Associate Administrator for Commercial Space Transportation, by a yea-
and-nay vote of 402 yeas to 1 nay, Roll No. 39.
