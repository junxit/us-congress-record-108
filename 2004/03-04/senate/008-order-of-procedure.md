# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, on our side, we have requests for 15 minutes 
and 20 minutes for morning business. I checked with the majority. There 
would be no objections so long as they have equal time. So I ask 
unanimous consent that both sides have 35 minutes for morning business 
this morning.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, on the minority side, we yield 20 minutes to 
the Senator from Connecticut, Mr. Lieberman; and then, following that, 
15 minutes to the Senator from Iowa, Mr. Harkin.

                          ____________________
```
