# HONORING FORMER PRESIDENT WILLIAM JEFFERSON CLINTON

```
          HONORING FORMER PRESIDENT WILLIAM JEFFERSON CLINTON

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of S. Res. 425 submitted earlier 
today by Senators Daschle, Reid, and others.

[[Page 18321]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 425) honoring former President 
     William Jefferson Clinton on the occasion of his 58th 
     birthday.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
```
