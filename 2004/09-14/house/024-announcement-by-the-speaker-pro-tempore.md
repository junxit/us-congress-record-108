# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Ose). The Chair will remind all Members 
that remarks in debate may not engage in personalities toward the 
President or the Vice President, or the acknowledged candidates for 
those offices.
  Policies may be addressed in critical terms. But personal references 
of an offensive or accusatory nature are not proper.

                          ____________________
```
