# Text of Senate Amendment 2270

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

```
  SA 2270. Ms. COLLINS submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by her to the bill S. 1072, to authorize funds for Federal-aid 
highways, highway safety programs, and transit programs, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following new section:

     SEC. __. FUNDS FOR REBUILDING FISH STOCKS.

       Section 105 of the Miscellaneous Appropriations and Offsets 
     Act, 2004 (division H of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 
     2004) is repealed.
                                 ______
```
