# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Shelby, Richard C. (S000320) [R-AL], Jeffords, James M. (J000072) [I-VT]

**Measures:** S. 1072

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Madam President, I have a unanimous consent request 
from Senator Baucus for people to be on the floor. I ask unanimous 
consent that the following interns for the Senate Finance Committee be 
granted floor privileges for the remainder of the debate on the highway 
bill: Jane Bergeson, Shannon Augure, Jeremy Sieglitz, Tyson Hill, Simon 
Chabel, and Trace Thaxton.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. SHELBY. Mr. President, I also ask unanimous consent that Rich 
Steinmann and Kate Mattice, detailees from the Federal Transit 
Administration serving the Banking Committee, be granted floor 
privileges for the duration of the consideration of this bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Peter 
Smallwood, a fellow in the office of Senator Lieberman, be granted the 
privilege of the floor for the consideration of S. 1072, the highway 
bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
