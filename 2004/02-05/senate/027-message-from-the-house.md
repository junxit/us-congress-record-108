# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 355, H.R. 3030

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:57 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3030. An act to amend the Community Service Block 
     Grant Act to provide for quality improvements.

  The message also announced that the House has agreed to the following 
concurrent resolution, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 355. Concurrent resolution congratulating the 
     University of Delaware men's football team for winning the 
     National Collegiate Athletic Association I-AA national 
     championship.

                          ____________________
```
