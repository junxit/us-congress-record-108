# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 6, 2004

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 6, 2004

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Friday, February 6. I further ask that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of S. 
1072, the highway bill, for the purpose of debate only.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
