# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on March 
22, 2004, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       GLEN L. BOWER, OF ILLINOIS, TO BE A JUDGE OF THE UNITED 
     STATES TAX COURT FOR A TERM OF FIFTEEN YEARS AFTER HE TAKES 
     OFFICE, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 7, 2003.
```
