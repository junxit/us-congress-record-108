# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2207

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

**Measures:** S. 2207

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2207

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
that is due for its second reading.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2207) to improve women's access to health care 
     services, and the access of all individuals to emergency and 
     trauma care services, by reducing the excessive burden the 
     liability system places on the delivery of such services.

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, in order to place the bill on the calendar 
under the provisions of rule XIV, I object to further proceeding.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The bill will be placed on the calendar.
  Mr. CORNYN. I thank the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sununu). The Senator from Texas.

                          ____________________
```
