# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO POLICY COMMITTEE OF WHITE HOUSE CONFERENCE ON AGING

```
APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO POLICY COMMITTEE OF WHITE HOUSE CONFERENCE ON 
                                 AGING

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 211 of the Older 
Americans Act Amendments of 2000 (42 U.S.C. 3001 Note), and the order 
of the House of December 8, 2003, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Members on the part of the House to the 
Policy Committee of the White House Conference on Aging:
  Mr. E. Clay Shaw, Jr., Fort Lauderdale, Florida; and
  Mr. Howard P. (Buck) McKeon, Santa Clarita, California.
```
