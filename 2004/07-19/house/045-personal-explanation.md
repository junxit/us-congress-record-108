# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Kilpatrick, Carolyn C. (K000180) [D-MI]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 114, H.R. 1587, S. 2264

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. KILPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, personal reasons prevent me from being 
present for legislative business scheduled for today, Monday, July 19, 
2004. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on H.R. 1587, the 
Viet Nam Human Rights Act of 2003 (rollcall No. 391); ``yea'' on S. 
Con. Res. 114, a resolution concerning the importance of the 
distribution of food in schools to hungry or malnourished people around 
the world (rollcall No. 392); and ``yea'' on S. 2264, the Northern 
Uganda Crisis Response Act (rollcall No. 393).
```
