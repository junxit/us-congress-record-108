# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on July 
19, 2004, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       ALBERT CASEY, OF TEXAS, TO BE A GOVERNOR OF THE UNITED 
     STATES POSTAL SERVICE FOR A TERM EXPIRING DECEMBER 8, 2009, 
     WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 21, 2004.
```
