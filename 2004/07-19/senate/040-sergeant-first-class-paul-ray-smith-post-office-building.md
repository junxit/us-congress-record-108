# SERGEANT FIRST CLASS PAUL RAY SMITH POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
        SERGEANT FIRST CLASS PAUL RAY SMITH POST OFFICE BUILDING

  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Governmental Affairs Committee be discharged from further consideration 
of H.R. 4380, and that the Senate proceed to its immediate 
consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered. The clerk 
will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 4380) to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 4737 Mile Stretch Drive in 
     Holiday, Florida, as the ``Sergeant First Class Paul Ray 
     Smith Post Office Building''.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
read a third time and passed, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon 
the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 4380) was read the third time and passed.
```
