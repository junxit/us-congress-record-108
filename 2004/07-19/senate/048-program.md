# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Enzi, Michael B. (E000285) [R-WY]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, tomorrow, following morning business, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the nomination of William Myers III 
to be a U.S. Circuit Court Judge for the Ninth Circuit. Under the 
previous order, the Senate will vote on the motion to invoke cloture on 
the nomination at 2:15 p.m.
  Over the next several days, it is the leader's intention to move 
forward with several important issues. We need to complete action with 
respect to the Department of Defense appropriations conference report 
and the conference report to accompany the Tax Relief, Simplification, 
and Equity Act. The House is expected to act on these items this week, 
and it is the leader's intention to bring them before the Senate. In 
addition, the Senate may act on the Morocco free trade bill and any 
appropriations bills which can be dispensed with in a timely manner. 
Therefore, Senators are encouraged to expect a busy week with rollcall 
votes throughout.

                          ____________________
```
