# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until 10 
a.m. The first half of the time will be under the control of the 
majority leader or his designee. The final time will be under the 
control of the Democratic leader or his designee.
  The majority leader.

                          ____________________
```
