# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 2004

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. Tuesday, February 24. I 
further ask that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate then resume consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 
2061, the OB/GYN medical malpractice bill.
  I further ask consent that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 
p.m. for the weekly party luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
