# ORDER OF BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                           ORDER OF BUSINESS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, for the information of my colleagues and 
those watching, we have a scheduled cloture vote on the motion to 
proceed to the OB/GYN medical liability bill tomorrow afternoon. As I 
stated earlier, along with many of my colleagues, I hope that cloture 
will be invoked and that the Senate will be able to consider this bill. 
If we are prevented from even debating this legislation, it is our hope 
to take up the bipartisan gun liability bill. Given the earlier 
objection, it was necessary to file that cloture motion to proceed 
tonight. So the vote will occur on the gun liability bill on Wednesday 
of this week.
```
