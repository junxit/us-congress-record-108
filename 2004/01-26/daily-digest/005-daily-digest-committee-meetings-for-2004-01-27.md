# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 2004-01-27

                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR TUESDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: to hold 
hearings to examine the current situation regarding the discovery of

  Special Committee on Aging: to hold hearings to examine retirement
