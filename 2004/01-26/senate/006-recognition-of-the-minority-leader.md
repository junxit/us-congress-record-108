```

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Democratic leader is 
recognized.

be no rollcall votes until Tuesday afternoon. Is that the decision made

no rollcall votes until after the policy luncheons tomorrow.
  Mr. DASCHLE. I thank the assistant Republican leader for his
```
