# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in 
which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1879. An act to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     revise and extend provisions relating to mammography quality 
     standards.

  The message also announced that pursuant to section 128, Public Law 
108-132, the Chair, on behalf of the Majority Leader, appoints the 
following individual to the Commission on Review of Overseas Military 
Facility Structure of the United States--
  Major General Lewis E. Curtis III USAF (Retired).

                          ____________________
```
