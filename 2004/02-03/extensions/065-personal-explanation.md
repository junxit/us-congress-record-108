# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** McIntyre, Mike (M000485) [D-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 1385, S. 1920, H.R. 3493

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE McINTYRE

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 3, 2004

  Mr. McINTYRE. Mr. Speaker, on Wednesday, January 27, 2004, I was 
unavoidably absent due to inclement weather for roll call vote 6 on 
passage of H.R 1385, and roll call vote 7 on passage of H.R. 3493. Had 
I been present, I would have voted ``yes'' on roll call votes 6 and 7.
  Mr. Speaker, on Thursday, January 28, 2004, I was unavoidably absent 
due to inclement weather for roll call vote 8 on passage of the Baldwin 
amendment; roll call vote 9 on the Schakowsky motion to recommit; roll 
call vote 10 on final passage of S. 1920; and roll call vote 11 on the 
motion to instruct conferees. Had I been present, I would have voted 
``yes'' on roll call votes 8, 9, 10, and 11.
```
