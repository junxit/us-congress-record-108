# APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT HIS EXCELLENCY JOSE MARIA AZNAR, PRESIDENT OF THE GOVERNMENT OF SPAIN

```
  APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT HIS EXCELLENCY JOSE MARIA AZNAR, 
                  PRESIDENT OF THE GOVERNMENT OF SPAIN

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the President 
of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the 
Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the House of 
Representatives to escort His Excellency Jose Maria Aznar, President of 
the Government of Spain, into the House Chamber for the joint meeting 
tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
