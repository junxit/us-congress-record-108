# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      Committee on Armed Services

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Armed Services be authorized to meet on February 4, 2004, at 9:30 
a.m., in the Carl Vinson Room of the Rayburn House Office Building, in 
open session to receive testimony on the Defense Authorization request 
for fiscal year 2005 and the future years Defense program.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
