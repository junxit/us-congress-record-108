# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, this morning there will be a period for 
morning business for up to 30 minutes with the majority leader or his 
designee in control of the first 15 minutes and the Democratic leader 
or his designee in control of the final 15 minutes. Following morning 
business, we will resume consideration of the motion to proceed to the 
marriage amendment. The time until 12 noon will be equally divided for 
debate on the motion. At noon, the Senate will proceed to a cloture 
vote on the motion to proceed to the joint resolution. The cloture vote 
will be the first vote of the day.
  The leader has mentioned the Australian free trade legislation and 
the desire to finish that bill this week. In addition, as mentioned 
last night, the Senate needs to move forward with respect to the FSC/
ETI JOBS measure and appoint conferees. Therefore, Senators should 
anticipate additional votes during the session.

                          ____________________
```
