# Text of House Amendment 16

**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4818

```
H.R. 4818

                        Offered By: Mrs. Maloney

       Amendment No. 16: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:


     limitation on contributions to united nations population fund

       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act under 
     the heading ``international organizations and programs'' may 
     be made available for the United Nations Population Fund for 
     activities other than the prevention, remedy, and repair of 
     obstetric fistula.
```
