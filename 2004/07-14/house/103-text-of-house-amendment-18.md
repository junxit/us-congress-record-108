# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Paul, Ron (P000583) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 4818

```
H.R. 4818

                          Offered By: Mr. Paul

       Amendment No. 18: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:


        prohibition on assistance to the government of pakistan

       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to provide assistance to the Government of Pakistan.
```
