# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2061

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2061

  Mr. FRIST. I understand S. 2061, introduced by Senator Gregg and 
others, is at the desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2061) to improve women's access to health care 
     services and provide improved medical care by reducing the 
     excessive burden the liability system places on the delivery 
     of obstetrical and gynecological services.

  Mr. FRIST. I now ask for its second reading and object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
