# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIGING MICHAEL Z. ZVOLENSKY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
            A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIGING MICHAEL Z. ZVOLENSKY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 11, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Michael Zvolensky has devoted himself to serving others 
through his membership in the Boy Scouts of America; and
  Whereas, Michael Zvolensky has shared his time and talent with the 
community in which he resides; and

[[Page E806]]

  Whereas, Michael Zvolensky has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, Michael Zvolensky must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication he put forth in earning the Eagle Scout Award;
  Therefore, I join with Troop 358 and the entire 18th Congressional 
District in congratulating Michael Zvolensky as he receives the Eagle 
Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```
