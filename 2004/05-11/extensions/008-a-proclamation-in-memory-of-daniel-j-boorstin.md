# A PROCLAMATION IN MEMORY OF DANIEL J. BOORSTIN

```
             A PROCLAMATION IN MEMORY OF DANIEL J. BOORSTIN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 11, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, I hereby offer my heartfelt condolences to the 
family and friends of Daniel Boorstin upon the death of this 
outstanding human being.
  Daniel Boorstin was born October 1, 1914. Along with his numerous 
roles as husband, teacher, innovator, and leader, Dr. Boorstin served 
as the Librarian of Congress from 1975 to 1987.
  Dr. Boorstin will certainly be remembered by those who knew him as a 
brilliant scholar, accomplished historian, and devotee of the written 
word. Dr. Boorstin founded the Library's Center for the Book which 
promotes reading and literacy both nationally and internationally.
  Dr. Boorstin's devotion to both family and his work embody the 
excellence displayed throughout his life. His life and love gave joy to 
all who knew him.
  While I understand how words cannot express our grief at this time, I 
offer this token of profound sympathy to the family and friends of Dr. 
Daniel Boorstin.

                          ____________________
```
