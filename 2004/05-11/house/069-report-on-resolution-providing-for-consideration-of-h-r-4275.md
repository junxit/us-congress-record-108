                              {time}  2000
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4275, 
  PERMANENT EXTENSION OF 10-PERCENT INDIVIDUAL INCOME TAX RATE BRACKET
