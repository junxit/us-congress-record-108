# CONCERNING YESTERDAY'S PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

**Speaking:** Kirk, Mark Steven (K000360) [R-IL]

```
              CONCERNING YESTERDAY'S PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  (Mr. KIRK asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. KIRK. Mr. Speaker, yesterday a Member of Congress led the House 
in the Pledge of Allegiance using his own personal version. He left the 
phrase out ``under God'' from our Pledge. While he may not love ``under 
God'' in our country's pledge, God still loves him.
  God still loves him.

                          ____________________
```
