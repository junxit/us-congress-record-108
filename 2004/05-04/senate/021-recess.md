# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
p.m. having almost arrived, the Senate stands in recess until the hour 
of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:26 p.m. recessed until 2:15 p.m., and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. 
Voinovich).
```
