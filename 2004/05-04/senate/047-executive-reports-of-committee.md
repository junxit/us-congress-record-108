  The following executive reports of committee were submitted:

    Treaty Doc. 108-22  Additional Protocol Concerning Business and 
         Economic Relations with Poland (Exec. Rpt. N. 108-13)

       The text of the resolution of ratification as reported by

       Resolved (two-thirds of the Senators present concurring

     ratification of the Additional Protocol Between the United 
     States of America and the Republic of Poland to the Treaty 
     Between the United States of America and the Republic of 
     Poland Concerning Business and Economic Relations of March 
     21, 1990, signed at Brussels on January 12, 2004 (T. Doc. 
     108-22).
