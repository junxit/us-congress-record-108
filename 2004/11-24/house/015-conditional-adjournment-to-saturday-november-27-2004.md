# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 27, 2004

**Speaking:** Wolf, Frank R. (W000672) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 529

```
         CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 27, 2004

  Mr. WOLF. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Saturday, November 27, 
2004, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its concurrence in the House amendment to the Senate 
amendment to House Concurrent Resolution 529, in which case the House 
shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Virginia?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
