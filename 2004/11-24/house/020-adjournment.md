# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. TOM DAVIS of Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now 
adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Accordingly, pursuant to the previous order of the House of today, 
the House adjourned until 2 p.m. on Saturday, November 27, 2004, unless 
it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting its 
concurrence in the House amendment to the Senate amendment to House 
Concurrent Resolution 529, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  Thereupon, (at 2 o'clock and 41 minutes p.m.), pursuant to the 
previous order of the House of today, the House adjourned until 2 p.m. 
on Saturday, November 27, 2004, unless it sooner has received a message 
from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in the House amendment to 
the Senate amendment to House Concurrent Resolution 529, in which case 
the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
```
