# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, Senator Reid and I did not expect to be 
back so soon, but we are here again for a very brief session. We 
convene to consider two housekeeping matters that have been received 
from the House. The House has not yet acted on the concurrent 
resolution which will correct the enrollment of the consolidated or 
Omnibus appropriations measure. Without that House action we will be 
unable to transmit the conference report to the House so that they may 
then transmit the bill to the President. Therefore, we are here today 
to pass a short-term continuing resolution which is at the desk.
```
