# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. CHAMBLISS. Tomorrow, following morning business, the majority 
leader will seek consent to begin consideration of calendar No. 472, S. 
2290, the asbestos litigation bill. If we are unable to begin 
consideration of that measure, the majority leader is expected to move 
to proceed to the bill. Additional Senators have indicated their desire 
to speak on the bill during tomorrow's session. Unfortunately, if we 
are unable to begin consideration of the asbestos bill, we will be 
unable to begin the amendment process. Therefore, it appears unlikely 
that any rollcall votes will occur tomorrow. Members will be notified 
when the first vote is scheduled.
```
