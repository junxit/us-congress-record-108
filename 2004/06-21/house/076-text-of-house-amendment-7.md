# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Velazquez, Nydia M. (V000081) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4613

```
H.R. 4613

                       Offered By: Ms. Velazquez

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill, add the following 
     new title:

                 TITLE X--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 10001. None of the funds made available in this Act 
     may be used to fund any contract in contravention of section 
     8(d)(6) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 637(d)(6)).
```
