# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 591, H.Res. 660, H.R. 4363

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on motions to suspend the rules previously postponed. Votes 
will be taken in the following order:
  H. Res. 591, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 4363, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Res. 660, by the yeas and nays.
  The first and third electronic votes will be conducted as 15-minute 
votes. The second vote in this series will be a 5-minute vote.

                          ____________________
```
