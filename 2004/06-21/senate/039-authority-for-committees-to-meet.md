# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```

Senate on Monday, June 21, 2004 at 3 p.m. to hold a hearing on 
Nominations.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
