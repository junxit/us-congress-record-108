# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 1822, H.R. 3855, H.R. 4037

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1822. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 3751 West 6th Street in Los 
     Angeles, California, as the ``Dosan Ahn Chang Ho Post 
     Office''; to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 3855. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 607 Pershing Drive in 
     Laclede, Missouri, as the ``General John J. Pershing Post 
     Office''; to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.
       H.R. 4037. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 475 Kell Farm Drive in Cape 
     Girardeau, Missouri, as the ``Richard G. Wilson Processing 
     and Distribution Facility''; to the Committee on Governmental 
     Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
