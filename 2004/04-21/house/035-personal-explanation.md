# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Frelinghuysen, Rodney P. (F000372) [R-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 1779, H.R. 3147, H.R. 3970, H.R. 4019, H.R. 4030

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. FRELINGHUYSEN. Mr. Speaker, Wednesday, April 21, I was in my 
district in New Jersey attending services associated with the funeral 
of LT John Wroblewski (United States Marines) of Jefferson Township. On 
rollcall No. 121, H.R. 3970--Green Chemistry Research and Development, 
I would have voted ``yea.'' On rollcall No. 122, H.R. 4030--
Congressional Medal for Outstanding Contributions in Math and Science, 
I would have voted ``yea.''
  On rollcall No. 123 H.R. 3147--James V. Hansen Federal Building 
Designation, I would have voted ``yea.'' On rollcall No. 124 H.R. 
4019--To address the participation of Taiwan in the WHO, I would have 
voted ``yea.'' On rollcall No. 125--H.R. 1779, Guardsmen and Reservists 
Financial Relief Act, I would have voted ``yea.''

                          ____________________
```
