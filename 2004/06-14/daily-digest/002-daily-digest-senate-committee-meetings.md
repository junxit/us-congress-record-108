NOMINATIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the nominations of Thomas Fingar, of Virginia, to be Assistant 
Secretary of State for Intelligence and Research, Ralph Leo Boyce, Jr., 
of Virginia, to be Ambassador to Thailand, James R. Kunder, of 
Virginia, to be an Assistant Administrator of the United States Agency 
for International Development, and Suzanne Hale, of Virginia, to be 
Ambassador to Micronesia, after each nominee testified and answered 
questions in their own behalf.
