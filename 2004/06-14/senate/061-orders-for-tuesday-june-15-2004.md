# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 15, 2004

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 15, 2004

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10:30 a.m. on 
Tuesday, June 15. I further ask that following the prayer and pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of 
Calendar No. 503, S. 2400, the Department of Defense authorization 
bill, as provided under the previous order.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the Senate recess from 12:30 
p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
