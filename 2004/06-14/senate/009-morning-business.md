# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 60 
minutes, with the time equally divided between the two leaders or their 
designees.
  Who seeks recognition?
  The Senator from Wyoming.
```
