# MORNING HOUR DEBATES

```
                          MORNING HOUR DEBATES

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the order of the House of January 20, 2004, 
the Chair will now recognize Members from lists submitted by the 
majority and minority leaders for morning hour debates. The Chair will 
alternate recognition between the parties, with each party limited to 
30 minutes, and each Member, except the majority leader, the minority 
leader, or the minority whip limited to 5 minutes.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Florida (Mr. Weldon) for 5 
minutes.

                          ____________________
```
