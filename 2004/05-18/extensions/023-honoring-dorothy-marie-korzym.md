# HONORING DOROTHY MARIE KORZYM

**Speaking:** McCotter, Thaddeus G. (M001147) [R-MI]

```
                     HONORING DOROTHY MARIE KORZYM

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. THADDEUS G. McCOTTER

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 18, 2004

  Mr. McCOTTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Dorothy Marie 
Korzym upon her retirement after 25 years of teaching the children of 
our community.
  After receiving her masters degree from the University of Michigan in 
1984, Ms. Korzym taught at various schools, including Our Lady Gate of 
Heaven in Detroit, St. Fabian in Farmington Hills, and Marygrove 
College.
  In 1995, Ms. Korzym began teaching at St. Edith in Livonia.There is 
no doubt, her wisdom and guidance were a great asset to our community. 
Although she will be sorely missed at the school, she will continue to 
have a presence at St. Edith as she has been a member of the parish for 
the past 20 years.
  Mr. Speaker, I extend my sincere appreciation to Dorothy Marie 
Korzym, upon her retirement after 25 years of teaching, for her fine 
service to our community and our country.
```
