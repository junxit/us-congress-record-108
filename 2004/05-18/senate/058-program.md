# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Tomorrow, following morning business, the Senate will 
resume debate on the Department of Defense authorization bill. We will 
continue the amendment process tomorrow morning. The chairman and 
ranking member will be here throughout the day to receive amendments, 
and I encourage all Members who have amendments to contact them at the 
earliest possible time. We would like to complete the Defense bill this 
week. We will need everyone's cooperation to conclude this bill before 
the recess. Therefore, rollcall votes should be anticipated throughout 
the day, and Senators will be notified when the first vote is 
scheduled.
```
