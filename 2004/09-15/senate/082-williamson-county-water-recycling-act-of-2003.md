# WILLIAMSON COUNTY WATER RECYCLING ACT OF 2003

```
             WILLIAMSON COUNTY WATER RECYCLING ACT OF 2003

  The bill (H.R. 1732), to amend the Reclamation Wastewater and 
Groundwater Study and Facilities Act to authorize the Secretary of the 
Interior to participate in the Williamson County, Texas, Water 
Recycling and Reuse Project, and for other purposes, was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
