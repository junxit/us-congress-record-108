# JOHNSTOWN FLOOD NATIONAL MEMORIAL BOUNDARY ADJUSTMENT ACT OF 2003

**Measures:** H.R. 1521

```
   JOHNSTOWN FLOOD NATIONAL MEMORIAL BOUNDARY ADJUSTMENT ACT OF 2003

  The bill (H.R. 1521) to provide for additional lands to be included 
within the boundary of the Johnstown Flood National Memorial in the 
State of Pennsylvania, and for other purposes, was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
