# MARTIN LUTHER KING, JR., NATIONAL HISTORIC SITE LAND EXCHANGE ACT

```
   MARTIN LUTHER KING, JR., NATIONAL HISTORIC SITE LAND EXCHANGE ACT

  The bill (H.R. 1616) to authorize the exchange of certain lands
```
