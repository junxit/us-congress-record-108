**Speaking:** Blunt, Roy (B000575) [R-MO]

```
ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 17, 2004, AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, 
           SEPTEMBER 17, 2004 TO TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 21, 2004
```
