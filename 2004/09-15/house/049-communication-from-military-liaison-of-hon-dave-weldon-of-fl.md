# COMMUNICATION FROM MILITARY LIAISON OF HON. DAVE WELDON OF FLORIDA, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
  COMMUNICATION FROM MILITARY LIAISON OF HON. DAVE WELDON OF FLORIDA, 
                           MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Toni Mahoney, Military Liaison of the Honorable Dave 
Weldon of Florida, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, September 8, 2004.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to formally notify you, pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives, 
     that I was served with a civil trial subpoena, issued by the 
     County Court for Brevard County, Florida, for testimony and 
     documents.
       After consulting with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     determined that compliance with the subpoena was inconsistent 
     with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Toni Mahoney,
     Military Liaison.

                          ____________________
```
