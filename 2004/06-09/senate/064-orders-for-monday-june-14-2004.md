# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 14, 2004

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 14, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 1 p.m. on Monday, 
June 14. I further ask that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in 
the day, and the Senate then begin a period of morning business for 60 
minutes, equally divided between the two leaders or their designees; 
provided that following morning business the Senate resume 
consideration of Calendar No. 503, S. 2400, the Department of Defense 
authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
