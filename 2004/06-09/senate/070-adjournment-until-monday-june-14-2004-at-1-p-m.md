# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JUNE 14, 2004, AT 1 P.M.

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S.Res. 371

```
           ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JUNE 14, 2004, AT 1 P.M.

  Mr. FRIST. If there is no further business to come before the Senate, 
I ask unanimous consent the Senate stand in adjournment under the 
provisions of S. Res. 371 as a mark of further respect for President 
Ronald Wilson Reagan.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 3:15 p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, June 14, 2004, at 1 p.m.
```
