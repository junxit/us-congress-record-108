# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 1086, S. 1233

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker 
has signed the following enrolled bills:

       S. 1233. An Act to authorize assistance for the National 
     Great Blacks in Wax Museum and Justice Learning Center.
       H.R. 1086. An Act to encourage the development and 
     promulgation of voluntary consensus standards by providing

     organizations with respect to conduct engaged in for the
```
