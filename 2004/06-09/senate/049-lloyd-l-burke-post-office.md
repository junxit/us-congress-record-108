# LLOYD L. BURKE POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 3059

```
                       LLOYD L. BURKE POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 3059), to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 304 West Michigan Street in Stuttgart, 
Arkansas, as the ``Lloyd L. Burke Post Office,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
