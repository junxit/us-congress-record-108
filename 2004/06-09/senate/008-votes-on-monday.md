# VOTES ON MONDAY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                            VOTES ON MONDAY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, if I can, through the Chair, does the 
majority leader think those Monday votes will start around 5 o'clock, 
or has he made a decision?
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, we will be in discussion with the 
Democratic leadership, but I think around 5 o'clock or not before 5 
o'clock. I think it is important we make substantial progress Monday. 
We set aside, very appropriately, this entire week in honor of 
President Reagan, and it means that if we are to finish the business 
before us, we will need to start fairly early Monday and work hard 
through the day and the week.

                          ____________________
```
