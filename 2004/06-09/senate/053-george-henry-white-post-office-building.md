# GEORGE HENRY WHITE POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3353

```
                GEORGE HENRY WHITE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3353) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 525 Main Street in Tarboro, North Carolina, 
as the ``George Henry White Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
