# BOBBY MARSHALL GENTRY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
               BOBBY MARSHALL GENTRY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4176) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 122 West Elwood Avenue in Raeford, North 
Carolina, as the ``Bobby Marshall Gentry Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
