# BRIGADIER GENERAL (AUS-RET.) JOHN H. McLAIN POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 3068

```
        BRIGADIER GENERAL (AUS-RET.) JOHN H. McLAIN POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 3068) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2055 Siesta Drive in Sarasota, Florida, as 
the ``Brigadier General (AUS-Ret.) John H. McLain Post Office,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
