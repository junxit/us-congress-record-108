# ARMY PVT. SHAWN PAHNKE MANHATTAN POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 3537

```
              ARMY PVT. SHAWN PAHNKE MANHATTAN POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 3537) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 185 State Street in Manhattan, Illinois, as 
the ``Army Pvt. Shawn Pahnke Manhattan Post Office,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
