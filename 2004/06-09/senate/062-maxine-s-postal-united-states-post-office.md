# MAXINE S. POSTAL UNITED STATES POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 3917

```
               MAXINE S. POSTAL UNITED STATES POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 3917) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 695 Marconi Boulevard in Copiague, New York, 
as the ``Maxine S. Postal United States Post Office,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
