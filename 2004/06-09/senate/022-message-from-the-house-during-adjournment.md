# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
               MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

  At 3:26 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following concurrent resolution:

       S. Con. Res. 115. Concurrent resolution authorizing the use 
     of the rotunda of the Capitol for the lying in state of the 
     remains of the late Ronald Wilson Reagan, 40th President of 
     the United States.

                          ____________________
```
