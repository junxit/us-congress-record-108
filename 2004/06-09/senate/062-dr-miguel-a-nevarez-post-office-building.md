# DR. MIGUEL A. NEVAREZ POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
               DR. MIGUEL A. NEVAREZ POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4299) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 410 South Jackson Road in Edinburg, Texas, as 
the ``Dr. Miguel A. Nevarez Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LIEBERMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
