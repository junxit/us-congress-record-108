# BEN ATCHLEY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3769

```
                    BEN ATCHLEY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3769) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 137 East Young High Pike in Knoxville, 
Tennessee, as the ``Ben Atchley Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
