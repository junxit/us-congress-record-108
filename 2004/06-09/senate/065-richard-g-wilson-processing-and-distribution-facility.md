# RICHARD G. WILSON PROCESSING AND DISTRIBUTION FACILITY

**Measures:** H.R. 4037

```
         RICHARD G. WILSON PROCESSING AND DISTRIBUTION FACILITY

  The bill (H.R. 4037) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 475 Kell Farm Drive in Cape Girardeau, 
Missouri, as the ``Richard G. Wilson Processing and Distribution 
Facility'', was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third
```
