# MYRON V. GEORGE POST OFFICE

```
                      MYRON V. GEORGE POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 3733) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 410 Huston Street in Altamont, Kansas, as the 
``Myron V. George Post Office,'' was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
