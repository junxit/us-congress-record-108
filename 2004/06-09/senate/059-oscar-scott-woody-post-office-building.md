# OSCAR SCOTT WOODY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3740

```
                 OSCAR SCOTT WOODY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3740) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 223 Main Street in Roxboro, North Carolina, 
as the ``Oscar Scott Woody Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
