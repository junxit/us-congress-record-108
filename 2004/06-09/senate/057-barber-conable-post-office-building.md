# BARBER CONABLE POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3690

```
                  BARBER CONABLE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3690) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2 West Main Street in Batavia, New York, as 
the ``Barber Conable Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
