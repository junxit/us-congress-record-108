# NEW BRIDGE LANDING POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 2130

```
                     NEW BRIDGE LANDING POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 2130) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 121 Kinderkamack Road in River Edge, New 
Jersey, as the ``New Bridge Landing Post Office,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
