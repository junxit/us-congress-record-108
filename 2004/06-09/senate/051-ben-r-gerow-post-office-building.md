# BEN R. GEROW POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3234

```
                   BEN R. GEROW POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3234) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 14 Chestnut Street in Liberty, New York, as 
the ``Ben R. Gerow Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
