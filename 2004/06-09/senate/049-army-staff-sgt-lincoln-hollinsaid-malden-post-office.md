# ARMY STAFF SGT. LINCOLN HOLLINSAID MALDEN POST OFFICE

```
         ARMY STAFF SGT. LINCOLN HOLLINSAID MALDEN POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 3536) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 210 Main Street in Malden, Illinois, as the 
``Army Staff Sgt. Lincoln Hollinsaid Malden Post Office,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
