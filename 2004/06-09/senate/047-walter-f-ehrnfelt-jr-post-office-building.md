# WALTER F. EHRNFELT, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
              WALTER F. EHRNFELT, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3300) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 15500 Pearl Road in Strongsville, Ohio, as 
the ``Walter F. Ehrnfelt, Jr. Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
