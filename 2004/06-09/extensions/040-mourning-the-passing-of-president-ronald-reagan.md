# MOURNING THE PASSING OF PRESIDENT RONALD REAGAN

```
            MOURNING THE PASSING OF PRESIDENT RONALD REAGAN

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                           HON. RALPH REGULA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 9, 2004

  Mr. REGULA. Mr. Speaker, President Ronald Reagan was a warm 
thoughtful leader who governed by the Golden Rule of caring for others. 
Many speakers have paid eloquent tribute to his role as both a world 
leader and a best friend of the American people. Because of our shared 
love of land I developed a personal relationship with President Reagan 
that I will always cherish.

                          ____________________
```
