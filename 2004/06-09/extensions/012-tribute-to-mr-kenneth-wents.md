# TRIBUTE TO MR. KENNETH WENTS

```
                      TRIBUTE TO MR. KENNETH WENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GARY G. MILLER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 9, 2004

  Mr. GARY G. MILLER of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise to pay tribute 
today to a man who served his country with dignity, and lived his life 
with honor. Mr. Kenneth Wents of Hacienda Heights, California, will 
always be remembered for his courage and heart.
  Mr. Wents led a full and exciting life. Even as a prisoner of war 
during World War II for forty-two months, he faced extraordinary 
challenges with determination and optimism.
  Mr. Wents touched the lives of many, especially the generous 
volunteers of the Greater La Puente Valley Meals on Wheels, who valued 
him and appreciated his warm spirit.
  Mr. Speaker, the life of Mr. Kenneth Wents has greatly impacted the 
lives of many in a positive way. It is my honor to join his family and 
friends in commemorating his legacy.

                          ____________________
```
