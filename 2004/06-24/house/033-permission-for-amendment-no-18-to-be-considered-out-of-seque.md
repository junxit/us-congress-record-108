# PERMISSION FOR AMENDMENT NO. 18 TO BE CONSIDERED OUT OF SEQUENCE AND WITHDRAWN AFTER DEBATE DURING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4663, SPENDING CONTROL ACT OF 2004

```
 PERMISSION FOR AMENDMENT NO. 18 TO BE CONSIDERED OUT OF SEQUENCE AND 
   WITHDRAWN AFTER DEBATE DURING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4663, 
                      SPENDING CONTROL ACT OF 2004

  Mr. DREIER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during further 
consideration of H.R. 4663 pursuant to House Resolution 692, amendment 
No. 18 in House Report 108-566 may be considered out of sequence in the 
Committee of the Whole, and that the amendment may be withdrawn by its 
proponent after debate thereon.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
