# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. FRIST. Madam President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that there be 1 hour of debate equally divided between the 
chairman and ranking member or designees, and that following that time, 
the Senate proceed to a vote on the nomination; provided further that 
following the vote, the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action and the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Ms. Murkowski). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
