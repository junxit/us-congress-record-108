# Text of Senate Amendment 3540

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
  SA 3540. Mr. STEVENS (for Mr. Conrad) proposed an amendment to the 
bill H.R. 4613, making appropriations for the Department of Defense for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 2005, and for other purposes; as 
follows:

       On page 112, between lines 13 and 14, insert the following:
       Sec. 8121. Of the amount appropriated by title III under 
     the heading ``Aircraft Procurement, Air Force'', up to 
     $7,000,000 may be available for F-16 Theater Airborne 
     Reconnaissance System upgrades.
                                 ______
```
