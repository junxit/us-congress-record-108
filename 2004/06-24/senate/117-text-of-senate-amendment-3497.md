# Text of Senate Amendment 3497

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
  SA 3497. Mr. STEVENS (for Mr. Leahy) proposed an amendment to the 
bill H.R. 4613, making appropriations for the Department of Defense for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 2005, and for other purposes; as 
follows:

       On page 112, between lines 13 and 14, insert the following:
       Sec. 8121. Of the amount appropriated by title under the 
     heading ``Other Procurement, Air Force'', up to $2,000,000 
     may be used for aircrew bladder relief device (ABRD) kits.
                                 ______
```
