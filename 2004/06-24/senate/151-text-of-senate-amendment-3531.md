# Text of Senate Amendment 3531

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
  SA 3531. Mr. STEVENS (for Mr. Roberts) proposed an amendment to the 
bill H.R. 4613, making appropriations for the Department of Defense for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 2005, and for other purposes; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place in the bill, insert the following:
       Sec. ___ Of the amount appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by title    of this Act under the heading 
     ``Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation, Army, up to 
     $3,000,000 may be available to establish redundant systems to 
     ensure continuity of operations and disaster recovery at the 
     United States Army Intelligence and Security Command's 
     Intelligence Dominance Center.
                                 ______
```
