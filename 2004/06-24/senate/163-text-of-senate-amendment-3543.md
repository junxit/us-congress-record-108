# Text of Senate Amendment 3543

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
  SA 3543. Mr. STEVENS (for Mrs. Feinstein) proposed an amendment to 
the bill H.R. 4613, making appropriations for the Department of Defense 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2005, and for other purposes; 
as follows:

       On page 112, between lines 13 and 14, insert the following:
       Sec. 8121. Of the amount appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by title IV of this Act under the heading 
     ``Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation, Navy'', up to 
     $5,000,000 may be available for support of the TIGER pathogen 
     detection system.
                                 ______
```
