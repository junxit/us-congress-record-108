# Text of Senate Amendment 3545

**Speaking:** Inouye, Daniel K. (I000025) [D-HI]

```
  SA 3545. Mr. INOUYE proposed an amendment to the bill H.R. 4613, 
making appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 2005, and for other purposes; as follows:

       On page 112, between lines 13 and 14, insert the following:
       Sec. 8121. Of the amount appropriated by title IV under the 
     heading ``Research, Development, Test and Evaluation, Army'', 
     up to $2,500,000 may be used for small business development 
     and transition.
```
