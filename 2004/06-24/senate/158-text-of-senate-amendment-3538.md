# Text of Senate Amendment 3538

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
  SA 3538. Mr. STEVENS (for Mr. Sununu) proposed an amendment to the 
bill H.R. 4613, making appropriations for the Department of Defense for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 2005, and for other purposes; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate time, insert the following:
       Of the funds appropriated, up to $2,000,000 may be 
     available for the Advanced Composite Radome Project.
                                 ______
```
