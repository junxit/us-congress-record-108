# Text of Senate Amendment 3505

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
  SA 3505. Mr. STEVENS (for Mr. Bayh (for himself and Mr. Lugar)) 
proposed an amendment to the bill H.R. 4613, making appropriations for 
the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2005, and for other purposes; as follows:

       On page 112, between lines 13 and 14, insert the following:
       Sec. 8121. Of the amount appropriated by title under the 
     heading ``Operation and Maintenance, Army'', up to 
     $21,900,000 may be used for M1A1 Tank Transmission 
     Maintenance.
                                 ______
```
