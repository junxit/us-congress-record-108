# Text of Senate Amendment 3501

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
  SA 3501. Mr. STEVENS (for Mr. Santorum) proposed an amendment to the 
bill H.R. 4613, making appropriations for the Department of Defense for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 2005, and for other purposes; as 
follows:

       On page 112, between lines 13 and 14, insert the following:
       Sec. 8121. Of the amount appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by title IV of this Act under the heading 
     ``Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation, Army'', up to 
     $3,000,000 may be available for Medical Advanced Technology 
     for the Intravenous Membrane Oxygenator.
                                 ______
```
