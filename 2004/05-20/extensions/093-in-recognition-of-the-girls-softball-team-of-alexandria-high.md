# IN RECOGNITION OF THE GIRLS SOFTBALL TEAM OF ALEXANDRIA HIGH SCHOOL

```
  IN RECOGNITION OF THE GIRLS SOFTBALL TEAM OF ALEXANDRIA HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 20, 2004

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to 
Girls Softball Team of Alexandria High School in Calhoun County, 
Alabama. On May 15, 2004, these young ladies won the 4-A Girls Softball 
State Championship.
  Congratulations to Amy Brown, Whitney Burt, Stevie Smith, Hollie 
Henderson, Allie Barker, Anna Pitts, Lindy Baird, Whitney Hurst, Haley 
Henderson, Hali Lash, Jordan Brown and Alyssa Brown. Thanks to Coaches 
Brian Hess and Toni Duncan for their leadership and the support of 
Manager Sara Borders and Bookkeeper Donya Barker.
  I know that Calhoun County, Alabama, and the entire Third District 
share with me their best wishes for this accomplishment. Only through 
hard work, dedication and a team effort could this be achieved. Thank 
you, Mr. Speaker.

                          ____________________
```
