# UNITED STATES ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN--PM 79

```
               UNITED STATES ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN--PM 79

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Governmental Affairs:

To the Congress of the United States:
  Consistent with the provisions of the Arctic Research and Policy Act 
of 1984, as amended (15 U.S.C. 4108(a)), I transmit herewith the eighth 
biennial revision (2004-2008) to the United States Arctic Research 
Plan, as prepared for the Congress and the Administration by the 
Interagency Arctic Research Policy Committee.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, May 20, 2004.

                          ____________________
```
