# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 21, 2004

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 2400

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 21, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Friday, May 21. I further 
ask that following the prayer and the pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date and the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate then resume consideration of Calendar No. 503, S. 2400, 
the Department of Defense authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
