# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 923, H.R. 3104

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House, reports that on May 20, 2004 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.R. 923. To amend the Small Business Investment Act of 
     1958 to allow certain premier certified lenders to elect to 
     maintain an alternative loss reserve.
       H.R. 3104. To provide for the establishment of separate 
     campaign medals to be awarded to members of the uniformed 
     services who participate in Operation Enduring Freedom and to 
     members of the uniformed services who participate in 
     Operation Iraqi Freedom.

                          ____________________
```
