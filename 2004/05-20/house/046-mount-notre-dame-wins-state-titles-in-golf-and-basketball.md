# MOUNT NOTRE DAME WINS STATE TITLES IN GOLF AND BASKETBALL

```
       MOUNT NOTRE DAME WINS STATE TITLES IN GOLF AND BASKETBALL

  (Mr. CHABOT asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CHABOT. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the achievements of two 
outstanding groups of young women from my District. Both happen to be 
from Mount Notre Dame High School.
  This past fall the Mount Notre Dame golf team registered its second 
consecutive Ohio State championship with an impressive 23-stroke 
victory over Cincinnati rival Ursuline Academy. The Cougars dominated, 
finishing 16 strokes better than their previous year's State title 
performance.
  Not to be outdone by their classmates, Mount Notre Dame's basketball 
team was crowned Ohio State champions with a convincing 59 to 44 win 
over Chaminade-Julienne. The victory topped off a perfect 28 and 0 
season and propelled the team to a number two national ranking in the 
prestigious USA Today poll.
  It gives me great pleasure to recognize the continued success of 
Mount Notre Dame High School, its coaches and administrators, and these 
young women who serve as ideal role models for future generations of 
high school student athletes.
  Congratulations, Mount Notre Dame.

                          ____________________
```
