# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 4200, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2005

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 4200, 
        NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2005

  Mr. HUNTER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 4200, the Clerk be authorized to correct

may be necessary to reflect the actions of the House in amending the 
bill.

request of the gentleman from California?
```
