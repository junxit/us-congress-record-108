# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 24, 2004

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 432

```
            CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 24, 2004

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 4 p.m. on Monday, May 24, 2004, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 432, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
