# A PROCLAMATION HONORING MARION STEWART ON HER 99TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
      A PROCLAMATION HONORING MARION STEWART ON HER 99TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 7, 2004

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Marion Stewart was born on July 19, 1905; and
  Whereas, Marion Stewart is celebrating her 99th birthday today; and
  Whereas, Marion Stewart, is a long-time active participant in the 
social and civic life of the community; and
  Whereas, Marion Stewart has exemplified a love for her family and 
friends and must be commended for her life-long dedication to helping 
others.
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District of Ohio in wishing Marion Stewart a very happy 99th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
