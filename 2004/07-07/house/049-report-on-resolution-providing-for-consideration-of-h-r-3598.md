# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3598, MANUFACTURING TECHNOLOGY COMPETITIVENESS ACT OF 2004

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3598, 
          MANUFACTURING TECHNOLOGY COMPETITIVENESS ACT OF 2004

  Mr. Hastings of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 108-589) on the resolution (H. Res. 706) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3598) to establish an 
interagency committee to coordinate Federal manufacturing research and 
development efforts in manufacturing, strengthen existing programs to 
assist manufacturing innovation and education, and expand outreach 
programs for small and medium-sized manufacturers, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
