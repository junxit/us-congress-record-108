# Text of House Amendment 27

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4754

```
H.R. 4754

                        Offered By: Mr. Sherman

       Amendment No. 27: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to detain for more

[[Page H5332]]

     than 30 days a United States citizen, apprehended on United 
     States territory, solely because that citizen is classified 
     as an enemy combatant.
```
