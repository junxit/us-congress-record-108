# Text of House Amendment 28

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4754

```
H.R. 4754

                        Offered By: Mr. Sherman

       Amendment No. 28: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to defend in court the detention for more than 30 
     days of a United States citizen, apprehended on United States 
     territory, solely because that citizen is classified as an 
     enemy combatant.
```
