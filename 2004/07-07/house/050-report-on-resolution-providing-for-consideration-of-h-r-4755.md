# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4755, LEGISLATIVE BRANCH APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2005

**Measures:** H.Res. 707, H.R. 4755

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4755, 
              LEGISLATIVE BRANCH APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2005

  Mr. Hastings of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 108-590) on the resolution (H. Res. 707) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4755) making 
appropriations for the Legislative Branch for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2005, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
