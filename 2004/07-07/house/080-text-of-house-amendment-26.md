# Text of House Amendment 26

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4754

```
H.R. 4754

                        Offered By: Mr. Sherman

       Amendment No. 26: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to detain for more than 30 days a United States 
     citizen, apprehended on United States territory, solely 
     because that citizen is classified as an enemy combatant.
       Sec. 802. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to defend in court the detention for more than 30 
     days of a United States citizen, apprehended on United States 
     territory, solely because that citizen is classified as an 
     enemy combatant.
       Sec. 803. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to classify any United States citizen as an enemy 
     combatant unless that citizen is apprehended outside the 
     United States.
```
