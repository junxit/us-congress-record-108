# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair must remind all Members that 
remarks in debate may not engage in personalities towards the President 
or the Vice President. Policies may be addressed in critical terms, but 
personal references of an offensive or accusatory nature are not 
proper.
```
