# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 60 
minutes, with the first 30 minutes under the control of the Democratic 
leader or his designee and the second 30 minutes under the control of 
the majority leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Iowa is recognized pursuant to previous agreement.
```
