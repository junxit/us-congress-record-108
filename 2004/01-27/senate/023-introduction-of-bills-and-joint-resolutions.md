# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2031, S. 2032, S. 2033

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. CORNYN (for himself, Mr. Dodd, and Mr. Lott):
       S. 2031. A bill to authorize the States to implement such 
     mechanisms as are necessary in a time of national crisis to 
     ensure the continuity of the Senate in the event that a 
     quorum of the Senate is not present due to the inability of 
     members of the Senate to discharge the powers and duties of 
     their office; to the Committee on Rules and Administration.
           By Mrs. BOXER:
       S. 2032. A bill to provide assistance and security for 
     women and children in Afghanistan and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Foreign Relations.
           By Mr. DOMENICI (for himself and Mr. Bingaman):
       S. 2033. A bill to designate the Federal building located 
     at Fifth and Richardson Avenues in Roswell, New Mexico, as 
     the ``Joe Skeen Federal Building''; to the Committee on 
     Environment and Public Works.

                          ____________________
```
