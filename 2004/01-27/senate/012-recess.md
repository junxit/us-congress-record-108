# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the hour 
of 12:30 having arrived, the Senate will stand in recess until the hour 
of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:38 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Craig).
```
