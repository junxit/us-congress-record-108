# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF S. 1920, BANKRUPTCY ABUSE PREVENTION AND CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT OF 2004

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF S. 1920, BANKRUPTCY 
          ABUSE PREVENTION AND CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT OF 2004

  Mr. LINCOLN DIAZ-BALART of Florida, from the Committee on Rules, 
submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 108-407) on the resolution (H. 
Res. 503) providing for consideration of the Senate bill (S. 1920) to 
extend for 6 months the period for which chapter 12 of title 11 of the 
United States Code is reenacted, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
