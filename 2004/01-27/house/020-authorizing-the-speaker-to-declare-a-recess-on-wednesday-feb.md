# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2004, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY JOSE MARIA AZNAR, PRESIDENT OF THE GOVERNMENT OF SPAIN

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 
2004, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY JOSE 
           MARIA AZNAR, PRESIDENT OF THE GOVERNMENT OF SPAIN

  Mr. GREENWOOD. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order at any time on Wednesday, February 4, 2004, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting His Excellency Jose Maria Aznar, President 
of the Government of Spain.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
```
