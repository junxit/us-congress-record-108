# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 502, H.Res. 503, S. 610, S. 1920

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. LINCOLN DIAZ-BALART of Florida: Committee on Rules. 
     House Resolution 502. Resolution providing for consideration 
     of the bill (S. 610) to amend the provisions of title 5, 
     United States Code, to provide for workforce flexibilities 
     and certain Federal personnel provisions relating to the 
     National Aeronautics and Space Administration, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. 108-406). Referred to the House Calendar.
       Mr. SESSIONS: Committee on Rules. House Resolution 503. 
     Resolution providing for consideration of the bill (S. 1920) 
     to extend for 6 months the period for which chapter 12 of 
     title 11 of the United States Code is reenacted (Rept. 108-
     407). Referred to the House Calendar.
```
