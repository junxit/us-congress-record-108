# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business with the first 4 
hours equally divided between the two leaders or their designees.
  As a Senator from Alaska, I ask I be notified if anyone makes a 
motion pertaining to any appropriations bill this morning.

                          ____________________
```
