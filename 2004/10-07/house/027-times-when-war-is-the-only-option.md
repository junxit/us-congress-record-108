# TIMES WHEN WAR IS THE ONLY OPTION

```
                   TIMES WHEN WAR IS THE ONLY OPTION

  (Mr. HAYES asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HAYES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in thanks and tribute to our 
wonderful troops and a volunteer Army in Iraq who are making the world 
safer, and I quote my friend Gary Bauer about Senator Kerry: Moreover, 
wind surfing is not a policy; it is an excuse or inability to act. He 
sounds like Neville Chamberlain clinging to a scrap of paper after 
accepting the lies of a lunatic and declaring peace in our time, the 
terrorists had been there all along.
  But Winston Churchill understood there are times when war is the only 
option. One more U.N. resolution, one more scrap of paper from Saddam 
Hussein would not have made America safer. Our President understood 
that and he stood up for America.

                          ____________________
```
