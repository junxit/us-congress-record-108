# Text of Senate Amendment 4009

**Speaking:** Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

```
  SA 4009. Mr. LEAHY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 3981 proposed by Mr. McConnell (for himself, Mr. Reid, Mr. 
Frist, and Mr. Daschle) to the resolution S. Res. 445, to eliminate 
certain restrictions on service of a Senator on the Senate Select 
Committee on Intelligence; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       In section 101(b)(1), after ``Service'' insert ``, the 
     Bureau of Customs and Border Protection, the Bureau of 
     Citizenship and Immigration Services, the Bureau of 
     Immigration and Customs Enforcement, and matters relating to 
     the immigration functions of the Directorate of Border and 
     Transportation Security''.
                                 ______
```
