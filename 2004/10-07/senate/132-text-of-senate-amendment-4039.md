# Text of Senate Amendment 4039

**Speaking:** Shelby, Richard C. (S000320) [R-AL]

```
  SA 4039. Mr. SHELBY (for himself and Mr. Sarbanes) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the resolution S. Res. 445, 
to eliminate certain restrictions on service of a Senator on the Senate 
Select Committee on Intelligence; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place insert the following: ``Provided, 
     That the jurisdiction provided under section 101(b)(1) shall 
     not include the Currency and Financial Transaction Reporting 
     Act.
                                 ______
```
