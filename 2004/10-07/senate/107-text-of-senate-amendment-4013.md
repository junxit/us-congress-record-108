# Text of Senate Amendment 4013

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
  SA 4013. Mr. KYL submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 3981 proposed by Mr. McConnell (for himself, Mr. Reid, Mr. 
Frist, and Mr. Daschle) to the resolution S. Res. 445, to eliminate 
certain restrictions on service of a Senator on the Senate Select 
Committee on Intelligence; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       On page 7, line 4, strike ``, and shall have full'' and all 
     that follows through line 6, and insert the following: ``. 
     Personal designated representatives shall have the same 
     access to select Committee staff, information, records, and 
     databases as select Committee staff, as determined by the 
     Chairman and Vice Chairman.''.
                                 ______
```
