# Text of Senate Amendment 4002

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
  SA 4002. Mr. McCAIN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 3981 proposed by Mr. McConnell (for himself, Mr. Reid, Mr. 
Frist, and Mr. Daschle) to the resolution S. Res. 445, to eliminate 
certain restrictions on service of a Senator on the Senate Select 
Committee on Intelligence; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       In section 101(b)(1), strike ``Coast Guard, to the 
     Transportation Security Administration,'' and insert ``Coast 
     Guard (other than functions of the Coast Guard related to 
     homeland securty),''.
                                 ______
```
