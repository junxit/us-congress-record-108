# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2938

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2938

  Mr. McCONNELL. I understand there is a bill at the desk, and I ask 
for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2938) to grant a Federal charter to the National 
     American Indian Veterans, Incorporated.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I now ask for its second reading, and in order to 
place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will receive its 
second reading on the next legislative day.
```
