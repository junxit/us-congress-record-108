# Text of Senate Amendment 4040

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM]

```
  SA 4040. Mr. BINGAMAN (for Mr. Domenici (for himself and Mr. 
Bingaman)) proposed an amendment to amendment SA 3981 proposed by Mr. 
McConnell (for himself, Mr. Reid, Mr. Frist, and Mr. Daschle) to the 
resolution S. Res. 445, to eliminate certain restrictions on service of 
a Senator on the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence; as follows:

       Section 101(b) is amended by--
       (1) striking paragraph (10); and
       (2) adding at the end the following:
     ``Matters relating to organization and management of United 
     States nuclear export policy (except programs in the Homeland 
     Security) shall be referred to the Committee on Energy and 
     Natural Resources.''.
                                 ______
```
