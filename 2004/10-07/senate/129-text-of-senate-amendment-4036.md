# Text of Senate Amendment 4036

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
  SA 4036. Mr. DURBIN proposed an amendment to amendment SA 3981 
proposed by Mr. McConnell (for himself, Mr. Reid, Mr. Frist, and Mr. 
Daschle) to the resolution S. Res. 445, to eliminate certain 
restrictions on service of a Senator on the Senate Select Committee on 
Intelligence; as follows:

       In section 201, at the end of subsection (g), add the 
     following:
       ``(d) Of the funds made available to the select Committee 
     for personnel--
       ``(1) not more than 55 percent shall be under the control 
     of the Chairman; and
       ``(2) not less than 45 percent shall be under the control 
     of the Vice Chairman.''.
                                 ______
```
