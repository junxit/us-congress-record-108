# Text of Senate Amendment 4017

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

```
  SA 4017. Mrs. HUTCHISON submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed to amendment SA 3981 proposed by Mr. McConnell (for himself, 
Mr. Reid, Mr. Frist, and Mr. Daschle) to the resolution S. Res. 445, to 
eliminate certain restrictions on service of a Senator on the Senate 
Select Committee on Intelligence; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       In section 402, strike the second sentence and insert ``The 
     Subcommittee on the Legislative Branch shall be combined with 
     the Subcommittee on VA, HUD, and Independent Agencies into 1 
     subcommittee.''.
                                 ______
```
