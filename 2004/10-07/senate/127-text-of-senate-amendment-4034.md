# Text of Senate Amendment 4034

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

```
  SA 4034. Ms. COLLINS submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
to amendment SA 3981 proposed by Mr. McConnell (for himself, Mr. Reid, 
Mr. Frist, and Mr. Daschle) to the resolution S. Res. 445, to eliminate 
certain restrictions on service of a Senator on the Senate Select 
Committee on Intelligence; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       In section 101(b), strike paragraph (1) and insert the 
     following:
       (1) Department of Homeland Security, except matters 
     relating to the functions of the Coast Guard not related to 
     homeland security, the Federal Law Enforcement Training 
     Center, and the revenue functions of the Customs Service.
                                 ______
```
