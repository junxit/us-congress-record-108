# Text of Senate Amendment 4024

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
  SA 4024. Mr. HATCH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 3981 proposed by Mr. McConnell (for himself, Mr. Reid, Mr. 
Frist, and Mr. Daschle) to the resolution S. Res. 445, to eliminate 
certain restrictions on service of a Senator on the Senate Select 
Committee on Intelligence; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the end of title I, insert the following:

     SEC. 102. CLARIFICATION OF JURISDICTION.

       Nothing in this resolution shall diminish the primary 
     jurisdiction of the Committee on the Judiciary over matters 
     relating to the administration of justice, including the 
     criminal law and law enforcement entities including the 
     Department of Justice and the Federal Bureau of 
     Investigation.
                                 ______
```
