# Text of Senate Amendment 3994

**Speaking:** Chambliss, Saxby (C000286) [R-GA]

```
  SA 3994. Mr. CHAMBLISS (for himself, Mr. Kennedy, and Mr. Specter) 
proposed an amendment to amendment SA 3981 proposed by Mr. McConnell 
(for himself, Mr. Reid, Mr. Frist, and Mr. Daschle) to the resolution 
S. Res. 445, to eliminate certain restrictions on service of a Senator 
on the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence; as follows:

       At the end of section 101(b)(1) insert the following: 
     ``except matters relating to the U.S. Citizenship and 
     Immigration Service and the immigration functions of the U.S. 
     Customs and Border Protection and the U.S. Immigration and 
     Customs Enforcement, the Directorate of Border and 
     Transportation Security.''.
```
