# Text of Senate Amendment 3991

**Speaking:** Hagel, Chuck (H001028) [R-NE]

**Measures:** S.Res. 400

```
  SA 3991. Mr. HAGEL (for himself and Mr. Chambliss) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 3981 proposed by Mr. 
McConnell (for himself, Mr. Reid, Mr. Frist, and Mr. Daschle) to the 
resolution S. Res. 445, to eliminate certain restrictions on service of 
a Senator on the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       Section 301(b) is amended by adding at the end the 
     following: ``The service of a member selected pursuant to 
     section 2(a)(1) of S. Res. 400 (94th Congress) shall not be 
     counted for purposes of paragraph 4(a)(1) of rule XXV of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate.''.
                                 ______
```
