# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on motions to suspend the rules previously postponed. Votes 
will be taken in the following order:
  H. Con. Res. 295, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Res. 612, by the yeas and nays; and

  The vote on S.J. Res. 28 will occur tomorrow.
```
