# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 83, PROPOSING AN AMENDMENT TO THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES REGARDING THE APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUALS TO FILL VACANCIES IN THE...

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 83, H.Res. 657

```
   REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 83, 
    PROPOSING AN AMENDMENT TO THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES 
REGARDING THE APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUALS TO FILL VACANCIES IN THE HOUSE 
                           OF REPRESENTATIVES

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington (during the Special Order of Mrs. Johnson 
of Connecticut), from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 108-519) on the resolution (H. Res. 657) providing 
for consideration of the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 83) proposing an 
amendment to the Constitution of the United States regarding the 
appointment of individuals to fill vacancies in the House of 
Representatives, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered 
to be printed.
```
