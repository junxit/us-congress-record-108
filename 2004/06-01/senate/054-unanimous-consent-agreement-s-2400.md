# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 2400

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 2400

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the agreement 
reached earlier today limiting amendments to the Department of Defense 
authorization bill be modified so that all second-degree amendments 
must be relevant to the amendment to which they are offered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
