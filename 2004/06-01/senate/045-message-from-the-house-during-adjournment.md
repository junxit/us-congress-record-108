# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S. 2092

```
               MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 2003, the 
Secretary of the Senate, during the adjournment of the Senate, on May 
24, 2004, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 2092. An act to address the participation of Taiwan in 
     the World Health Organization.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Stevens) on today, June 1, 2004.

                          ____________________
```
