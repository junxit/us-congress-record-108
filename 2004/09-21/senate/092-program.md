# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow, following morning business, the Senate will begin 
consideration of the Goss nomination. Under the previous order, there 
will be up to 6 hours of debate prior to a vote on confirmation. It is 
my hope we will not require the entire allotment of debate time. 
Senators should expect a vote on the nomination sometime tomorrow 
afternoon, and that vote will be the first vote of tomorrow's session.
```
