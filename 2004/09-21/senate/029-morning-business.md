# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there be a 
period for morning business for the purpose of statements only until 
3:45 p.m.--that is the next hour--with the time equally divided between 
the two leaders or their designees?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. I also say, Mr. President, if there is some concern because 
we used the last 15 minutes, if the Republicans want to come and get a 
little extra time because of that, we would be happy to take care of 
that.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Florida.
```
