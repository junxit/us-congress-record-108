# RECESS

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:53 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m, and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. 
Voinovich).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from New Mexico.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
