# CONSIDERATION OF MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2119

**Speaking:** Butterfield, G. K. (B001251) [D-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2119

```
         CONSIDERATION OF MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2119

  Mr. BUTTERFIELD. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 2119, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Ballance of North Carolina, for 
the purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant 
to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
