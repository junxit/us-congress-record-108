# COMMUNICATION FROM HON. NANCY PELOSI, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
        COMMUNICATION FROM HON. NANCY PELOSI, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER laid before the House the following communication from 
the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                    U.S. House of Representatives,


                              Office of the Democratic Leader,

                                                December 16, 2003.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker of the House, U.S. House of Representatives, 
         Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to section 214(a) of the Help

     Willie L. Brown, Jr. of San Francisco, California to the

                                                     Nancy Pelosi.
```
