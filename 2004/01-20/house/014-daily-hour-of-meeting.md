# DAILY HOUR OF MEETING

```
                         DAILY HOUR OF MEETING

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 488) 
and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 488

       Resolved, That unless otherwise ordered, before Monday, May 
     31, 2004, the hour of daily meeting of the House shall be 2 
     p.m. on Mondays; noon on Tuesdays; and 10 a.m. on all other 
     days of the week; and from Monday, May 31, 2004, and for the 
     remainder of the 108th Congress, the hour of daily meeting of 
     the House shall be noon on Mondays, 10 a.m. on Tuesdays, 
     Wednesdays and Thursdays; and 9 a.m. on all other days of the 
     week.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
