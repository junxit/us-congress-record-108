# JOINT SESSION DISSOLVED

```
                        JOINT SESSION DISSOLVED

  The SPEAKER. The Chair declares the joint meeting of the two Houses 
now dissolved.
  Accordingly, at 10 o'clock and 12 minutes p.m., the joint meeting of 
the two Houses was dissolved.
  The Members of the Senate retired to their Chamber.
```
