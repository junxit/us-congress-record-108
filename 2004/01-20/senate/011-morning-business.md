# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there be a 
period for morning business until the hour of 4:30 today, with the time 
equally divided between both sides, and that Senators be limited to 5 
minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
