# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Jones, Stephanie Tubbs (J000284) [D-OH]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. STEPHANIE TUBBS JONES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 8, 2004

  Mrs. JONES of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, yesterday, I was unavoidably 
detained and missed rollcall votes Nos. 422 and 423. Had I been present 
for the votes, I would have voted ``Aye'' for both measures to name The 
General William Cary Lee Post Office Building, and to name The Harvey 
and Bernice Jones Post Office Building, respectively.
  I ask unanimous consent that my vote statement appear in the 
appropriate place in the Congressional Record behind the day's recorded 
votes.
```
