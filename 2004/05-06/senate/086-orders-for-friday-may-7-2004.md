# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 7, 2004

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                     ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 7, 2004

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Friday, May 7. I further ask that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the Journal of the 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then begin a 
period of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
