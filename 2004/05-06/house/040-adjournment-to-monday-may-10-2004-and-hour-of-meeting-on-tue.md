# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 10, 2004, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, MAY 11, 2004

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

```
 ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 10, 2004, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, 
                              MAY 11, 2004

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at noon on Monday, May 10, 2004; and 
further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 
12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, May 11, for morning hour debates.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
